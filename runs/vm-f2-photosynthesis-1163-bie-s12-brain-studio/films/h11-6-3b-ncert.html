h11-6-3b · Electron transport proton gradient kaise banata hai?

10 / 10 NCERT lines film mein padhayi gayi · NCERT page 140, 141

Film ka dhaancha: pehle ki baat: 4 · concept: 26 · kahan kaam aata hai / asar: 3 · yaad rakhiye: 1

140(a) Since splitting of the water molecule takes place on the inner side of the membrane, the protons or hydrogen ions that are produced by the splitting of water accumulate within the lumen of the thylakoids.✓
  • Seedhe lumen mein Water-splitting complex ki jagah yaad karo: PS II se juda, lumen ki taraf khulta hai.
  • Seedhe lumen mein Do waters ka hisaab tha: 2H₂O → 4H⁺ + O₂ + 4e⁻.
  • Seedhe lumen mein Protons or hydrogen ions, dono yahaan H⁺ ke naam hain.
  • Seedhe lumen mein In H⁺ ko stroma se membrane paar nahi karni padegi, kyunki water splitting lumen-facing side par hoti hai.
  • Seedhe lumen mein Reaction inner side par hoti hai, isliye H⁺ seedhe lumen mein milte hain.
  • Pehla kaam aur electron Baandh wali tasveer mein pehla kaam mila: jama hone wali taraf H⁺ badh gaye.
  • Teen kaamon ka hisaab Water splitting ke chaar H⁺ lumen mein mile; carrier ne aur H⁺ andar pahunchaye.
141(b) As electrons move through the photosystems, protons are transported across the membrane.✓
  • Pehla kaam aur electron Reaction centre ka electron pehle primary acceptor leta hai, phir carriers use aage dete hain.
  • H carrier Isi stroma-se-lumen movement ke kaaran bahar H⁺ kam aur andar zyada hote hain.
  • H carrier Electron aur H⁺ ke safar jude hain, lekin dono ka raasta ek nahi hai.
141This happens because the primary accepter of electron which is located towards the outer side of the membrane transfers its electron not to an electron carrier but to an H carrier.✓
  • H carrier Ab aisa carrier socho jo electron ke saath hydrogen ka hissa bhi le jaaye.
  • H carrier Isi ko H carrier kehte hain; H hydrogen ka sanket hai.
  • H carrier Outer-side primary acceptor se electron is H carrier tak pahunchta hai.
141Hence, this molecule removes a proton from the stroma while transporting an electron.✓
  • H carrier Electron lete waqt carrier stroma se H⁺ bhi leta hai.
  • H carrier Yahaan stroma ke H⁺ isliye ghate kyunki carrier unhe lekar lumen mein chhod aaya.
  • Teen kaamon ka hisaab Water splitting ke chaar H⁺ lumen mein mile; carrier ne aur H⁺ andar pahunchaye.
141When this molecule passes on its electron to the electron carrier on the inner side of the membrane, the proton is released into the inner side or the lumen side of the membrane.✓
  • H carrier Ab aap inner side dekho: electron agle carrier ko mila, H⁺ lumen mein chhoot gaya.
  • H carrier Yahaan stroma ke H⁺ isliye ghate kyunki carrier unhe lekar lumen mein chhod aaya.
  • H carrier Electron aur H⁺ ke safar jude hain, lekin dono ka raasta ek nahi hai.
  • Teen kaamon ka hisaab Water splitting ke chaar H⁺ lumen mein mile; carrier ne aur H⁺ andar pahunchaye.
141(c) The NADP reductase enzyme is located on the stroma side of the membrane.✓
  • Stromal H⁺ ka istemal NADP reductase yeh badlav karaata hai; woh electrons ka mool source nahi hai.
  • Stromal H⁺ ka istemal Yeh enzyme stroma ki taraf kaam karta hai; isi face ko stroma side kehte hain.
141Along with electrons that come from the acceptor of electrons of PS I, protons are necessary for the reduction of NADP⁺ to NADPH+ H⁺.✓
  • Stromal H⁺ ka istemal Yaad karo, PS I ke acceptor se aaye electrons aakhir NADP⁺ ko milte hain.
  • Stromal H⁺ ka istemal NADP⁺ electrons leta hai; isi electron lene ko reduction kehte hain.
  • Stromal H⁺ ka istemal Aap pehle ka hisaab dekho: NADP⁺ + 2e⁻ + H⁺ → NADPH.
141These protons are also removed from the stroma.✓
  • Stromal H⁺ ka istemal NADPH banne mein yeh H⁺ stroma se aata hai, isliye wahaan bache H⁺ aur ghatte hain.
  • Stromal H⁺ ka istemal Aksar lagta hai ki teesra kaam bhi H⁺ ko lumen bhejta hai, par aisa nahi hai.
  • Stromal H⁺ ka istemal Yahaan H⁺ stromal reaction mein istemal hua; isi se dono taraf ka fark badha.
  • Teen kaamon ka hisaab Saath hi NADPH banne mein stroma ke H⁺ istemal hue.
141Hence, within the chloroplast, protons in the stroma decrease in number, while in the lumen there is accumulation of protons.✓
  • H carrier Isi stroma-se-lumen movement ke kaaran bahar H⁺ kam aur andar zyada hote hain.
  • Stromal H⁺ ka istemal Yahaan H⁺ stromal reaction mein istemal hua; isi se dono taraf ka fark badha.
  • Teen kaamon ka hisaab Isliye lumen mein H⁺ zyada aur stroma mein kam hote gaye.
141This creates a proton gradient across the thylakoid membrane as well as a measurable decrease in pH in the lumen.✓
  • Teen kaamon ka hisaab Yeh fark thylakoid membrane ke aar-paar hai, chloroplast ki baahari boundary par nahi.
  • Lumen ka pH Ab zyada H⁺ wale ghol ko socho: uski acidity badh jaati hai.
  • Lumen ka pH Acidity bataane wale scale ko pH kehte hain; H⁺ ki concentration badhe toh pH ghatta hai.
  • Lumen ka pH Isliye lumen ka pH ghatta hai, aur is kami ko naapa ja sakta hai.
