s3 · Protein ka return raasta

  • ATP synthase (Violet exposed catalytic beta lobe at 60,29, part of the authentic six-lobed head, fully visible with room to close.) · kata 4.36% tasveer
#LineKya hilta hai
1Ab aap membrane mein laga proton return ka raasta dekho.—
2ADP aur inorganic phosphate jodkar ATP banane wala enzyme ATP synthase hai.ATP synthase kasta hai (dabaav)
3Synthase ka banaane wala matlab hum turbine ki tasveer mein khol chuke hain.—
4Is enzyme ke do hisse hain: membrane mein dhansa CF₀ aur stroma ki taraf nikla CF₁.—
5CF₀ proton channel hai; CF₁ membrane ki outer stromal surface par nikla head hai.—
6Channel membrane ke aar-paar hai; aise aar-paar faile hone ko transmembrane kehte hain.—

s5 · Flow se banawat badalti hai

  • Banawat (Exposed violet catalytic beta lobe at 60,29, with authentic protein surface and visible nucleotide cleft, part of CF₁.) · kata 3.69% tasveer
  • CF₁ (Adjacent violet catalytic beta lobe at 73,29 in the same six-lobed CF₁ head, fully visible with room to compress.) · kata 3.16% tasveer
#LineKya hilta hai
1Aapne CF₀ ka raasta aur CF₁ ka stromal head dekh liya.—
2Par H⁺ ka bahav ADP aur phosphate jodne ka kaam kaise chalaata hai?—
3H⁺ ka bahav yeh kaam protein ki banawat badalne ki energy dekar chalaata hai.Banawat dabta hai (sikudta, phir lautta)
4Protein ki teen dishaon mein phaili banawat uski conformation hai.—
5Conformational ka matlab isi banawat se juda hai; iske badalne ko conformational change kehte hain.Banawat khulta hai
6Gradient breakdown ki energy CF₁ mein yahi badlav karaati hai.CF₁ dabta hai (sikudta, phir lautta)
7Isse enzyme ATP ke kai molecules banaata hai; kai ke liye yahaan several kaha gaya hai.—
8In ATP molecules mein kaam ke liye energy hoti hai, isliye inhe energy-packed keh rahe hain.—
9Aap yahaan ek H⁺ se ek ATP ka fixed anupaat mat nikaalna.—

s7 · Chemiosmosis ka poora order

  • Proton pump (Brick-red authentic dimeric transmembrane complex at 28,59, fully exposed with stromal uptake above and lumen release below.) · kata 13.4% tasveer
  • CF₁ (Violet outward-facing catalytic beta lobe at 60,29 in the six-lobed stromal head, exposed cleft and room to compress.) · kata 3.15% tasveer
#LineKya hilta hai
1Chemiosmosis ko chaar cheezein chahiye: membrane, proton pump, proton gradient aur ATP synthase.Proton pump chamakta hai
2Energy lagaakar H⁺ membrane ke paar jama karne wali machinery proton pump hai.Proton pump molecule ko pakadkar us paar chhodta hai, savaar H+
3Thylakoid lumen wahi andar ki jagah hai, jahaan humne high H⁺ concentration dekhi.—
4Pehle pumping mein energy lagti hai; waapas gradient ki disha mein diffusion se energy milti hai.—
5ATP synthase channel waapasi ka raasta deta hai; enzyme mili energy se ATP formation karaata hai.—
6Aap order jodo: gradient bana, CF₀ se H⁺ laute, CF₁ ki banawat badli, ATP bana.CF₁ dabta hai (sikudta, phir lautta)
7Electron seedhe ADP se judkar ATP nahi bana raha.—
