140Like in respiration, in photosynthesis too, ATP synthesis is linked to development of a proton gradient across a membrane.✓
  • Energy ka beech ka padaav Electron transport ki energy ATP banane tak H⁺ ka fark banaakar pahunchti hai; unka lautna ATP synthesis chalaata hai.
  • Band thaili aur gradient Membrane ke dono taraf H⁺ ki concentration aur electrical haalat ke fark ko proton gradient kehte hain.
  • Chemiosmotic hypothesis Hamaari is hypothesis mein membrane paar H⁺ ka jaana chemical kaam, yaani ATP banana, chalaata hai.
  • Accumulation ki taraf Respiration mein bhi membrane ke aar-paar proton gradient ATP synthesis se juda hai.
140This time these are the membranes of thylakoid.✓
  • Band thaili aur gradient Thylakoid ki band thaili yaad karo: andar lumen hai aur bahar stroma hai.
  • Accumulation ki taraf Photosynthesis mein H⁺ thylakoid lumen mein jama hote hain, stroma mein nahi.
140There is one difference though, here the proton accumulation is towards the inside of the membrane, i.e., in the lumen.✓
  • Accumulation ki taraf Photosynthesis mein H⁺ thylakoid lumen mein jama hote hain, stroma mein nahi.
140In respiration, protons accumulate in the intermembrane space of the mitochondria when electrons move through the ETS (Chapter 12).✓
  • Mitochondrial comparison Respiration ka poora kaam aage dekhenge; abhi mitochondrion ki outer aur inner membrane socho.
  • Mitochondrial comparison Inke beech ki jagah intermembrane space hai; inter yahaan beech ka matlab deta hai.
  • Mitochondrial comparison Respiration ki electron transport system, ya ETS, chalne par H⁺ isi space mein jama hote hain.
  • Mitochondrial comparison Yaad rakho, mitochondrial accumulation intermembrane space mein hai, matrix mein nahi.
140Let us understand what causes the proton gradient across the membrane.✓
  • Gradient ke teen kaaran Ab hum isi safar mein gradient banane wale teen kaam dhoondhenge.
  • Gradient ke teen kaaran Pehla, water splitting lumen mein H⁺ deti hai.
  • Gradient ke teen kaaran Doosra, carrier stroma se H⁺ lumen mein le jaata hai.
  • Gradient ke teen kaaran Teesra, NADPH banne mein stroma ke H⁺ istemal hote hain.
