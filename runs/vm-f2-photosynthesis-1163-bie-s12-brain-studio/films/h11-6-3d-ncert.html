h11-6-3d · Electron transport proton gradient kaise banata hai?

Kitab se bahar ke shabd (sirf jaankari, film inse rukti nahi): PQ, PQH, B6f, PC, FNR

142Along with the NADPH produced by the movement of electrons, the ATP will be used immediately in the biosynthetic reaction taking place in the stroma, responsible for fixing CO₂, and synthesis of sugars.✓
  • Stroma mein istemaal Ab aap membrane par bana ATP aur electron movement se bana NADPH ek saath dekho.
  • Stroma mein istemaal ATP energy deta hai; NADPH naye organic compounds banane ke liye electrons deta hai.
  • Stroma mein istemaal Inka kaam surrounding stroma mein hoga, jahaan sugar banane ki reactions hoti hain.
  • Stroma mein istemaal Sugar banane ko energy aur electrons chahiye, isliye ATP aur NADPH dono turant in reactions mein lagte hain.
  • Stroma mein istemaal Naye jeev-padarth banane ke is kaam ko biosynthetic kehte hain; aage hum iske steps kholenge.
  • Carbon ka organic jod In reactions mein CO₂ ka carbon organic molecule mein judta hai; isi ko fixing CO₂ kehte hain.
  • Carbon ka organic jod Is CO₂ fixation ka poora raasta aage dekhenge; abhi ise stroma mein sugar banne se jodo.
  • Carbon ka organic jod Ab ATP ki energy aur NADPH ke electrons taiyaar hain; agle heading mein dekhenge ki stroma inhe sugar banane mein kahaan lagaata hai.
