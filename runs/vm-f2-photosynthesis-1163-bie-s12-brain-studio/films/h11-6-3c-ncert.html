141Why are we so interested in the proton gradient?✓
  • Jama H⁺ se aage Ruko, pehle khud socho: proton gradient mein hamaari itni dilchaspi kyun hai?
141This gradient is important because it is the breakdown of this gradient that leads to the synthesis of ATP.✓
  • Jama H⁺ se aage Proton gradient mein dilchaspi isliye hai kyunki H⁺ ka sahi raaste lautna ATP synthesis ko energy deta hai.
  • CF₀ se return Is fark mein jama energy proton return par milti hai, isliye ATP synthesis chal sakti hai.
  • Analogy se biological naam Pehle proton gradient banao, phir ATP synthase se H⁺ lautaakar ATP banao; yahi chemiosmosis hai.
  • Chemiosmosis ka poora order Aap order jodo: gradient bana, CF₀ se H⁺ laute, CF₁ ki banawat badli, ATP bana.
141The gradient is broken down due to the movement of protons across the membrane to the stroma through the transmembrane channel of the CF₀ of the ATP synthase.✓
  • Protein ka return raasta Ab aap membrane mein laga proton return ka raasta dekho.
  • CF₀ se return H⁺ CF₀ se lumen se stroma lautte hain, kyunki unka electrochemical gradient isi disha mein hai.
  • CF₀ se return Lautne se dono taraf ka fark ghatta hai; isi ko breakdown of this gradient kehte hain.
  • CF₀ se return Aksar lagta hai ki protons toot gaye, par asal mein unki dono taraf ki asamaanta ghati hai.
  • Chemiosmosis ka poora order Aap order jodo: gradient bana, CF₀ se H⁺ laute, CF₁ ki banawat badli, ATP bana.
142The ATP synthase enzyme consists of two parts: one called the CF₀ is embedded in the thylakoid membrane and forms a transmembrane channel that carries out facilitated diffusion of protons across the membrane.✓
  • Protein ka return raasta Is enzyme ke do hisse hain: membrane mein dhansa CF₀ aur stroma ki taraf nikla CF₁.
  • Protein ka return raasta CF₀ proton channel hai; CF₁ membrane ki outer stromal surface par nikla head hai.
  • Protein ka return raasta Channel membrane ke aar-paar hai; aise aar-paar faile hone ko transmembrane kehte hain.
  • CF₀ se return Isi phailne ko diffusion kehte hain; yahaan H⁺ electrochemical gradient ki disha mein chalte hain.
  • CF₀ se return Protein ka raasta milne par aise paar jaane ko facilitated diffusion kehte hain.
  • CF₀ se return Facilitated ka matlab yahaan raasta milne se aasaan hua hai.
  • CF₀ se return H⁺ CF₀ se lumen se stroma lautte hain, kyunki unka electrochemical gradient isi disha mein hai.
  • Flow se banawat badalti hai Aapne CF₀ ka raasta aur CF₁ ka stromal head dekh liya.
142The other portion is called CF₁ and protrudes on the outer surface of the thylakoid membrane on the side that faces the stroma.✓
  • Protein ka return raasta Is enzyme ke do hisse hain: membrane mein dhansa CF₀ aur stroma ki taraf nikla CF₁.
  • Protein ka return raasta CF₀ proton channel hai; CF₁ membrane ki outer stromal surface par nikla head hai.
  • Flow se banawat badalti hai Aapne CF₀ ka raasta aur CF₁ ka stromal head dekh liya.
142The break down of the gradient provides enough energy to cause a conformational change in the CF₁ particle of the ATP synthase, which makes the enzyme synthesise several molecules of energy-packed ATP.✓
  • Flow se banawat badalti hai H⁺ ka bahav yeh kaam protein ki banawat badalne ki energy dekar chalaata hai.
  • Flow se banawat badalti hai Conformational ka matlab isi banawat se juda hai; iske badalne ko conformational change kehte hain.
  • Flow se banawat badalti hai Gradient breakdown ki energy CF₁ mein yahi badlav karaati hai.
  • Flow se banawat badalti hai Isse enzyme ATP ke kai molecules banaata hai; kai ke liye yahaan several kaha gaya hai.
  • Flow se banawat badalti hai In ATP molecules mein kaam ke liye energy hoti hai, isliye inhe energy-packed keh rahe hain.
  • Chemiosmosis ka poora order Aap order jodo: gradient bana, CF₀ se H⁺ laute, CF₁ ki banawat badli, ATP bana.
142Chemiosmosis requires a membrane, a proton pump, a proton gradient and ATP synthase.✓
  • Chemiosmosis ka poora order Chemiosmosis ko chaar cheezein chahiye: membrane, proton pump, proton gradient aur ATP synthase.
142Energy is used to pump protons across a membrane, to create a gradient or a high concentration of protons within the thylakoid lumen.✓
  • Chemiosmosis ka poora order Energy lagaakar H⁺ membrane ke paar jama karne wali machinery proton pump hai.
  • Chemiosmosis ka poora order Thylakoid lumen wahi andar ki jagah hai, jahaan humne high H⁺ concentration dekhi.
  • Chemiosmosis ka poora order Pehle pumping mein energy lagti hai; waapas gradient ki disha mein diffusion se energy milti hai.
142ATP synthase has a channel that allows diffusion of protons back across the membrane; this releases enough energy to activate ATP synthase enzyme that catalyses the formation of ATP.✓
  • Protein ka return raasta ADP aur inorganic phosphate jodkar ATP banane wala enzyme ATP synthase hai.

NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • pehle ki baat Ab lumen mein H⁺ jama hain, lekin ADP mein phosphate judna abhi samjhaana baaki hai.
    Jama protons aur abhi samajhne baaki phosphorylation ko jodta checkpoint.
  • pehle ki baat Aap baandh se turbine ki taraf bahav yaad karo; sirf paani jama karna kaafi nahi tha.
    Baandh aur turbine ka bahav stored gradient aur useful return se jodta hai.
  • concept ADP aur inorganic phosphate jodkar ATP banane wala enzyme ATP synthase hai.
    ATP banne ke substrates ADP aur inorganic phosphate ko catalytic site par milte dikhata hai.
  • pehle ki baat Synthase ka banaane wala matlab hum turbine ki tasveer mein khol chuke hain.
    Synthase ke banaane wale arth ki pehle ki analogy se yaad.
  • concept Channel membrane ke aar-paar hai; aise aar-paar faile hone ko transmembrane kehte hain.
    Transmembrane ka aar-paar wala arth kholta hai.
  • pehle ki baat Ab particles ka apne-aap zyada concentration se kam ki taraf phailna socho.
    Diffusion ka concentration-based prerequisite.
  • concept Isi phailne ko diffusion kehte hain; yahaan H⁺ electrochemical gradient ki disha mein chalte hain.
    H⁺ ke movement ki electrochemical direction saaf karta hai.
  • concept Facilitated ka matlab yahaan raasta milne se aasaan hua hai.
    Facilitated shabd ka seedha arth.
  • concept H⁺ CF₀ se lumen se stroma lautte hain, kyunki unka electrochemical gradient isi disha mein hai.
    Return ki electrochemical wajah kholta hai.
  • concept Aksar lagta hai ki protons toot gaye, par asal mein unki dono taraf ki asamaanta ghati hai.
    Breakdown ka matlab protons tootna nahi hai.
  • pehle ki baat Protein ki teen dishaon mein phaili banawat uski conformation hai.
    Conformation ka three-dimensional banawat wala arth.
  • concept Conformational ka matlab isi banawat se juda hai; iske badalne ko conformational change kehte hain.
    Conformational change ka arth kholta hai.
  • concept Aap yahaan ek H⁺ se ek ATP ka fixed anupaat mat nikaalna.
    Galat one-proton-one-ATP anupaat nikaalne se rokta hai.
  • yaad rakhiye Ab hum baandh aur turbine ka poora sambandh biological naam se jodte hain.
