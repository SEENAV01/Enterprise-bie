s3 · H carrier

#LineKya hilta hai
1Ab aisa carrier socho jo electron ke saath hydrogen ka hissa bhi le jaaye.carrier chamakta hai
2Isi ko H carrier kehte hain; H hydrogen ka sanket hai.carrier chamakta hai
3Outer-side primary acceptor se electron is H carrier tak pahunchta hai.carrier molecule ko pakad leta hai, savaar e-
