h11-6-2c · Cyclic aur non-cyclic photophosphorylation mein kya difference hai?

6 / 6 NCERT lines film mein padhayi gayi · NCERT page 140

Film ka dhaancha: sawaal: 1 · pehle ki baat: 7 · concept: 19 · kahan kaam aata hai / asar: 8 · yaad rakhiye: 6

140When only PS I is functional, the electron is circulated within the photosystem and the phosphorylation occurs due to cyclic flow of electrons (Figure 11.6).✓
  • Electron ka return route Saath mein ADP aur Pi se ATP banne ka kaam juda hai.
  • Functional PS I Yahaan sirf PS I kaam kar raha hai; usse nikla electron transport chain se PS I par laut sakta hai.
  • Functional PS I Isi kaam kar rahe hone ko yahaan functional kehte hain.
  • Product checkpoint Lekin ATP ban sakta hai, kyunki cyclic electron transport se bhi ATP banne ka kaam juda hai.
  • Dono routes ki tulna Cyclic mein sirf PS I ka electron acceptor aur transport system se ghoomkar PS I par laut aata hai.
140A possible location where this could be happening is in the stroma lamellae.✓
  • Grana aur stroma lamellae Un dheron ko jodne wali bina dher ki membrane pattiyan stroma lamellae hain.
  • Possible location Stroma lamellae ko possible location isliye keh rahe hain kyunki yahaan electron NADP⁺ ko dene ke bajay chain se PS I par laut sakta hai.
  • Possible location Isliye stroma lamellae cyclic photophosphorylation ki ek possible location hain; ise eklauti location mat samajhna.
140While the membrane or lamellae of the grana have both PS I and PS II the stroma lamellae membranes lack PS II as well as NADP reductase enzyme.✓
  • Grana aur stroma lamellae Grana ki membranes ya lamellae mein PS I aur PS II, dono milte hain.
  • Grana aur stroma lamellae Stroma lamellae membranes mein PS II aur NADP reductase enzyme nahi hote.
  • Possible location Ab aapke saamne stroma lamellae ki tasveer hai: PS II aur NADP reductase nahi, lekin PS I wala raasta hai.
140The excited electron does not pass on to NADP⁺ but is cycled back to the PS I complex through the electron transport chain (Figure 11.6).✓
  • Electron ka return route Acceptor se electron transport system mein gaya aur wahaan se P700 par laut aaya.
  • Electron ka return route Electron NADP⁺ tak gaya hi nahi, isliye is chakkar se NADPH nahi bana.
  • Functional PS I Yahaan sirf PS I kaam kar raha hai; usse nikla electron transport chain se PS I par laut sakta hai.
  • Possible location Stroma lamellae ko possible location isliye keh rahe hain kyunki yahaan electron NADP⁺ ko dene ke bajay chain se PS I par laut sakta hai.
  • Return route ka sawaal Ab dono raaston ka clue tumhare paas hai: NADPH banne ke liye electrons NADP⁺ tak jaane chahiye.
  • Return route ka sawaal ATP ke saath NADPH nahi banega, kyunki electron NADP⁺ ko milne ke bajay P700 par laut raha hai, jaisa aapne socha hoga.
  • Dono routes ki tulna Cyclic mein sirf PS I ka electron acceptor aur transport system se ghoomkar PS I par laut aata hai.
140The cyclic flow hence, results only in the synthesis of ATP, but not of NADPH + H⁺.✓
  • Electron ka return route Saath mein ADP aur Pi se ATP banne ka kaam juda hai.
  • Electron ka return route Electron NADP⁺ tak gaya hi nahi, isliye is chakkar se NADPH nahi bana.
  • Functional PS I Is cyclic flow se ATP banta hai, lekin NADPH + H⁺ nahi banta.
  • Return route ka sawaal ATP ke saath NADPH nahi banega, kyunki electron NADP⁺ ko milne ke bajay P700 par laut raha hai, jaisa aapne socha hoga.
  • Product checkpoint Lekin ATP ban sakta hai, kyunki cyclic electron transport se bhi ATP banne ka kaam juda hai.
  • Product checkpoint Par cyclic flow ATP banata hai, bina net NADPH aur O₂ formation ke.
  • Dono routes ki tulna Is flow ka product ATP hai; net NADPH ya O₂ nahi bante.
140Cyclic photophosphorylation also occurs when only light of wavelengths beyond 680 nm are available for excitation.✓
  • 680 nm se aage Cyclic photophosphorylation tab bhi hoti hai jab excitation ke liye sirf 680 nm se aage ki wavelengths milti hain.
  • 680 nm se aage Aage ka matlab yahaan 680 nm se badi wavelength hai, zyada energy wali light kehna nahi.
  • 680 nm se aage PS II ka centre P680 hai, jabki PS I ka P700; is condition mein PS I wala cyclic raasta chal sakta hai.
