139Living organisms have the capability of extracting energy from oxidisable substances and store this in the form of bond energy.✓
  • Energy aur electrons Jeev oxidisable substances se energy nikaalkar use chemical roop mein rakh sakte hain.
  • Bonds aur energy Unki chemical banawat se judi energy ko yahaan bond energy keh rahe hain.
139Special substances like ATP, carry this energy in their chemical bonds.✓
  • Energy aur electrons Pehle yaad karo, ATP cells ke kaamon ko kharch karne layak energy deta hai.
  • Bonds aur energy Unki chemical banawat se judi energy ko yahaan bond energy keh rahe hain.
  • Bonds aur energy ATP jaise molecules isi chemical roop mein energy lekar cells ke doosre kaamon tak pahunchate hain.
  • Bonds aur energy ATP se ADP aur phosphate banne wala poora badlav cells ko kaam ki energy de sakta hai.
139The process through which ATP is synthesised by cells (in mitochondria and chloroplasts) is named phosphorylation.✓
  • Cell mein phosphorylation Yahaan ADP mein phosphate jodkar ATP banane ko phosphorylation kehte hain.
  • Cell mein phosphorylation Cells mein mitochondria aur chloroplasts, dono mein ATP banne ka yeh kaam hota hai.
  • Cell mein phosphorylation Ab aap dekho, ATP dono jagah banta hai, lekin chloroplast mein uske banne ko light se jod rahe hain.
  • Photo-phosphorylation Photo ka matlab light hai, aur phosphorylation yahaan phosphate jodkar ATP banana hai.
140Photo-phosphorylation is the synthesis of ATP from ADP and inorganic phosphate in the presence of light.✓
  • Teesra phosphate Yeh alag se maujood phosphate inorganic phosphate kehlaata hai.
  • Cell mein phosphorylation Ab aap dekho, ATP dono jagah banta hai, lekin chloroplast mein uske banne ko light se jod rahe hain.
  • Photo-phosphorylation Light ki madad se ATP banne ko alag naam isliye dete hain kyunki yeh naam energy ke light se milne ko pehchaanta hai.
  • Photo-phosphorylation Chloroplast mein jab light ki madad se ADP aur inorganic phosphate se ATP banta hai, use photo-phosphorylation kehte hain.
  • Photo-phosphorylation Photo ka matlab light hai, aur phosphorylation yahaan phosphate jodkar ATP banana hai.
  • Photo-phosphorylation ADP + Pi se ATP banta hai; is jodne ke liye energy light-driven kaam se milti hai.
