h11-6-2b · Cyclic aur non-cyclic photophosphorylation mein kya difference hai?

3 / 3 NCERT lines film mein padhayi gayi · NCERT page 140

Film ka dhaancha: pehle ki baat: 10 · concept: 30 · kahan kaam aata hai / asar: 2 · yaad rakhiye: 1

140When the two photosystems work in a series, first PS II and then the PS I, a process called non-cyclic photo-phosphorylation occurs.✓
  • Non-cyclic ka arth Aise flow se jude ATP banne ko non-cyclic photo-phosphorylation kehte hain.
  • Dono systems aur products Ismein dono photosystems ek ke baad ek kaam karte hain: pehle PS II, phir PS I.
140The two photosystems are connected through an electron transport chain, as seen earlier – in the Z scheme.✓
  • Electron ki manzil PS II se electron acceptor aur carriers ki kadi ke raaste PS I tak pahunchta hai.
  • Electron ki manzil Electron ko ek se doosre tak dene wali isi kadi ko electron transport chain kehte hain.
  • Dono systems aur products Inhe jodne wali electron transport chain wahi hai jo humne Z scheme mein dekhi.
140Both ATP and NADPH + H⁺ are synthesised by this kind of electron flow (Figure 11.5).✓
  • Dono systems aur products Is electron flow se ATP aur NADPH, dono bante hain; water splitting se O₂ bhi milti hai.
  • Dono systems aur products NADPH + H⁺ likha mile, toh yaad rakho: reduced carrier NADPH hai; saath ka H⁺ proton hai.
