145C4 plants are special: They have a special type of leaf anatomy, they tolerate higher temperatures, they show a response to high light intensities, they lack a process called photorespiration and have greater productivity of biomass.✓
  • C4 naam, Calvin ka kaam Dry tropical regions mein adapted C4 plants ki special leaf anatomy ko hum pehchaan chuke hain; ab stroma mein iska faayda samajhte hain.
  • Bachaa carbon, zyada biomass Iska faayda hai ki C4 plants higher temperatures tolerate karte hain aur high light intensities par achchha response dikhate hain.
  • Bachaa carbon, zyada biomass Inmein photorespiration nahi hoti, jisse carbon loss bachta hai aur biomass productivity zyada hoti hai; bani sugar bahar jaakar growth ke kaam aati hai.

145The particularly large cells around the vascular bundles of the C4 plants are called bundle sheath cells, and the leaves which have such anatomy are said to have ‘Kranz’ anatomy. ‘Kranz’ means ‘wreath’ and is a reflection of the arrangement of cells.✓
  • Kranz ka haar Ab isi maize section mein vascular bundle ko gherne wali particularly large cells dekhiye, jinhe bundle sheath cells kehte hain.
  • Kranz ka haar Inki haar jaisi arrangement Kranz anatomy hai, kyunki Kranz ka matlab wreath hota hai.
145The bundle sheath cells may form several layers around the vascular bundles; they are characterised by having a large number of chloroplasts, thick walls impervious to gaseous exchange and no intercellular spaces.✓
  • Kranz ka haar Bundle sheath several layers bhi bana sakti hai, aur in cells mein chloroplasts badi sankhya mein milte hain; ab ek cell ke andar chalte hain.
  • Tight packing ka faayda Dekhiye, gaseous exchange ke liye impervious thick walls aur intercellular spaces ka na hona, band dabbe ki tarah CO2 ka leakage kam karte hain.
