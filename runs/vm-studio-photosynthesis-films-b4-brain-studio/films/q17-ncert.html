q17 · Ek glucose banane ka ATP-NADPH bill kitna aata hai?

Kitab se bahar ke shabd (sirf jaankari, film inse rukti nahi): G3P

145Hence for every CO2 molecule entering the Calvin cycle, 3 molecules of ATP and 2 of NADPH are required.✓
  • Cycle ka hisaab Regeneration mein ATP lagakar RuBP dobara banta hai; poore cycle ka har CO2 par total bill 3 ATP aur 2 NADPH hai.
145It is probably to meet this difference in number of ATP and NADPH used in the dark reaction that the cyclic phosphorylation takes place.✓
  • Supply ki taraf Par ATP zyada chahiye toh bharpai kaise ho? Isme kaam aa sakti hai cyclic photophosphorylation, jisme electron PSI se nikal kar PSI par laut aata hai.
  • Supply ki taraf Yeh route extra ATP de sakta hai, NADPH nahi; aaiye right wali ATP synthase ko paas se dekhein.
  • Extra ATP ka istemaal Ab CF1 par ADP aur Pi milkar ATP banate hain, jisse Calvin cycle ki extra ATP demand poori ho sakti hai.
145To make one molecule of glucose 6 turns of the cycle are required.✓
  • Chhah turns Ab ek glucose ke liye kitne turns chahiye? Iska seedha sa jawab hai chhah; Calvin cycle ka fixation, reduction aur regeneration wala wheel dekhiye.
  • Chhah turns Fixation ke har turn mein ek CO2 aata hai, isliye chhah turns mein 6 CO2 glucose ke chhah carbon dete hain.
  • Chhah turns Reduction se in chhah turns mein net do G3P nikalte hain, jo ek glucose-equivalent hain.
145Work out how many ATP and NADPH molecules will be required to make one molecule of glucose through the Calvin pathway.✓
  • Dono currencies ka multiplication Isi stroma mein chhah turns ka bill kaise niklega? Iska seedha sa jawab hai ki har CO2 ka bill chhah se multiply karte hain.
  • Dono currencies ka multiplication Dekhiye, 6 guna 3, yaani poore Calvin cycle ke chhah turns mein total 18 ATP kharch hote hain.
  • Dono currencies ka multiplication Waise hi 6 guna 2, yaani 12 NADPH reduction mein lagte hain; jaise chhah meals ke bill mein dono cheezon ka kharch alag multiply hota hai.
145It might help you to understand all of this if we look at what goes in and what comes out of the Calvin cycle.✓
  • Cycle ka hisaab Stroma mein bill kaise ginen? Aaiye, Calvin cycle mein kya aata aur nikalta hai, use fixation, reduction aur regeneration ke wheel par samajhte hain.
  • Supply ki taraf Ab paas ki thylakoid par hain; Calvin cycle se nikle 18 ADP aur 12 NADP+ light reactions ki taraf pahunchte hain.
