NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • pehle ki baat Aaiye jaante hain. Pehle yaad kijiye ki patte mein aane wala CO2 sugar ke liye carbon laata hai; ab isi patte ke andar chalte hain.
    Prerequisite: sugar ka carbon CO2 se aata hai.
  • concept Chloroplast ke andar stroma mein CO2 pahunchta hai; experiments ka unexpected jawab 5-carbon ketose sugar ribulose bisphosphate, ya RuBP, nikalta hai.
    Calvin pathway ki jagah stroma se jodte hain.
  • concept Jaise karigar ke paas samaan pahunchta hai, waise yahan CO2 pahunchta hai; ab enzyme ki working site ko paas se dekhte hain.
    Enzyme ko karigar se jodne wali everyday analogy.
  • concept Dekhiye, RuBP carboxylase par 5-carbon RuBP aur ek CO2 milte hain, jisse do 3-carbon 3-PGA molecules bante hain; carbon ki ginti 5 + 1 = 3 + 3 rehti hai.
    Carbon conservation ko 5 + 1 = 3 + 3 se saaf karte hain.
  • concept Jaise chhe motiyon ke teen-teen wale do hisse bante hain, waise yahan do 3-PGA molecules alag nikalte hain.
    Motiyon ki analogy se do product molecules samajhate hain.
  • concept Regeneration mein har CO2 par ek ATP phosphorylation mein lagta hai, jisse RuBP lautkar cycle chalata rehta hai.
    ATP kharch ko per fixed CO2 accounting se jodte hain.
  • concept Isme ATP kaam aata hai; har fixed CO2 se aaye do 3-PGA ki phosphorylation ke liye do ATP lagte hain, jaise kaam ke do tickets.
    ATP accounting ko tickets ki analogy se jodte hain.
  • concept Dekhiye, 3-PGA aur ATP enzyme par milte hain, jisse phosphorylated intermediate aur ADP bante hain; intermediate agle reduction enzyme ki taraf badhta hai.
    Phosphorylated intermediate aur ADP return dikhate hain.
  • concept Phosphorylated intermediate aur NADPH se triose phosphate G3P banta hai, jiske saath NADP+ aur inorganic phosphate Pi nikalte hain.
    Immediate G3P product aur carrier return dikhate hain.
  • concept Yeh reduction reactions glucose banane ki raah kholti hain; ek glucose equivalent ke liye chhe CO2 fix hote hain aur chhe turns lagte hain.
    Glucose equivalent se immediate triose aur net hexose yield ka farq saaf karte hain.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Iska kuch triose phosphate bahar sucrose banane ki raah par jaata hai, aur kuch stroma mein starch banane ki raah par, jisse carbon transport ya storage ke kaam aata hai.
    Sucrose transport aur starch storage ke liye carbon ki branch dikhate hain.
