### Part c09.1 · definition
_frame: process: A paper claim represents a fixed gold entitlement. · how: The issuer returns gold when the holder presents the redeemable note. · when: Under a historical gold-redemption arrangement, on demand. · conditions: A credible fixed-gold redemption promise is required. · atmosphere: A hypothetical bank counter, not today's Indian rupee system._

**b2 · example · step 1: a concrete example first** — पुराने hypothetical bank में 1 note लौटाएँ तो? Bank उसे लेकर 1 gram gold देता है; आपके हाथ में अब gold है। आज का rupee ऐसा नहीं।
  _screen: Historical illustration: return 1 note → receive 1 gram gold; not today's rupee_

**b3 · explain · step 2: the book's definition word by word** — यह gold-backed paper currency है: माँगने पर तय gold मिले। Intrinsic value यानी material की कीमत; यहाँ value paper से नहीं, redemption promise से है, जैसे सामान की receipt।
  _screen: Paper material ≠ redeemable gold claim_

### Part c09.2 · cause_effect
_frame: process: Gold backing constrains additional note issue. · how: Additional claims require supporting gold under the illustrative arrangement. · when: Reserves support issue before redemption is demanded. · conditions: The fully backed arithmetic is illustrative, not a historical reserve ratio. · atmosphere: A bank balances gold stock against promised claims._

**b6 · example · step 1: the effect students can see** — Bank 11वाँ note कैसे दे? मानिए, 10 grams के पीछे 10 notes हैं, हर note पर 1 gram का वादा। Fully backed उदाहरण में अगला note देने को 1 gram और चाहिए।

### Part c10.1 · definition
_frame: process: Fiat money supports purchases without commodity redemption. · how: Government authority and confidence in continued acceptance support monetary value. · when: A seller accepts payment expecting to spend it again. · conditions: Material value, purchasing power and gold convertibility are different. · atmosphere: A modern Indian kirana purchase._

**b19 · example · step 1: a concrete example first** — दुकानदार paper क्यों ले? आप अपने ₹100 देकर चावल लेते हैं। दुकानदार उन्हें स्वीकार करता है क्योंकि आगे खुद खर्च कर पाने का भरोसा है।
  _screen: Buyer hands over ₹100 for rice → seller accepts → seller can make a later payment_

**b20 · explain · step 2: the book's definition word by word** — यह fiat money है: value government authority और public trust से, material से नहीं। यानी आज मिला note कल groceries दिलाएगा।
  _screen: Authority + continued acceptance, not material value_

**b21 · compare · step 3: a non-example that tests the boundary** — Gold-backed paper का आधार redemption था; इसलिए हर paper note fiat नहीं।
  _screen: Paper form ≠ fiat basis_

**b22 · recap · step 4: where the term is used next in the chapter** — Authority और trust वाला यही आधार आगे समझाएगा: gold बिना issue कैसे control होता है।
  _screen: Fiat basis → next: issuance and monetary-policy governance_

### Part c11.1 · definition
_frame: process: Recognised currency settles obligations, with or without aggregate tender ceilings. · how: Legal status and denomination determine payment capacity; circulation designs do not change denomination. · when: Existing monetary obligations are settled using genuine, valid money. · conditions: Applicable territory, legal rules and amount limits govern the result. · atmosphere: Indian grocery debt settlements using notes and coins._
