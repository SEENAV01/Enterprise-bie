  • pehle ki baat Ab hum mesophyll cell ke andar hain, jiske chloroplast light ki energy pakadte hain.

  • pehle ki baat Chloroplast ke andar yeh band, chapti thailiyaan thylakoids hain, jinki membrane mein light wali electron relay chalti hai.

    Electron conservation ke liye baton analogy.
  • concept Nahi, acceptor use cytochromes wale electron transport system ko deta hai, jismein yeh cytochrome b6f bhi kaam karta hai.

  • concept Redox-potential scale par electron ka transfer downhill hai, jaise seedhiyaan utarta yatri energy chhodta hai; yeh membrane mein neeche girna nahi hai.
    Yatri analogy; energy downhill aur spatial direction ka farq.
  • concept Excited electron doosre acceptor ko milta hai, jiska redox potential excited donor se greater hai, jisse transfer aage badhta hai.
    Greater redox potential ka reference excited donor hai.

    Simultaneous operation aur stromal electron-transfer partners.
  • concept Dekhiye, electron phir downhill transfer se final acceptor NADP+ tak aata hai, jisse energy-rich NADPH ban sakta hai.
    Energy-rich reduced product NADPH ko kaha hai.

    Balanced net hisaab aur reduction ka matlab.

    Charged battery analogy aur carbon reduction ko NADPH delivery.
