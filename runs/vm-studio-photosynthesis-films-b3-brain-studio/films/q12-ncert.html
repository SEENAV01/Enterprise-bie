NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • pehle ki baat ATP apne chemical bonds mein yeh energy carry karta hai, jaise kharch karne layak chhota energy-wallet.
    ATP ko kharch karne layak energy-wallet se joda hai.
  • pehle ki baat Chloroplast ke andar yeh grana ki stacks hain, jinhe stroma lamellae ki membranes jodti hain.
    Grana aur connecting membranes ka spatial sambandh bataya hai.
  • concept Dekhiye, PSII ke paas water splitting se H2O tootkar electrons, H+ aur O2 deta hai, jisse oxygen bahar nikalti hai.
    Water source aur oxygen release ka q11 se basic bridge diya hai.
  • concept Phir light PSI ke electron ko excite karti hai; yeh series non-cyclic photophosphorylation hai, jiska energy pattern Z scheme kehlata hai.
    Z scheme physical zigzag nahi, energy pattern hai.
  • concept Is raaste mein electron acceptor se electron NADP reductase tak jaata hai, jaise ek taraf jaane wali delivery.
    Electron delivery ko one-way safar se samjhaya hai.
  • concept Yahaan NADP+ do electrons aur H+ lekar NADPH banta hai; non-cyclic flow ka reducing output NADPH + H+ likha jaata hai.
    NADP+ reduction ki electron aur proton requirement batayi hai.
  • concept Phir ETC electron bhejti hai aur H+ lumen mein jama karne mein madad karti hai, jisse ATP banane ki energy milti hai.
    ETC ko proton gradient se jodne ka q13 bridge hai.
  • concept Aakhir electron PSI mein laut aata hai, jaise gol chakkar ki bus, jisse loop band ho jaata hai.
    Return loop ko gol chakkar ki bus se samjhaya hai.
  • concept Baandh ke paani jaise H+ CF0 se wapas behkar central stalk ghumate hain, jisse CF1 ke catalytic parts ki shape badalti hai.
    q14 ka basic mechanism aur baandh analogy diya hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Dekhiye, ATP stroma ke enzyme tak pahunchta hai, jahaan iski energy carbon compounds ko sugar banne ki taraf badhaati hai.
    ATP ka stromal carbon metabolism mein use dikhaya hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar ATP carbon compound ko phosphate dekar ADP banta hai, jisse phosphorylated carbon compound aage ke liye taiyaar hota hai.
    Carbon compound phosphorylation se ATP consumption dikhaya hai.
  • yaad rakhiye Is video se aapko yeh baatein yaad rakhni hain: non-cyclic mein PSII-phir-PSI, electron NADP+ tak, water splitting aur ATP–NADPH–O2 milte hain, jabki cyclic mein sirf PSI, electron waapsi, water splitting nahi aur sirf ATP milta hai.
    Cyclic mein water splitting na hone ka explicit comparison diya hai.
