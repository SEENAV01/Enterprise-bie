140Like in respiration, in photosynthesis too, ATP synthesis is linked to development of a proton gradient across a membrane.✓
  • Membrane ke dono taraf Chloroplast ke andar yeh thylakoid sacs hain; photosynthesis mein ATP synthesis inki membrane ke aar-paar proton gradient banne se judi hai.

140This time these are the membranes of thylakoid.✓
  • Membrane ke dono taraf Chloroplast ke andar yeh thylakoid sacs hain; photosynthesis mein ATP synthesis inki membrane ke aar-paar proton gradient banne se judi hai.
140There is one difference though, here the proton accumulation is towards the inside of the membrane, i.e., in the lumen.✓
  • Band sac ke andar jamav Yahan band thylakoid ka andar wala paani-bhara hissa lumen hai, aur bahar stroma hai; H+ ka jamav lumen mein hota hai.

140(a) Since splitting of the water molecule takes place on the inner side of the membrane, the protons or hydrogen ions that are produced by the splitting of water accumulate within the lumen of the thylakoids.✓
  • Pehla contribution: water splitting Dekhiye, lumen wali inner side par do H2O tootkar chaar H+, chaar electrons aur ek O2 dete hain, jisse H+ seedhe lumen mein judte hain.

141This happens because the primary accepter of electron which is located towards the outer side of the membrane transfers its electron not to an electron carrier but to an H carrier.✓
  • Doosra contribution: H-carrier Isme H-carrier kaam aata hai: outer side ka primary acceptor electron ko sirf electron carrier nahi, H-carrier ko deta hai.
141Hence, this molecule removes a proton from the stroma while transporting an electron.✓
  • Doosra contribution: H-carrier Yeh H-carrier electron le jaate hue stroma se H+ bhi leta hai, jaise ferry apne saath ek aur yaatri bitha leti hai.
141When this molecule passes on its electron to the electron carrier on the inner side of the membrane, the proton is released into the inner side or the lumen side of the membrane.✓
  • Doosra contribution: H-carrier Ab H-carrier inner-side electron carrier ko electron deta hai aur H+ lumen mein chhodta hai, jisse stroma se lumen tak transfer poora hota hai.

141Hence, within the chloroplast, protons in the stroma decrease in number, while in the lumen there is accumulation of protons.✓
  • Teesra contribution: stroma mein khapat Is tarah stroma mein protons ghatte hain aur lumen mein jama hote hain, jisse gradient badhta aur lumen ka pH maapne laayak neeche jaata hai.
