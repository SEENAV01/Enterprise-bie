145The particularly large cells around the vascular bundles of the C4 plants are called bundle sheath cells, and the leaves which have such anatomy are said to have ‘Kranz’ anatomy. ‘Kranz’ means ‘wreath’ and is a reflection of the arrangement of cells.✓
  • Wreath ka reveal Maize section ke andar vascular bundle ke gird particularly large bundle sheath cells dekhiye; yeh Kranz anatomy hai, jahan Kranz ka matlab wreath, ya phoolon ki maala hai.
145The bundle sheath cells may form several layers around the vascular bundles; they are characterised by having a large number of chloroplasts, thick walls impervious to gaseous exchange and no intercellular spaces.✓
  • Wreath ka reveal Yeh bundle sheath several layers bhi bana sakti hai; ab isi maala ki ek cell aur uski wall ko paas se samajhte hain.
  • Sealed neighbourhood Yahan bundle sheath cells ki thick walls aur unke beech absent intercellular spaces dekhiye; aakhir aisi packing ka faayda kya hai?
  • Sealed neighbourhood Iska seedha sa jawab hai ki gaseous exchange ke liye impervious walls CO2 ko aasani se leak nahi hone deti, jaise achchi packing dabbe ki hawa sambhaalti hai.
  • Sealed neighbourhood Saath hi in cells mein large number of chloroplasts hote hain; ab dhoop se roshan isi jade chloroplast ke andar chalte hain.

NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • concept Aaiye jaante hain. Plant ko externally dekhkar C3 ya C4 reliably nahi bata sakte, kyunki dono ke patte hare ho sakte hain.
    Dono ka hara hona external appearance ki limitation samjhata hai.
  • pehle ki baat Pehle yaad kar lein ki dhoop photosynthesis ko energy deti hai aur patte ke mesophyll cells mein chloroplasts hote hain; ab tissue ko paas se dekhte hain.
    Mesophyll, chloroplast aur light energy ka prerequisite.
  • concept Dekhiye, light mein is C3 section ka palisade aur spongy mesophyll alag dikhta hai, jabki maize mein bundle ke gird special arrangement ubharti hai.
    Typical C3 dicot ka example hai; har C3 leaf ka mesophyll aisa hona zaroori nahi.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Diverse species ke patte collect karke sections compare kijiye; maize ya sorghum mein Kranz anatomy aur mesophyll distribution dekhiye, jaise kapde ki bunai paas se pehchaante hain.
    Kapde ki bunai ka analogy internal arrangement samjhata hai.
  • concept Maize section ke andar vascular bundle ke gird particularly large bundle sheath cells dekhiye; yeh Kranz anatomy hai, jahan Kranz ka matlab wreath, ya phoolon ki maala hai.
    Phoolon ki maala se wreath ki arrangement samjhayi.
  • concept C3 aur C4 pehchaanne ke liye kaunsi internal structure dekhein? Iska jawab hai vascular bundle ki bundle sheath, par sirf uski presence nahi, specialised Kranz arrangement dekhiye.
    C3 mein bhi bundle sheath ho sakti hai, isliye presence akeli diagnostic nahi.
  • concept Iska seedha sa jawab hai ki gaseous exchange ke liye impervious walls CO2 ko aasani se leak nahi hone deti, jaise achchi packing dabbe ki hawa sambhaalti hai.
    Impervious walls ka functional matlab low gas leakage; packing analogy.
  • concept Inmein photorespiration practically absent hoti hai, jisse carbon ka nuksaan bachta hai aur biomass productivity zyada hoti hai; ab sugar banne ki jagah andar dekhte hain.
    Photorespiration ka carbon-loss se basic connection aur practically absent ki precision.
  • pehle ki baat Chloroplast ke andar yeh stroma sugar-making ki jagah hai; par C4 naam hone par bhi Calvin cycle ki zaroorat kyun padti hai?
    Stroma ka prerequisite recall.
  • concept Dekhiye, C4 ka first CO2 fixation product four-carbon oxaloacetic acid, ya OAA, hota hai; woh shuruaati fixation mesophyll mein hoti hai, yahan nahi.
    Initial fixation ka mesophyll location; OAA ko bundle sheath mein banta nahi dikhaya.
  • concept Lekin main biosynthetic pathway C3 pathway, ya Calvin cycle, hi hai; jaise delivery aur rasoi alag kaam hain, carbon pahunchana aur sugar banana alag hain.
    Delivery-rasoi analogy se initial fixation aur biosynthesis ka farq.
  • concept Stroma mein Calvin cycle kaise chalta hai? Dekhiye, is wheel ki teen stations hain: fixation, reduction aur regeneration.
    Calvin cycle ka basic returning wheel; detailed energy reactions apne film mein.
  • concept Fixation mein Rubisco, CO2 ko RuBP se jodta hai, jisse 3-PGA banta hai.
    Wheel ki fixation station par CO2, RuBP aur 3-PGA ka basic sambandh.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Ab reduction mein 3-PGA se G3P banta hai, jiska kuch hissa sugar banane ke liye wheel se nikalta hai.
    G3P ka basic sugar-precursor output dikhata hai; energy cofactors ka detail yahan nahi.
  • concept Phir regeneration mein bache carbon se RuBP dobara banta hai, jisse naya CO2 pakadne ke liye wheel taiyaar rehta hai.
    Pehle se chalte wheel mein regeneration aur acceptor ki wapsi samjhayi.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Ab isi product corridor ke paas hain; G3P banne ke baad plant ko iska faayda kaise milta hai?
    Sugar precursor ko plant ke agle use se jodne wala sawaal.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Iska seedha sa jawab hai ki kuch G3P chloroplast se bahar jaata hai, jisse cell ko sugar banane ka raw material milta hai.
    Chloroplast se triose phosphate export ka basic use, transporter ke through.
