  • pehle ki baat Aaiye jaante hain: patte par light aati hai, lekin uski saari energy andar absorb nahi hoti.

    Green appearance ko reflection aur transmission se jodta hai.
  • pehle ki baat Ab hum mesophyll cell ke andar hain, jahan chloroplast tak light pahunchti hai.
    Mesophyll aur chloroplast ka spatial prerequisite.
  • pehle ki baat Jaise solar panel ko roshni chahiye, waise hi yahan light ki energy kaam aati hai; aaiye isi chloroplast mein chalein.
    Solar panel ki everyday analogy.
  • pehle ki baat Chloroplast ke andar yeh thylakoid band chapti thailiyon ki tarah hain; light inki membrane tak aati hai.
    Closed thylakoid aur membrane ki location recall.
  • concept Dekhiye, chlorophyll b light absorb karke energy chlorophyll a ko deta hai, jaise relay mein baton aage jaata hai.
    Energy transfer ki relay analogy; pigment khud travel nahi karta.

    Photo-oxidation ka saral arth.

    Carotenoid excitation quenching aur safety-valve analogy.

    Chief pigment ko charge separation ke basic mechanism se jodta hai.
