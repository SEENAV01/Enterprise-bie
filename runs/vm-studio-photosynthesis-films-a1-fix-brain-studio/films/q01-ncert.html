  • pehle ki baat Aaiye jaante hain; pehle yaad kijiye ki khana humein badhne ka samaan aur kaam karne ki energy deta hai.
    Khana energy ke saath growth ka material bhi deta hai.
  • concept Dekhiye, roti ka connection gehun se hai, aur dahi ka doodh dene wali gaay ke chaare se; insaan aur doosre animals food ke liye plants par depend karte hain.
    Roti aur dahi se direct aur indirect plant dependence trace hoti hai; leaf endpoint paudhon ka connection dikhata hai.
  • concept Yeh photosynthesis se apna organic food banate hain, isliye autotrophs kehlate hain; samajhiye, apni rasoi khud chalate hain.
    Khud rasoi chalane ki analogy autotroph ka arth samjhati hai.
  • pehle ki baat Patte ke andar yeh mesophyll cell hai, jiske kinaare hare chloroplasts hain; ab in tak sunlight pahunchti dekhiye.
    Mesophyll cell aur chloroplast ka basic location diya hai; detailed compartmentation agle film mein hai.
  • pehle ki baat Chloroplast ke andar yeh chapte thylakoids ke stacks hain, aur inke beech fluid stroma hai.
    Thylakoid aur stroma ka basic spatial context diya hai.
  • concept Dekhiye, photosynthesis ek physico-chemical process hai: light pakadne ke physical step se chemical reactions ko energy milti hai, jisse organic compounds bante hain.
    Physico-chemical shabd ko light absorption aur chemical reactions mein khola hai.
  • concept Yaani photosynthetic machinery ki reactions light energy ko chemical energy mein badalti hain; jaise solar charging mein energy sambhalti hai, yahan food mein sambhalti hai.
    Solar charging ki analogy energy conversion aur storage ko samjhati hai; spark energy ka visual sanket hai.
  • concept Ab stroma ke paas hain, jahan enzymes reactions chalate hain; par food banane ka carbon aata kahan se hai?
    Organic food ke carbon source ki zaroori bridge question.
  • pehle ki baat Isme kaam aati hai CO2, jo stroma ke enzymes tak pahunchti hai; isi ka carbon aage organic food ka hissa banta hai.
    CO2 organic food ka carbon source hai; Calvin cycle ki details yahan nahi kholi hain.
  • concept Kai reactions ke baad sugar jaise organic food milte hain, isliye photosynthesis dharti ke food ka primary source hai.
    Sugar ko organic food ka example diya hai; output kai reactions ka net nateeja hai.
  • concept Saath hi photosynthesis mein bani oxygen thylakoid se bahar nikalti hai aur aage atmosphere tak pahunchti hai; hare paudhe hawa ko oxygen dete hain.
    Oxygen ka chloroplast se bahar diffusion basic roop mein dikhaya hai; uski water-origin chemistry alag film mein hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Ab wapas thaali par aaiye: paudhon se juda yeh food hamare andar jaata hai, jisse humein jeene ka material aur energy milti hai.
    Plant food ka growth aur energy ke liye human use dikhaya hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Yahan chehre ke paas dekhiye: oxygen na ho toh hamara aerobic respiration nahi chal paayega; ab oxygen saans ke saath andar aati hai, jisse food se energy nikaalne mein madad milti hai.
    Oxygen ka aerobic respiration mein role aur inhalation dikhaya hai.
