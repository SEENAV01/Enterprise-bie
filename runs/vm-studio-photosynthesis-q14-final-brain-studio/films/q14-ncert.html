q14 · Jama hue protons wapas niklein toh ATP kaise ban jaata hai?

12 / 12 NCERT lines film mein padhayi gayi · NCERT page 141, 142, 151

Film ka dhaancha: sawaal: 1 · pehle ki baat: 4 · concept: 13 · kahan kaam aata hai / asar: 3 · yaad rakhiye: 1

141Why are we so interested in the proton gradient?✓
  • Band thylakoid ka gradient Aakhir proton gradient mein itni dilchaspi kyun? Iski wajah samajhne ke liye ab membrane ke paas chalte hain.
141This gradient is important because it is the breakdown of this gradient that leads to the synthesis of ATP.✓
  • Gradient mein stored energy Isse baandh ke peechhe jama paani jaisa samajhiye; H+ lautne se gradient ghatta hai, jisse ATP synthesis ki energy milti hai.
141The gradient is broken down due to the movement of protons across the membrane to the stroma through the transmembrane channel of the CF0 of the ATP synthase.✓
  • CF0 aur CF1 Yahaan ATP synthase ke do parts hain; membrane mein dhansa CF0, H+ ko lumen se stroma ki taraf facilitated diffusion ka raasta deta hai.
142The ATP synthase enzyme consists of two parts: one called the CF0 is embedded in the thylakoid membrane and forms a transmembrane channel that carries out facilitated diffusion of protons across the membrane.✓
  • CF0 aur CF1 Yahaan ATP synthase ke do parts hain; membrane mein dhansa CF0, H+ ko lumen se stroma ki taraf facilitated diffusion ka raasta deta hai.
142The other portion is called CF1 and protrudes on the outer surface of the thylakoid membrane on the side that faces the stroma.✓
  • CF0 aur CF1 Doosra part CF1 hai, jo thylakoid membrane ki baahari satah par stroma ki taraf nikla hai.
142The break down of the gradient provides enough energy to cause a conformational change in the CF1 particle of the ATP synthase, which makes the enzyme synthesise several molecules of energy-packed ATP.✓
  • Shape badalti hai, ATP banta hai Iska seedha sa jawab hai: CF0 se H+ lautne par gradient ghatta hai, aur mili energy stalk ko ghumaakar CF1 ki shape badalti hai.
  • Shape badalti hai, ATP banta hai Isi conformational change se CF1, ADP aur Pi ko jodkar energy-packed ATP ke kai molecules banata hai.
142Chemiosmosis requires a membrane, a proton pump, a proton gradient and ATP synthase.✓
  • Gradient mein stored energy Chemiosmosis ke liye membrane, proton pump, proton gradient aur ATP synthase chahiye; ab isi ATP synthase ko paas se dekhte hain.
  • Leak mein energy ka nuksaan Nahin, yahaan H+ ATP synthase ko bypass karte hain, isliye gradient ki energy waste hoti hai; chemiosmosis ke liye intact membrane zaroori hai.
142Energy is used to pump protons across a membrane, to create a gradient or a high concentration of protons within the thylakoid lumen.✓
  • Gradient mein stored energy Yahaan proton pump energy lekar H+ ko stroma se lumen mein bhejta hai, jisse lumen mein unki concentration zyada ho jaati hai.
142ATP synthase has a channel that allows diffusion of protons back across the membrane; this releases enough energy to activate ATP synthase enzyme that catalyses the formation of ATP.✓
  • Shape badalti hai, ATP banta hai Yaani channel se proton diffusion enzyme ko kaam karne ki energy deta hai; ATPase ka F0 bhi isi CF0 part ka naam hai.
142Along with the NADPH produced by the movement of electrons, the ATP will be used immediately in the biosynthetic reaction taking place in the stroma, responsible for fixing CO2, and synthesis of sugars.✓
  • ATP ka stroma mein istemaal Ab intact membrane ke stroma waale hisse mein lautte hain; bana ATP turant biosynthetic reactions tak pahunchta hai.
  • ATP ka stroma mein istemaal Par sugar banane mein aur kya chahiye? Isme kaam aata hai electrons ki movement se bana NADPH, jo yahaan reducing power deta hai.
  • ATP ka stroma mein istemaal Inki madad se stroma ki reactions CO2 fix karti hain aur kai steps ke baad sugars banaati hain, jo paudhe ka khana banti hain.
151During this process a proton gradient is created across the membrane of the thylakoid.✓
  • Band thylakoid ka gradient Light reactions ke dauraan lumen mein H+ jama hote hain, jisse thylakoid membrane ke aar-paar proton gradient banta hai.
151The breakdown of the protons gradient due to movement through the F0 part of the ATPase enzyme releases enough energy for synthesis of ATP.✓
  • Shape badalti hai, ATP banta hai Iska seedha sa jawab hai: CF0 se H+ lautne par gradient ghatta hai, aur mili energy stalk ko ghumaakar CF1 ki shape badalti hai.
  • Shape badalti hai, ATP banta hai Yaani channel se proton diffusion enzyme ko kaam karne ki energy deta hai; ATPase ka F0 bhi isi CF0 part ka naam hai.

NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • pehle ki baat Aaiye jaante hain. Yeh patta dhoop le raha hai; ab isi ke andar chalte hain.
    Patte se andar ki journey ka spatial bridge.
  • pehle ki baat Ab hum patte ki cell mein hain, jiske chloroplast dhoop ki energy ko chemical energy mein badalte hain.
    Photosynthesis ki location aur energy conversion ka prerequisite.
  • pehle ki baat Chloroplast ke andar yeh thylakoid ek band chapti thaili hai; iske lumen ke baahar stroma hai.
    Band lumen aur baahar ke stroma ki spatial pehchaan.
  • concept Isse baandh ke peechhe jama paani jaisa samajhiye; H+ lautne se gradient ghatta hai, jisse ATP synthesis ki energy milti hai.
    Baandh ka paani stored gradient energy ki everyday analogy hai.
  • concept Iska seedha sa jawab hai: CF0 se H+ lautne par gradient ghatta hai, aur mili energy stalk ko ghumaakar CF1 ki shape badalti hai.
    Central stalk rotation ko CF1 conformational change se jodta hai.
  • concept Isi conformational change se CF1, ADP aur Pi ko jodkar energy-packed ATP ke kai molecules banata hai.
    ADP aur Pi ko ATP synthesis ke substrates ke roop mein dikhata hai.
  • concept Yaani channel se proton diffusion enzyme ko kaam karne ki energy deta hai; ATPase ka F0 bhi isi CF0 part ka naam hai.
    F0 aur CF0 ki terminology ka sambandh saaf karta hai.
  • concept Ab paas ki membrane mein ek leak ho jaaye, toh kya H+ ke nikalne se bhi ATP banega?
    Hypothetical leak se coupling ka sawaal uthata hai.
  • concept Nahin, yahaan H+ ATP synthase ko bypass karte hain, isliye gradient ki energy waste hoti hai; chemiosmosis ke liye intact membrane zaroori hai.
    Uncontrolled leak gradient ko ATP synthesis se uncouple karke energy waste karta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Par sugar banane mein aur kya chahiye? Isme kaam aata hai electrons ki movement se bana NADPH, jo yahaan reducing power deta hai.
    NADPH ki reducing power ka role ATP ki energy se alag karta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Inki madad se stroma ki reactions CO2 fix karti hain aur kai steps ke baad sugars banaati hain, jo paudhe ka khana banti hain.
    Sugar ko kai enzymatic steps ka overall outcome aur paudhe ka khana batata hai; cycle ki details nahin kholta.
