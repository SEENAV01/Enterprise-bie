### Money: acceptance, scope and characteristics · definition

Book basis: Money is anything accepted for buying goods and services and settling debts, so it includes cash, bank deposits and accepted digital balances rather than notes and coins alone. Good money is acceptable, durable, portable, divisible and uniform; explain uniformity through equal monetary units, not by claiming that different denominations have equal values. The book adds that limited supply helps preserve value.

### Four functions of money · classification

Book basis: Money serves as medium of exchange, unit of account, store of value and standard of deferred payment. ### Barter and the problems money solves · cause_effect

Book basis: Barter directly exchanges goods and services without money, with value settled by mutual agreement; it was common in early civilizations and survives in limited informal exchanges. Both parties must want what the other offers, while the absence of a common value measure, indivisibility, difficult wealth storage and difficult deferred payment restrict exchange. ### Commodity money: uses, advantages and limitations · classification

Book basis: Commodity money has value outside monetary use and becomes a community-accepted medium, removing the need for direct double coincidence of wants. Examples are salt in Ancient Rome and Africa, cattle in pastoral societies, cowries in India, China and Africa, tea bricks in Central Asia and Tibet, and tobacco in colonial America. Differences in quality, poor divisibility, perishability, transport difficulty and limited standardisation constrain its usefulness.

### Tughlaq's token currency: credibility and state control · cause_effect

Book basis: In 1329–1330 CE Muhammad bin Tughlaq declared brass and copper tokens equal in face value to the silver Tanka, aiming to address military and administrative costs, silver scarcity and trade's monetary needs. ### Gresham's Law and selective spending · cause_effect

Book basis: ‘Bad money drives out good money’ applies under the stated conditions of legal-tender currencies, a government-fixed exchange value, undervaluation of one currency relative to intrinsic worth and freedom to choose what to spend.
