h11-6-2b · Cyclic aur non-cyclic photophosphorylation mein kya difference hai?

140When only PS I is functional, the electron is circulated within the photosystem and the phosphorylation occurs due to cyclic flow of electrons (Figure 11.6).✓
  • Electron ka hisaab Electron flow ATP synthesis support karta hai, lekin ek loop ko exactly ek ATP ke barabar nahi maan sakte.
  • Bus se electron tak Yahaan travelling token electron hai, circular route PS I ka cyclic flow hai, aur depot P700 hai.
  • Chloroplast mein jagah Sirf PS I functional ho, toh excited electron transport chain se PS I par laut sakta hai aur ATP-only cyclic photophosphorylation hoti hai.
  • Dono routes Cyclic flow mein P700 excite hota hai, electron acceptor aur transport system se guzarta hai, phir P700 par laut aata hai.
140A possible location where this could be happening is in the stroma lamellae.✓
  • Chloroplast mein jagah Stroma lamellae, wahi unstacked membrane connections, is flow ki possible location hain; inhe exclusive location mat samjho.
140While the membrane or lamellae of the grana have both PS I and PS II the stroma lamellae membranes lack PS II as well as NADP reductase enzyme.✓
  • Chloroplast mein jagah Grana membranes mein PS I aur PS II dono hote hain, jabki stroma lamellae mein PS II aur NADP reductase absent hote hain.
140The excited electron does not pass on to NADP⁺ but is cycled back to the PS I complex through the electron transport chain (Figure 11.6).✓
  • Return ticket Ab P700 se nikle ek marked electron ko follow karo: light excitation ke baad woh acceptor aur transport system se hokar P700 par laut aata hai.
  • Electron ka hisaab Iska seedha sa jawab hai, electron return route mein raha, isliye NADP⁺ ko zero net electrons mile aur NADPH nahi bana.
  • Return route ki excitation Cyclic flow mein NADP⁺ reduction ke bajay return route chalta hai, isliye net NADPH ya O₂ nahi banti.
  • Product prediction Iska seedha sa jawab hai, returning electron NADP⁺ ko reduce nahi karta aur is route mein PS II-associated water splitting nahi chalti, isliye cyclic flow ATP deta hai, net NADPH aur O₂ nahi.
  • Dono routes Cyclic flow mein P700 excite hota hai, electron acceptor aur transport system se guzarta hai, phir P700 par laut aata hai.
140The cyclic flow hence, results only in the synthesis of ATP, but not of NADPH + H⁺.✓

140Cyclic photophosphorylation also occurs when only light of wavelengths beyond 680 nm are available for excitation.✓
  • Return route ki excitation Jab excitation ke liye sirf 680 nm se beyond wavelengths available hon, tab bhi cyclic photophosphorylation ho sakti hai.

NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • concept Ab P700 se nikle ek marked electron ko follow karo: light excitation ke baad woh acceptor aur transport system se hokar P700 par laut aata hai.
    Marked electron se return route track karte hain.
  • concept Starting P700 ne ek electron diya aur loop complete hone par ek electron wapas paaya.
    P700 ka minus-one phir plus-one electron hisaab.
  • concept Is batch se NADP⁺ ko kitne net electrons mile?
    Net electron delivery ka prediction.
  • concept Iska seedha sa jawab hai, electron return route mein raha, isliye NADP⁺ ko zero net electrons mile aur NADPH nahi bana.
    Zero delivery ko product ledger se jodte hain.
  • concept Electron flow ATP synthesis support karta hai, lekin ek loop ko exactly ek ATP ke barabar nahi maan sakte.
    Loop aur ATP ki fixed one-to-one stoichiometry nahi hoti.
  • concept Is cyclic route mein water splitting nahi judi, isliye isse net O₂ production bhi nahi hoti.
    Water splitting absent hone se oxygen output zero.
  • pehle ki baat Socho, ek bus depot se nikal kar circular route par stations se guzarti hai aur usi depot par laut aati hai.
    Circular bus trip electron return ki analogy hai.
  • pehle ki baat Doosri bus onward route lekar alag terminal tak pahunchti hai; woh is trip mein starting depot par nahi lautti.
    Onward trip net delivery samjhata hai.
  • pehle ki baat Dono trips mein movement hai, lekin unki destination ka farq kya hai?
    Movement aur net transfer ka farq.
  • pehle ki baat Hota yeh hai ki circular trip starting supply ko wapas deti hai, jabki onward trip passenger ko alag final destination tak pahunchati hai.
    Depot ko starting supply aur terminal ko final destination se map karte hain.
  • concept Yahaan travelling token electron hai, circular route PS I ka cyclic flow hai, aur depot P700 hai.
    Bus analogy ko biological parts se map karte hain.
  • concept Onward route water se photosystems ke through NADP⁺ tak non-cyclic flow hai; dono routes ATP formation support karte hain, par NADP⁺ ko delivery sirf onward route deta hai.
    Non-cyclic delivery aur shared ATP output ka mapping.
  • concept Stroma lamellae, wahi unstacked membrane connections, is flow ki possible location hain; inhe exclusive location mat samjho.
    Possible ko exclusive location se alag rakhte hain.
  • pehle ki baat NADP reductase woh enzyme hai jo supplied electrons se NADP⁺ ko NADPH banane mein madad karta hai.
    NADP reductase ka basic catalytic role.
  • pehle ki baat Ab aap sochoge, NADP⁺ reduction ke electrons kis carrier se milte hain?
    Carrier aur enzyme ke roles ka sawaal.
  • concept Hota yeh hai ki ferredoxin naam ka carrier electrons supply karta hai aur NADP reductase reaction catalyse karta hai; aksar log dono roles ko ek samajhte hain, par asal mein yeh alag hain.
    Ferredoxin donor aur reductase catalyst ke alag roles.
  • concept Cyclic flow mein NADP⁺ reduction ke bajay return route chalta hai, isliye net NADPH ya O₂ nahi banti.
    Oxygen absence ko return-route comparison se jodte hain.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Socho, phir aage badhenge: PS I ka electron acceptor aur carriers se P700 par laut raha hai; kya ATP ke saath NADPH aur oxygen bhi zaroor banengi?
    Return route se products predict karne ka checkpoint.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Apna jawab electron ki destination aur water-splitting requirement se jodo.
    Destination aur oxygen source se reasoning.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Iska seedha sa jawab hai, returning electron NADP⁺ ko reduce nahi karta aur is route mein PS II-associated water splitting nahi chalti, isliye cyclic flow ATP deta hai, net NADPH aur O₂ nahi.
    PS II-associated splitting absent hone se O2 nahi milta.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Aksar log ATP bante dekhkar saare light-reaction products maan lete hain, par product list electron route par depend karti hai.
    ATP se saare products infer karne ki common mistake.
  • yaad rakhiye Ab dono routes order mein jodo: non-cyclic flow mein water electrons deta hai, PS II aur PS I successive light boosts dete hain, aur electrons NADP⁺ tak pahunchte hain.
    Non-cyclic source, photosystems aur destination ka contrast.
  • yaad rakhiye Isse ATP aur NADPH milte hain, aur water oxidation O₂ release karti hai.
    Non-cyclic products aur oxygen ka water source.
  • yaad rakhiye Is return route ka net product ATP hai; NADPH aur O₂ nahi.
    O2 absence bhi final comparison mein hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Ab dono mein ATP banta kaise hai?
    Shared ATP output ke mechanism ka agla sawaal.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Hota yeh hai ki electron route aur products pehchaan liye hain; ab thylakoid membrane ke across proton arrangement se ATP formation ka actual connection kholenge.
    Separated compartments mein proton distribution ka introduction.
  • yaad rakhiye Electron flow ATP formation support karta hai, lekin beech ka energy link abhi chhupa hai; chemiosmotic hypothesis proton gradient se yeh link samjhayegi.
    Chemiosmotic hypothesis ke agle film ka bridge.
