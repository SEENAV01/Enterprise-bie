141Hence, within the chloroplast, protons in the stroma decrease in number, while in the lumen there is accumulation of protons.✓

141Why are we so interested in the proton gradient?✓
  • Gradient mein energy Par proton gradient mein itni dilchaspi kyun?
141This gradient is important because it is the breakdown of this gradient that leads to the synthesis of ATP.✓
  • Gradient mein energy Iska seedha sa jawab hai: baandh ke peeche paani jama karne jaisi H+ pumping mein energy lagti hai; controlled return se gradient ka breakdown ATP synthesis ko energy deta hai.
141The gradient is broken down due to the movement of protons across the membrane to the stroma through the transmembrane channel of the CF₀ of the ATP synthase.✓
  • Controlled return Dekhiye, membrane mein embedded CF0 transmembrane channel deta hai, jisse H+ facilitated diffusion se lumen se stroma lautkar gradient ghataate hain.
142The ATP synthase enzyme consists of two parts: one called the CF₀ is embedded in the thylakoid membrane and forms a transmembrane channel that carries out facilitated diffusion of protons across the membrane.✓
  • Controlled return Dekhiye, membrane mein embedded CF0 transmembrane channel deta hai, jisse H+ facilitated diffusion se lumen se stroma lautkar gradient ghataate hain.
142The other portion is called CF₁ and protrudes on the outer surface of the thylakoid membrane on the side that faces the stroma.✓
  • Controlled return Doosra hissa CF1 outer surface se stroma ki taraf nikla hai, isliye ATP banne ki jagah bhi stroma side hoti hai.
142The break down of the gradient provides enough energy to cause a conformational change in the CF₁ particle of the ATP synthase, which makes the enzyme synthesise several molecules of energy-packed ATP.✓
  • Flow se turning CF0 se H+ ka return central stalk ghumata hai, jisse CF1 ke catalytic parts ki shape, ya conformational change, hoti hai.
  • Flow se turning Isi energy se CF1 par ADP aur Pi judkar energy-packed ATP banate hain, aur repeat hone par several molecules of ATP milte hain.
142Chemiosmosis requires a membrane, a proton pump, a proton gradient and ATP synthase.✓
  • Chaar zarooratein Yahin chemiosmosis ki chaar zarooratein check kijiye: membrane, proton pump, proton gradient aur ATP synthase; inmein stored energy kaam kab karti hai?
142Energy is used to pump protons across a membrane, to create a gradient or a high concentration of protons within the thylakoid lumen.✓
  • Gradient mein energy Iska seedha sa jawab hai: baandh ke peeche paani jama karne jaisi H+ pumping mein energy lagti hai; controlled return se gradient ka breakdown ATP synthesis ko energy deta hai.
142ATP synthase has a channel that allows diffusion of protons back across the membrane; this releases enough energy to activate ATP synthase enzyme that catalyses the formation of ATP.✓
  • Chaar zarooratein Jab channel se H+ wapas diffuse karte hain, released energy ATP synthase ko activate karti hai; gradient breakdown ka matlab H+ ka mitna nahi, fark ka ghatna hai.
142Along with the NADPH produced by the movement of electrons, the ATP will be used immediately in the biosynthetic reaction taking place in the stroma, responsible for fixing CO₂, and synthesis of sugars.✓
  • Stroma ko supply Dekhiye, ATP aur electron movement se bana NADPH turant stromal biosynthetic reactions ko milte hain, jisse CO2 fix hota hai aur sugars banti hain.

NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • pehle ki baat Pehle samajhiye: membrane compartments alag rakhti hai, zyada H+ se pH ghatta hai, aur diffusion electrochemical gradient, yaani concentration aur charge ke fark, ke saath hota hai.
    pH ki direction aur electrochemical gradient ke concentration aur charge components kholte hain.
  • concept Isme kaam aata hai H carrier PQ: primary acceptor electron sirf electron carrier ko nahi, PQ ko deta hai, jo stroma se H+ lekar PQH2 banta hai.
    H carrier ko PQ/PQH2 identity se jodte hain.
  • concept Phir PQH2 inner-side cytochrome b6f ko electron deta hai aur H+ lumen mein chhodta hai; electron PC se P700 PSI, phir Fd tak jaata hai.
    Figure ke carrier names locate karte hain, route ki detailed reteaching nahi.
  • concept Is reduction mein stroma ke H+ net kharch hote hain, isliye lumen mein jama kiye bina bhi dono taraf ka fark badhta hai.
    Net consumption ko lumen mein direct addition se alag karte hain.
  • concept Iska seedha sa jawab hai: baandh ke peeche paani jama karne jaisi H+ pumping mein energy lagti hai; controlled return se gradient ka breakdown ATP synthesis ko energy deta hai.
    Baandh analogy se stored energy aur controlled return samjhate hain.
  • concept Ab ATP synthase ke paas hain; baandh ki turbine jaisa controlled return kis raaste hota hai?
    Baandh analogy ko synthase ke controlled return se jodte hain.
  • concept Doosra hissa CF1 outer surface se stroma ki taraf nikla hai, isliye ATP banne ki jagah bhi stroma side hoti hai.
    Orientation se ATP formation ki side predict karte hain.
  • concept CF0 se H+ ka return central stalk ghumata hai, jisse CF1 ke catalytic parts ki shape, ya conformational change, hoti hai.
    CF0 rotor aur central stalk se catalytic conformational coupling kholte hain.
  • concept Isi energy se CF1 par ADP aur Pi judkar energy-packed ATP banate hain, aur repeat hone par several molecules of ATP milte hain.
    ADP aur Pi ko CF1 catalytic site par milte dikhate hain.
  • concept Jab channel se H+ wapas diffuse karte hain, released energy ATP synthase ko activate karti hai; gradient breakdown ka matlab H+ ka mitna nahi, fark ka ghatna hai.
    Gradient breakdown ko proton destruction se alag karte hain.
