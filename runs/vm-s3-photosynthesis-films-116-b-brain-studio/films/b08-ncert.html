NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • pehle ki baat Aaiye jaante hain; pehle samajhiye ki H+ positive proton hai, e- negative electron hai aur O2 do oxygen atoms se bani molecule hai.
    Proton, electron aur molecular oxygen ki prerequisite pehchaan.
  • concept Ticket dispenser se tickets nikalte rehne ke liye peeche refill roll chahiye; yahan water electron supply ka source banta hai.
    Ticket dispenser ke refill roll se continuous replacement samjhaya.
  • concept PS II se judi splitting of water mein ek H2O ke hisaab se do H+, [O] aur electrons milte hain; [O] oxygen ka shorthand hai.
    [O] oxygen accounting ka shorthand hai, free atom intermediate nahi.
  • concept Dekhiye, do H2O se chaar H+, ek O2 aur chaar e- milte hain; yeh kai steps ka net hisaab hai.
    Multi-step water oxidation ka net hisaab.
  • pehle ki baat Dono taraf chaar hydrogen aur do oxygen hain; chaar positive H+ aur chaar negative e- ka total charge zero hai.
    Atom aur charge conservation se equation verify ki.
  • concept Wahi ticket dispenser samajhiye: refill roll se tickets aate rehte hain, waise hi water se mile electrons PS II ko refill karte hain.
    Wahi ticket-refill analogy dobara electron replacement se jodi.
  • concept Yeh oxygen photosynthesis ke net products mein se ek hai, yaani kul milakar milne wala utpaad, aur O2 aage diffuse kar sakti hai.
    Lumen mein bani oxygen ka aage diffusion.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Isi water oxidation se bani oxygen bahar pahunchkar atmosphere ki photosynthetic oxygen mein yogdaan deti hai.
    Water oxidation ko atmospheric photosynthetic oxygen se joda.
  • concept Isme kaam aata hai PS II, jiske electrons electron transport chain se PS I tak pahunchkar uski kami bharte hain.
    PS II se PS I tak replacement ka chain-mediated route.
  • concept Isliye water splitting PS II se judi hai, PS I par alag se paani todkar refill nahi hota.
    PS I par independent photolysis wali galatfahmi door ki.
