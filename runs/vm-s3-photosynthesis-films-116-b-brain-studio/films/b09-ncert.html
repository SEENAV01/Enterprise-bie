139The process through which ATP is synthesised by cells (in mitochondria and chloroplasts) is named phosphorylation.✓
  • ATP-only challenge ka jawab Iska jawab hai: cells mein ATP synthesis phosphorylation hai, jo mitochondria aur chloroplasts mein hota hai.
140Photo-phosphorylation is the synthesis of ATP from ADP and inorganic phosphate in the presence of light.✓
  • ATP-only challenge ka jawab Light ki presence mein ADP aur inorganic phosphate, yaani Pi, se ATP banna photo-phosphorylation hai.
140When the two photosystems work in a series, first PS II and then the PS I, a process called non-cyclic photo-phosphorylation occurs.✓
  • Do routes, ek membrane network Dekhiye, pehle PS II, phir electron transport chain se juda PS I, series mein kaam karte hain; yahi Z scheme ka non-cyclic photophosphorylation hai.
140The two photosystems are connected through an electron transport chain, as seen earlier – in the Z scheme.✓
  • Do routes, ek membrane network Dekhiye, pehle PS II, phir electron transport chain se juda PS I, series mein kaam karte hain; yahi Z scheme ka non-cyclic photophosphorylation hai.
140Both ATP and NADPH + H⁺ are synthesised by this kind of electron flow (Figure 11.5).✓
  • Do routes, ek membrane network Is flow mein ATP aur NADPH + H+ dono bante hain; electron NADP+ par pahunchta hai, jaise one-way bus terminal par.
140When only PS I is functional, the electron is circulated within the photosystem and the phosphorylation occurs due to cyclic flow of electrons (Figure 11.6).✓
  • Return ticket wala electron Dekhiye, light P700 ko excite karti hai, jisse electron acceptor ki taraf uthta hai.
  • Return ticket wala electron Phir ETS electron ko PS I par lauta deti hai; yeh circular bus route hai, terminal tak one-way service nahi.
  • Sirf PS I ka light boost Ab stroma lamella ke PS I ke paas hain; sirf yahi functional ho, toh excited electron kahaan jaayega?
  • Sirf PS I ka light boost P700 light leta hai, jisse excited electron acceptor tak pahunchta hai.
  • Return se ATP ki energy Dekhiye, ETS se electron PS I par lautta hai, aur is cyclic electron flow se phosphorylation ke liye energy milti hai.
140A possible location where this could be happening is in the stroma lamellae.✓
  • Is route ka asli ghar Inmein PS II aur NADP reductase enzyme nahi hote, isliye stroma lamellae cyclic flow ki ek possible location hain.
140While the membrane or lamellae of the grana have both PS I and PS II the stroma lamellae membranes lack PS II as well as NADP reductase enzyme.✓
  • Is route ka asli ghar Baayein grana membranes mein PS I aur PS II dono hain; ab daayein stroma lamellae par chaliye.
  • Is route ka asli ghar Inmein PS II aur NADP reductase enzyme nahi hote, isliye stroma lamellae cyclic flow ki ek possible location hain.
140The excited electron does not pass on to NADP⁺ but is cycled back to the PS I complex through the electron transport chain (Figure 11.6).✓
  • Return ticket wala electron Ab acceptor electron ko NADP+ nahi, electron transport chain, yaani ETS, ki taraf bhejta hai.
  • Return ticket wala electron Phir ETS electron ko PS I par lauta deti hai; yeh circular bus route hai, terminal tak one-way service nahi.
  • Sirf PS I ka light boost Phir electron NADP+ ko nahi milta, balki ETS ki taraf mudta hai, jaise circular bus apne return route par mudti hai.
140The cyclic flow hence, results only in the synthesis of ATP, but not of NADPH + H⁺.✓
  • Return se ATP ki energy Isliye cyclic flow sirf ATP banwata hai, NADPH + H+ nahi; PS II wala water splitting na hone se O2 bhi nahi banta.
140Cyclic photophosphorylation also occurs when only light of wavelengths beyond 680 nm are available for excitation.✓
  • Sirf PS I ka light boost Cyclic photophosphorylation tab bhi hota hai jab excitation ke liye sirf 680 nm se zyada wavelength ki light available ho.
