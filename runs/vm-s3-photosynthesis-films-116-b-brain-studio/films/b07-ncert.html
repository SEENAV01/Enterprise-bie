b07 · PS II se NADP⁺ tak electron ka Z-shaped route kya hai?

10 / 10 NCERT lines film mein padhayi gayi · NCERT page 138, 139

Film ka dhaancha: sawaal: 1 · pehle ki baat: 3 · concept: 15 · kahan kaam aata hai / asar: 1 · yaad rakhiye: 1

138In photosystem II the reaction centre chlorophyll a absorbs 680 nm wavelength of red light causing electrons to become excited and jump into an orbit farther from the atomic nucleus.✓
  • P680 ka pehla boost Dekhiye, P680 680 nm red light absorb karta hai, jisse electron excited hokar nucleus se door waale higher-energy orbit mein pahunchta hai.
138These electrons are picked up by an electron acceptor which passes them to an electrons transport system consisting of cytochromes (Figure 11.5).✓
  • P680 ka pehla boost Ab electron acceptor ise pakad leta hai; acceptor ka matlab electron lene waala hai, aur ab iska agla handover dekhte hain.
  • Electron kharch nahi hota Ab membrane mein acceptor electron aage deta hai, par kya raste mein electron kharch ho jaata hai?
  • Electron kharch nahi hota Iska seedha sa jawab hai, nahi; electron transport system mein cytochromes waale carriers ise aage pass karte hain.
139This movement of electrons is downhill, in terms of an oxidation-reduction or redox potential scale.✓
  • Electron kharch nahi hota Yeh transfer oxidation-reduction, ya redox potential scale par downhill hai; energy girti hai, lekin electron consume hue bina PS I ke pigments tak pahunchta hai.
139The electrons are not used up as they pass through the electron transport chain, but are passed on to the pigments of photosystem PS I.✓
  • Electron kharch nahi hota Iska seedha sa jawab hai, nahi; electron transport system mein cytochromes waale carriers ise aage pass karte hain.
  • Electron kharch nahi hota Yeh transfer oxidation-reduction, ya redox potential scale par downhill hai; energy girti hai, lekin electron consume hue bina PS I ke pigments tak pahunchta hai.
139Simultaneously, electrons in the reaction centre of PS I are also excited when they receive red light of wavelength 700 nm and are transferred to another accepter molecule that has a greater redox potential.✓
  • P700 ka doosra boost Dekhiye, PS II ke saath-saath PS I bhi 700 nm red light leta hai, jisse P700 ke electrons excited hote hain.
  • P700 ka doosra boost Ab excited electron doosre acceptor ko milta hai, jiska redox potential greater hai; is tarah doosra light-driven boost milta hai.
139These electrons then are moved downhill again, this time to a molecule of energy-rich NADP⁺.✓
  • P700 ka doosra boost Ab yeh electrons phir downhill transfer se energy-rich NADP⁺ ki taraf badhte hain; aaiye stroma mein iska endpoint dekhte hain.
139The addition of these electrons reduces NADP⁺ to NADPH + H⁺.✓
  • NADP⁺ se NADPH Dekhiye, do electrons aur do H⁺ ke saath NADP⁺ reduce hota hai, jisse NADPH plus H⁺ milta hai; reduced carrier NADPH hai.
139This whole scheme of transfer of electrons, starting from the PS II, uphill to the acceptor, down the electron transport chain to PS I, excitation of electrons, transfer to another acceptor, and finally down hill to NADP⁺ reducing it to NADPH + H⁺ is called the Z scheme, due to its characterstic shape (Figure 11.5).✓
  • Poore route ki pehchaan Is NADPH tak route mein PS II se acceptor tak uphill transfer hota hai, phir chain se PS I tak downhill transfer hota hai.
  • Poore route ki pehchaan Phir PS I mein excitation se doosra acceptor electron leta hai, aur downhill NADP⁺ reduction se NADPH plus H⁺ milta hai; characteristic Z shape se is poore scheme ka naam Z scheme hai.
139Figure 11.5 Z scheme of light reaction✓
  • Poora route Is Z scheme of light reaction mein carriers, yaani electron aage dene waale, redox potential scale par sequence mein hain; yeh membrane ka shape nahi hai.
139This shape is formed when all the carriers are placed in a sequence on a redox potential scale.✓
  • Poora route Is Z scheme of light reaction mein carriers, yaani electron aage dene waale, redox potential scale par sequence mein hain; yeh membrane ka shape nahi hai.
