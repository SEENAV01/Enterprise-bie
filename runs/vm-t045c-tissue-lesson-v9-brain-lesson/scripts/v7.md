### Part c22.2 · cause_effect
_frame: process: Absence of intercellular spaces produces compact packing. · how: Neighbouring cells lie closely against one another without intervening gaps. · when: Explain packing after examining cell features. · conditions: Spaces between cells must be distinguished from compartments within cells. · atmosphere: Adjacent cell outlines viewed together under magnification._

**b7 · explain · step 1: the effect students can see** — साथ में देखो तो cells compactly packed हैं, यानी बहुत पास लगे हैं। क्यों?

**b8 · explain · step 2: the cause** — कारण है intercellular spaces absent होना: पड़ोसी cells के बीच gap नहीं है।

**b9 · explain · step 3: each link of the chain with its mechanism** — बीच में gap नहीं, इसलिए cells पास-पास; इसी से compact arrangement बनता है।

  _screen: Highlight cause and effect in the book statement._

**b11 · misconception · step 5: the exception or limit** — Intercellular space cells के बीच है; vacuole cell के अंदर compartment है। दोनों अलग चीजें हैं।

### Part c22.3 · process
_frame: process: Cell division supplies new cells for plant growth. · how: Cells prepare for division and divide; their descendants can enlarge and specialise. · when: During active growth; activity need not be constant under all conditions. · conditions: Division needs cellular preparation; without division the region does not supply new cells through that mechanism. · atmosphere: An active meristem followed through a simplified division sequence._

**b13 · explain · step 1: the result first (what it makes)** — इनकी मुख्य activity cell division है। इससे cell number बढ़ता है और growth के लिए नए cells मिलते हैं।
  _screen: Division supplies new cells._

**b14 · explain · step 2: inputs and the place** — Active meristematic region में division के लिए genetic material की copying, cellular components और energy चाहिए।
  _screen: Active region and division requirements._
