### Part c07.1 · structure
_frame: process: Epithelium forms continuous coverings and linings over organs and cavities. · how: Closely packed cells with little intervening material lie above an extracellular fibrous basement membrane. · when: Read spatially from the exposed sheet through adjacent cells to the tissue beneath. · conditions: Intercellular spaces are almost absent; the book qualifies basement-membrane separation as usual. · atmosphere: A magnified section through an organ covering or cavity lining._

**b2 · explain · step 1: the whole in context** — Epithelium most organs को cover करता और body cavities को line करता है। Covering structure के ऊपर है; lining अंदर की जगह या passage के साथ है।
  _screen: Organ covering and cavity lining highlighted._

**b3 · explain · step 2: zoom into each part in a fixed path (outside to inside)** — ऊपर से नीचे देखो। Tightly packed epithelial cells continuous sheet बनाते हैं। बीच में थोड़ा cementing material और लगभग कोई intercellular spaces नहीं—यानी पड़ोसी cells के बीच खाली जगह लगभग नहीं।

**b4 · explain · step 2: zoom into each part in a fixed path (outside to inside)** — नीचे extracellular fibrous basement membrane usually epithelium को underlying tissue से अलग करती है। Extracellular यानी cells के बाहर; यह individual cell को घेरने वाली cell membrane नहीं है।

### Part c07.2 · cause_effect
_frame: process: An epithelial barrier keeps different body systems separate. · how: A continuous closely joined sheet limits uncontrolled passage while specialised transport remains possible. · when: Barrier and permitted transport operate together, not as successive stages. · conditions: Barrier does not mean complete impermeability; transport depends on epithelial type. · atmosphere: Neighbouring body compartments separated by a living lining._

**b8 · explain · step 1: the effect students can see** — इस arrangement का नतीजा separation है। Epithelium barrier बनाकर different body systems को अलग रखता है।
  _screen: Compartments separated by epithelium._

**b9 · explain · step 2: the cause** — कारण close packing है। जुड़े हुए cells खुली gaps कम रखते हैं, इसलिए boundary के आर-पार uncontrolled passage कम होता है।
  _screen: Close packing limits open gaps._

**b10 · explain · step 3: each link of the chain with its mechanism** — Cells और junctions uncontrolled passage सीमित करते हैं। इससे पड़ोसी compartments में अलग conditions बनी रह सकती हैं—यही arrangement से barrier तक का link है।
  _screen: Joined cells → restricted passage → separate conditions._

**b11 · explain · step 4: the evidence** — Book का continuous sheet और लगभग absent spaces वाला description इसका आधार है। उसी table में transport functions भी हैं।
  _screen: Sheet description alongside transport functions._

**b12 · misconception · step 5: the exception or limit** — Barrier मतलब हर substance पर रोक नहीं। Type के हिसाब से selected substances diffusion, absorption या secretion से पार जा सकते हैं।

### Part c07.3 · process
_frame: process: Epithelial specialisation and inward folding produce glandular epithelium. · how: Cells acquire secretion capability; sometimes a portion of the sheet folds inward to form a multicellular gland. · when: Specialisation and the described inward-folding sequence are taught without assigning an age or duration. · conditions: Folding occurs sometimes, not in every epithelial sheet; no failure pathology is supplied. · atmosphere: A surface sheet viewed in section as part of it folds into underlying tissue._

**b14 · explain · step 1: the result first (what it makes)** — एक specialisation glandular epithelium है। Epithelial cells gland cells बनकर surface पर substances secrete कर सकते हैं। Secretion substance बनाकर release करना है, केवल covering नहीं।
