### Part c37.1 · definition
_frame: process: Identify directly RBI-created reserve money. · how: An RBI security purchase credits a bank's reserve account. · when: Central-bank money provides a base for subsequent banking activity. · conditions: Reserves support settlement and potential lending, not compulsory lending. · atmosphere: RBI and a commercial bank's reserve account._

**b22 · example · step 1: a concrete example first** — Bank का RBI balance कैसे बढ़े? RBI ₹100 की security खरीदकर credit करे, तो उसका balance ₹100 से ₹200 होगा।
  _screen: Hypothetical purchase; RBI account ₹100 → ₹200._

**b23 · explain · step 2: the book's definition word by word** — सीधे RBI-created base M0 है: Reserve Money या High-Powered Money। Building के foundation जैसा, यह आगे bank money बनने का आधार है।
  _screen: M0 foundation beneath bank deposits._

**b24 · misconception · step 3: a non-example that tests the boundary** — तुम्हारा deposit M0 नहीं। Bank का RBI balance अलग है; यह withdrawals और interbank payments सँभालता है।

  _screen: Reserve base supports conditional lending._

### Part c37.3 · cause_effect
_frame: process: Bank lending creates deposit money across the banking system. · how: An ₹80 loan and a subsequent ₹60 loan create ₹140 of deposits under stated assumptions. · when: Lending and interbank settlement occur in successive rounds. · conditions: Reserves, capital, liquidity, lending standards and credit demand constrain expansion. · atmosphere: Two banks, a business borrower and a supplier._

**b31 · example · step 1: the effect students can see** — Loan से balance कैसे बढ़ता है? सारी ज़रूरी शर्तें पूरी मानो: Bank A ₹80 loan deposit बनाता है; borrower Bank B के supplier को देता है।
  _screen: ₹80 loan creates deposit; supplier receives payment at Bank B._

**b32 · example · step 2: the cause** — Bank B शर्तें पूरी करके ₹60 loan देता है। Repayments और cash leakage छोड़ें तो इन loans से नए deposits ₹0 से ₹140 हुए।
  _screen: Second loan ₹60; illustrated new deposits total ₹140._

**b33 · explain · step 3: each link of the chain with its mechanism** — यह reserves-supported multiple expansion है। Loan deposit बनाता है; banks के बीच payment settle करने के लिए reserve base चाहिए।
  _screen: Reserve support → lending → deposit → settlement._

**b34 · explain · step 4: the evidence** — Account में loan credit देखो। Recipient पैसे bank में रखे तो deposits system में रहते हैं।
  _screen: Loan credit followed by recipient's bank balance._

**b35 · misconception · step 5: the exception or limit** — Expansion guaranteed नहीं: withdrawals, reserve needs, capital, lending standards और loan demand इसे रोक सकते हैं। Bank बिना सीमा lending नहीं कर सकता।
