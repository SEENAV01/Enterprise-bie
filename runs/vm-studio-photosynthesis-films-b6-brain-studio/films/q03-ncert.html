134Using a similar setup as the one used by Priestley, but by placing it once in the dark and once in the sunlight, Jan Ingenhousz (1730-1799) showed that sunlight is essential to the plant process that somehow purifies the air fouled by burning candles or breathing animals.✓
  • Light ka test Iska jawab Jan Ingenhousz dete hain, jinka jeevankaal 1730 se 1799 tha: similar setup ko kabhi dark aur kabhi sunlight mein rakhkar woh dikhaate hain ki candles aur animals se kharaab air sudhaarne ke liye sunlight zaroori hai.
134Ingenhousz in an elegant experiment with an aquatic plant showed that in bright sunlight, small bubbles were formed around the green parts while in the dark they did not.✓
  • Green parts ke bubbles Dekhiye, aquatic plant ke green parts ke aas-paas chhote bubbles bante aur upar uthte hain; isi plant ko dark mein rakhne par yeh bubbles nahi bante.
134Later he identified these bubbles to be of oxygen.✓
  • Green parts ke bubbles Baad mein Ingenhousz in bubbles ko oxygen pehchaante hain, jisse saaf hota hai ki sunlight mein sirf green parts oxygen release karte hain.
