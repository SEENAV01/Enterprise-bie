NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Aaiye jaante hain: suitable light mein C3 crops tomatoes aur bell pepper ko CO2-enriched atmosphere dene se photosynthesis badhti hai, jisse higher productivity aur yield mil sakti hai.
    Suitable light ki shart aur shoot growth se productivity ka basic example.
  • pehle ki baat Patte par yeh light padti hai; photosynthesis mein light energy deti hai aur CO2 khana banane ke liye carbon deta hai.
    Light energy aur CO2 carbon source ka prerequisite.
  • pehle ki baat Jaise solar panel dhoop se battery charge karta hai, waise patta light ki energy ko chemical energy mein badalta hai.
    Solar panel aur battery ki everyday analogy se light ka energy dene wala role samjhaya.
  • pehle ki baat Patte ke andar is mesophyll cell mein chloroplasts hain, jahan light ki energy photosynthesis ke kaam aati hai.
    Mesophyll aur chloroplast ki spatial prerequisite.
  • concept Iska seedha sa jawab hai: low light mein C3 aur C4, dono groups high CO2 ka khaas response nahi dete, kyunki energy ki supply kam padti hai.
    Low-light response ko energy limitation se samjhaya.
  • concept Jaise bijli kam ho toh factory mein extra maal se machine tez nahi chalti; ab isi chloroplast ke andar chalte hain.
    Bijli aur raw material ki factory analogy.
  • pehle ki baat Chloroplast ke andar thylakoid stacks light pakadte hain, aur aas-paas stroma mein carbon fixation hoti hai.
    Light capture aur carbon fixation ki jagah.
  • pehle ki baat Stroma mein yeh Rubisco hai; ab sawaal banta hai ki CO2 ka carbon yahan pakda kaise jaata hai?
    Fixation ke molecular mechanism ka sawaal.
  • pehle ki baat Dekhiye, Rubisco par CO2 aur RuBP milte hain, jisse do 3-PGA molecules bante hain.
    Ek CO2 aur RuBP se do 3-PGA ka basic fixation context.
  • concept Yeh 3-PGA aage sugar banane ke steps mein jaata hai, jinhe light se milne wale ATP aur NADPH ki supply bhi chahiye.
    Sugar synthesis ke liye energy supply ka context.
  • concept Ise chulhe par roti banana samajhiye: extra aata tabhi kaam aata hai jab aanch bhi ho; ab C3 aur C4 ka response graph par compare karte hain.
    Chulhe par roti ki everyday picture: aata carbon supply jaisa aur aanch energy supply jaisi hai.
  • concept Is graph mein CO2 daayein aur photosynthesis rate upar badhti hai; par high light mein dono kahaan rukte hain?
    Graph ke axes ka matlab samjhaya.
  • concept Dekhiye, C4 lagbhag 360 microlitre per litre par saturate hota hai, jiske baad extra CO2 rate nahi badhata.
    Saturation ka seedha arth samjhaya.
