15011.10.3 Temperature The dark reactions being enzymatic are temperature controlled.✓
  • Enzymes ka temperature window Nahi, inhe seedhe photons nahi chahiye; yahaan CO2 enzymes tak aati hai, aur yeh reactions enzymatic hone se temperature-controlled hain, jaise rasoi ka kaam sahi garmi par behtar hota hai.
150Though the light reactions are also temperature sensitive they are affected to a much lesser extent.✓
  • Garmi ke do targets Light reactions bhi temperature-sensitive hain, lekin carbon reactions ke mukaable un par temperature ka asar bahut kam padta hai.
150The C4 plants respond to higher temperatures and show higher rate of photosynthesis while C3 plants have a much lower temperature optimum.✓
  • Har paudhe ka optimum alag Nahi, C4 higher temperatures par higher photosynthesis rate dikhaate hain, jabki C3 ka optimum kaafi lower hai.
150The temperature optimum for photosynthesis of different plants also depends on the habitat that they are adapted to.✓
  • Har paudhe ka optimum alag Optimum habitat adaptation par bhi depend karta hai; tropical plants ka optimum temperate climates ke plants se generally higher hai.
150Tropical plants have a higher temperature optimum than the plants adapted to temperate climates.✓
  • Har paudhe ka optimum alag Optimum habitat adaptation par bhi depend karta hai; tropical plants ka optimum temperate climates ke plants se generally higher hai.
15011.10.4 Water Even though water is one of the reactants in the light reaction, the effect of water as a factor is more through its effect on the plant, rather than directly on photosynthesis.✓
  • Band darwaaze par CO2 Paani light reaction ka reactant zaroor hai, par iska bada asar plant ke badlaav se aata hai, na ki photosynthesis par sirf seedhi paani ki kami se.
150Water stress causes the stomata to close hence reducing the CO2 availability.✓
  • Band darwaaze par CO2 Dekhiye, water stress stomata ko close karata hai, jisse CO2 availability ghat jaati hai; is sankre raaste se ab bahut kam CO2 andar pahunchti hai.
150Besides, water stress also makes leaves wilt, thus, reducing the surface area of the leaves and their metabolic activity as well.✓
  • Paani support bhi deta hai Is water stress se leaves wilt hoti hain, jisse exposed surface area aur metabolic activity dono ghatte hain; ab neeche stomata ke paas chaliye.

NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • pehle ki baat Aaiye jaante hain; patte ko sugar banane ke liye roshni, paani aur hawa se CO2 chahiye.
    Sugar production ke basic inputs ka prerequisite recall.
  • concept Yahaan patte ke andar paani ghat raha hai; aakhir patta dheela kyun padta hai?
    Water loss ko wilting ke sawal se jodta hai.
  • pehle ki baat Iska seedha sa jawab hai ki cells ka turgor ghatta hai, jaise hawa kam hone par gubbaara dheela padta hai.
    Turgor loss ko gubbaare ki everyday analogy se samjhata hai.
  • concept Is water stress se leaves wilt hoti hain, jisse exposed surface area aur metabolic activity dono ghatte hain; ab neeche stomata ke paas chaliye.
    Surface area ko exposed area ke roop mein spasht karta hai.
  • concept Ab hum stomata par hain; paani bachane ke liye yeh darwaaza band ho, toh CO2 ka kya hoga?
    Darwaaze ki analogy water conservation aur carbon supply ka trade-off kholti hai.
  • pehle ki baat Ab hum mesophyll cell mein hain, jahaan bachi hui CO2 chloroplast ki taraf pahunchti hai.
    Stomatal supply ko mesophyll chloroplast tak spatially jodta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Factory mein maal kam aaye toh production ghatta hai; waise hi kam CO2 milne par sugar banane ki raftaar ghat sakti hai.
    CO2 limitation ka sugar output par effect aur factory analogy.
  • pehle ki baat Chloroplast ke andar thylakoid ki band chapti thailiyaan hain, aur baahar stroma hai; roshni thylakoid par light reactions chalati hai.
    Light aur carbon reactions ki jagahon ka prerequisite recall.
  • pehle ki baat Ab stroma ke enzymes ke paas hain; carbon reactions ko dark reactions bhi kehte hain, par kya yeh sirf andhere mein chalti hain?
    Dark ka matlab night-only hone ki galatfahmi kholta hai.
  • concept Nahi, inhe seedhe photons nahi chahiye; yahaan CO2 enzymes tak aati hai, aur yeh reactions enzymatic hone se temperature-controlled hain, jaise rasoi ka kaam sahi garmi par behtar hota hai.
    Direct photon requirement ka farq, CO2 ka enzymes tak aana aur rasoi ki analogy.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar CO2 se kai enzyme-driven steps ke baad sugar banti hai; optimum se zyada garmi par poori production chain slow pad sakti hai.
    Optimum se upar sugar output ghatne ka functional outcome.
  • concept Kya sabka temperature optimum, yaani maximum photosynthesis wala temperature, ek hai?
    Optimum ka matlab maximum rate wala temperature samjhata hai.
