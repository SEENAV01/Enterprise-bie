### Part c17.3 · cause_effect
_frame: process: Heart-wall contraction helps pump and distribute blood to body tissues. · how: Coordinated contraction produces pressure, moving blood into vessels and supporting tissue supply. · when: Blood is ejected during contraction, and relaxation permits renewed filling. · conditions: Pumping must be adequate for tissue oxygen and nutrient requirements. · atmosphere: A heart-to-vessel-to-body view showing the consequence of adequate or inadequate flow._

**b22 · explain · step 1: the effect students can see** — अब इस muscle activity का नतीजा देखिए। Heart खून को pump करता है और शरीर तक भेजने में मदद करता है। Cardiac muscle का काम केवल heart का आकार बदलना नहीं, blood flow बनाना भी है।
  _screen: Heart → blood vessels → body tissues; highlight pumping and distribution._

  _screen: Cause chain: coordinated contraction → chamber pressure → blood enters vessels._
