### Part c01.2 · classification
_frame: process: Tissues are grouped as plant or animal tissues by the organism supplying their cells. · how: Read a two-branch tree, identify each branch, and clarify that combining does not mean cell fusion. · when: After the tissue definition and before explaining division of labour. · conditions: Use cell origin for this division; shared function still matters within each tissue. · atmosphere: Place a growing plant beside an animal and compare their organised cell groups._

**b21 · explain · step 1: the basis of division first** — पहला division cells किस organism की हैं, इस basis पर है। Plant की cells से plant tissues बनते हैं; animal की cells से animal tissues। यहां फर्क size या रंग का नहीं, cells के plant या animal होने का है।
