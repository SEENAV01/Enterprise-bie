## g1 · Bullet kitni oonchai tak jaayegi?

- Sawaal: A bullet is fired from a gun at 280 m/s, at 30° above the horizontal. What is the maximum height? Take g = 9.8 m/s² and sin 30° = 0.5.
- Model: projectile · model se jaancha: sahi (option 4)
- Concept: Maximum height initial velocity ke vertical component se milti hai, poori speed se nahi.
- Gates: 3 · families: 2 · rounds: 2
- Review ke liye (learner ko nahi dikhta): Ideal projectile model ignores drag, wind and variation of gravity; a real bullet need not reach this height.; The whole-speed trap gives a value outside the supplied options.; Options 1 and 2 do not uniquely identify a specific misconception.; Same-level landing is needed for full-flight quantities, not for maximum rise.

## p1 · Range: poori flight ka horizontal safar

- Sawaal: A ball is thrown from level ground at 20 m/s, 60° above the horizontal. Taking g = 10 m/s², what is its horizontal range?
- Model: projectile · model se jaancha: sahi (option 2)
- Concept: Horizontal speed constant rehti hai; vertical motion flight ka samay tay karta hai.
- Gates: 4 · families: 2 · rounds: 2
- Review ke liye (learner ko nahi dikhta): Same-level flight-time aur range relations unequal landing height par seedhe apply nahi hote.; Air drag aur changing gravity model mein included nahi hain.; Option 3 aur option 4 ke liye dedicated projectile trap identifiers available nahi hain.
