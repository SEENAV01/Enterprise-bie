h11-6-2c · Cyclic aur non-cyclic photophosphorylation mein kya difference hai?

140When only PS I is functional, the electron is circulated within the photosystem and the phosphorylation occurs due to cyclic flow of electrons (Figure 11.6).✓
  • Electron ki waapsi Aisa tab hota hai jab sirf PS I kaam kar raha ho.
  • Electron ki waapsi Electron PS I ke andar hi chakkar lagata rehta hai.
  • Electron ki waapsi Is gol bahav ke saath jo phosphorylation hoti hai, wahi cyclic photo-phosphorylation hai.
  • Lambi wavelength Sirf PS I chalta hai, aur cyclic photo-phosphorylation hoti hai.
  • Ek electron ka safar Roshni P 700 par padi, aur uska ek electron energy lekar uchhal gaya.
  • Dono raaston ka comparison Aisi roshni mein sirf PS I chalega, electron gol chakkar lagayega, aur sirf ATP banega.
  • Dono raaston ka comparison Gol raaste, yaani cyclic, mein sirf PS I kaam karta hai, aur electron wapas P 700 par aata hai.
140A possible location where this could be happening is in the stroma lamellae.✓
  • Sirf ATP Isliye stroma lamellae ko cyclic photo-phosphorylation ki ek sambhav jagah maana jaata hai.
140While the membrane or lamellae of the grana have both PS I and PS II the stroma lamellae membranes lack PS II as well as NADP reductase enzyme.✓
  • Membrane ka farq Grana ki membrane mein dono photosystem hote hain, PS I bhi aur PS II bhi.
  • Membrane ka farq Par stroma lamellae ki membrane mein PS II nahi hota.
  • Membrane ka farq Aur NADP reductase enzyme bhi nahi hota.
  • Membrane ka farq Toh socho, stroma lamellae mein uchhle electron ko NADP⁺ tak pahunchane wala enzyme hi nahi hai.
  • Ek electron ka safar Kadi ke aage use NADP⁺ tak le jaane wala NADP reductase mila hi nahi, kyunki yahan woh enzyme hai hi nahi.
140The excited electron does not pass on to NADP⁺ but is cycled back to the PS I complex through the electron transport chain (Figure 11.6).✓
  • Electron ki waapsi Jab NADP⁺ tak raasta na ho, toh electron carriers ki kadi se hokar wapas PS I par laut aata hai.
  • Membrane ka farq Toh socho, stroma lamellae mein uchhle electron ko NADP⁺ tak pahunchane wala enzyme hi nahi hai.
  • Sirf ATP Isliye electron ke paas wapas lautne ke siva koi raasta nahi.
  • Sirf ATP Par aisa nahi hai: gol raaste mein electron NADP⁺ tak jaata hi nahi, aur PS II ke bina paani bhi nahi tootta, isliye sirf ATP banta hai.
  • Ek electron ka safar e⁻ acceptor ne use pakda, aur woh carriers ki kadi mein aage badha.
  • Ek electron ka safar Kadi ke aage use NADP⁺ tak le jaane wala NADP reductase mila hi nahi, kyunki yahan woh enzyme hai hi nahi.
  • Ek electron ka safar Toh electron kadi se ghoom kar wapas P 700 par aa gaya, aur agli roshni ka intezaar karne laga.
  • Dono raaston ka comparison Gol raaste, yaani cyclic, mein sirf PS I kaam karta hai, aur electron wapas P 700 par aata hai.
140The cyclic flow hence, results only in the synthesis of ATP, but not of NADPH + H⁺.✓
  • Sirf ATP Nateeja pakka yaad rakho: gol raaste mein sirf ATP banta hai, NADPH + H⁺ nahi banta.
  • Sirf ATP Par aisa nahi hai: gol raaste mein electron NADP⁺ tak jaata hi nahi, aur PS II ke bina paani bhi nahi tootta, isliye sirf ATP banta hai.
  • Ek electron ka safar Is ek chakkar ke saath ATP bana.
  • Ek electron ka safar NADPH ek bhi nahi bana, aur O₂ bhi nahi nikli.
  • Dono raaston ka comparison Aapne socha hoga ki NADPH nahi banega, aur yahi sahi hai.
  • Dono raaston ka comparison Aisi roshni mein sirf PS I chalega, electron gol chakkar lagayega, aur sirf ATP banega.
  • Dono raaston ka comparison Sirf ATP banta hai; na NADPH, na O₂.
140Cyclic photophosphorylation also occurs when only light of wavelengths beyond 680 nm are available for excitation.✓
  • Lambi wavelength Jab sirf 680 nm se aage ki roshni mile, toh PS II theek se nahi jaagta.
  • Lambi wavelength Sirf PS I chalta hai, aur cyclic photo-phosphorylation hoti hai.
  • Lambi wavelength Aksar log ise ulta samajh lete hain, par 680 nm se aage ka matlab hai 680 se zyada, yaani lambi wavelength, kam wavelength nahi.
  • Dono raaston ka comparison Aapne socha hoga ki NADPH nahi banega, aur yahi sahi hai.

    Lamella ka arth batata hai.

    Enzyme ka substrate aur product recall.
  • pehle ki baat Reductase yaani reduction karaane wala.
    Reductase ka shabdik arth.
  • concept Toh socho, stroma lamellae mein uchhle electron ko NADP⁺ tak pahunchane wala enzyme hi nahi hai.
    Enzyme absence ka functional matlab.
  • concept Aksar log sochte hain ki light reaction mein hamesha ATP, NADPH aur O₂ teeno banenge.
    Common misconception uthata hai.
  • concept Par aisa nahi hai: gol raaste mein electron NADP⁺ tak jaata hi nahi, aur PS II ke bina paani bhi nahi tootta, isliye sirf ATP banta hai.
    PS II aur water splitting ka sambandh.
  • pehle ki baat Yaad karo, roshni lehron mein chalti hai, aur ek lehar ki chot se agli chot tak ki doori wavelength hai.
    Wavelength ka prerequisite.
  • pehle ki baat PS II ka centre P680 hai, jo lagbhag 680 nm ki roshni chahta hai.
    P680 excitation recall.
  • pehle ki baat PS I ka P700, 700 nm tak ki roshni bhi pakad leta hai.
    P700 excitation recall.
  • concept Jab sirf 680 nm se aage ki roshni mile, toh PS II theek se nahi jaagta.
    PS II ki kam excitation se condition samjhata hai.
  • concept Aksar log ise ulta samajh lete hain, par 680 nm se aage ka matlab hai 680 se zyada, yaani lambi wavelength, kam wavelength nahi.
    Longer versus shorter wavelength confusion saaf.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Paudhe ko yeh gol raasta kyon chahiye, iska ek bada kaaran hum aage Calvin cycle mein dekhenge.
    Calvin cycle ki demand ka aage ka sambandh.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Abhi bas itna jaan lo ki kabhi paudhe ko NADPH se zyada ATP chahiye hota hai.
    Relative ATP demand, fixed ratio nahi.
  • concept Ab ek electron ko pakad kar stroma lamellae mein uska poora safar chalate hain.
    Tracked electron ka worked example.
  • concept Roshni P 700 par padi, aur uska ek electron energy lekar uchhal gaya.
    P700 excitation ko track karta hai.
  • concept e⁻ acceptor ne use pakda, aur woh carriers ki kadi mein aage badha.
    Acceptor aur carrier hand-off ka example.
  • concept NADPH ek bhi nahi bana, aur O₂ bhi nahi nikli.
    Oxygen evolution bhi absent.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Ruko, pehle khud socho: agar kisi patti par sirf 700 nm wali roshni pade, toh usme NADPH banega ya nahi?
    700 nm condition ka checkpoint.
  • concept Seedhe raaste, yaani non-cyclic, mein electron paani se chalta hai: pehle PS II, phir PS I, aur aakhir NADP⁺.
    Non-cyclic source, order aur endpoint ka comparison.
  • concept ATP, NADPH aur O₂ teeno bante hain.
    Non-cyclic ke teen products.
  • concept Sirf ATP banta hai; na NADPH, na O₂.
    Cyclic mein oxygen bhi absent.

    Indirect energy coupling ki agli film ka sawaal.
