s3 · Membrane ka farq

  • NADP reductase (Blue native enzyme with exposed catalytic cleft at 78,25.) · kata 4.45% tasveer
#LineKya hilta hai
1Grana ki membrane mein dono photosystem hote hain, PS I bhi aur PS II bhi.—
2Par stroma lamellae ki membrane mein PS II nahi hota.—
3Aur NADP reductase enzyme bhi nahi hota.—
4NADP reductase woh enzyme hai jo NADP⁺ se NADPH banne ka kaam karata hai.NADP reductase kasta hai (dabaav)
5Reductase yaani reduction karaane wala.NADP reductase chamakta hai
6Toh socho, stroma lamellae mein uchhle electron ko NADP⁺ tak pahunchane wala enzyme hi nahi hai.—

s7 · Ek electron ka safar

  • e⁻ acceptor (Violet exposed acceptor at 38,26.) · kata 1.08% tasveer
  • carriers ki kadi (Copper redox docking region at 58,33.) · kata 2.25% tasveer
  • kadi (Copper compact soluble return carrier at 61,55.) · kata 1.28% tasveer
#LineKya hilta hai
1Ab ek electron ko pakad kar stroma lamellae mein uska poora safar chalate hain.—
2Roshni P 700 par padi, aur uska ek electron energy lekar uchhal gaya.—
3e⁻ acceptor ne use pakda, aur woh carriers ki kadi mein aage badha.e⁻ acceptor molecule ko pakad leta hai, savaar e-; carriers ki kadi molecule ko pakadkar us paar chhodta hai, savaar e-
4Kadi ke aage use NADP⁺ tak le jaane wala NADP reductase mila hi nahi, kyunki yahan woh enzyme hai hi nahi.kadi molecule ko pakad leta hai, savaar e-
5Toh electron kadi se ghoom kar wapas P 700 par aa gaya, aur agli roshni ka intezaar karne laga.kadi ghoomta hai (-20°); kadi molecule ko pakadkar us paar chhodta hai, savaar e-
6Is ek chakkar ke saath ATP bana.—
7NADPH ek bhi nahi bana, aur O₂ bhi nahi nikli.—
