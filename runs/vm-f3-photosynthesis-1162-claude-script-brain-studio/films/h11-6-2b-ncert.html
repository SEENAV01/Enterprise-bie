140When the two photosystems work in a series, first PS II and then the PS I, a process called non-cyclic photo-phosphorylation occurs.✓
  • Z scheme ki kadi Usme do photosystem the: pehle PS II, phir PS I. Dono par roshni padti thi.

  • Paani se NADPH tak PS II aur PS I se hokar, aakhir mein NADP⁺ ke haath mein pahunchta hai.
  • Dono raaston ko pehchaaniye Isliye ise non-cyclic photo-phosphorylation kehte hain.
  • Dono raaston ko pehchaaniye Yaad rakho: non-cyclic mein dono photosystem ek ke baad ek kaam karte hain, pehle PS II, phir PS I. Aur ATP ke saath NADPH bhi banta hai.
