h11-6-2a · Agar PS I ke aage NADP⁺ tak jaane ka raasta hi band ho, toh roshni se uchhla electron kahan jaayega, aur kya tab bhi kuch banega?

4 / 4 NCERT lines film mein padhayi gayi · NCERT page 139, 140

Film ka dhaancha: sawaal: 3 · pehle ki baat: 19 · concept: 12 · kahan kaam aata hai / asar: 4 · yaad rakhiye: 1

139Living organisms have the capability of extracting energy from oxidisable substances and store this in the form of bond energy.✓
  • Energy ki zaroorat Yeh energy jeev kuch cheezon se nikaalte hain, jaise khaane ka glucose.
  • Oxidation aur reduction Jo cheezein oxidation mein electron de sakti hain, unhe oxidisable substances kehte hain.
  • Oxidation aur reduction Glucose aisi hi ek cheez hai.
  • Oxidation aur reduction Abhi bas itna jaan lo ki aisi cheezon se energy nikalti hai.
  • Energy ka molecular safar Use molecules ke andar band karke rakh lete hain.
  • Chemical bonds aur ATP In bonds mein bhi aise hi energy band rehti hai; ise bond energy kehte hain.
139Special substances like ATP, carry this energy in their chemical bonds.✓
  • Chemical bonds aur ATP ATP ek aisa hi khaas molecule hai.
  • Chemical bonds aur ATP Yeh apne chemical bonds mein energy lekar chalta hai, aur cell ke har kaam tak pahunchata hai.
139The process through which ATP is synthesised by cells (in mitochondria and chloroplasts) is named phosphorylation.✓
  • Phosphorylation aur uski jagah ADP mein phosphate jodkar ATP banane ko phosphorylation kehte hain.
  • Phosphorylation aur uski jagah Phosphate jodna, isliye naam phosphorylation.
  • Phosphorylation aur uski jagah Cell yeh kaam do jagah karta hai: mitochondria mein aur chloroplast mein.
140Photo-phosphorylation is the synthesis of ATP from ADP and inorganic phosphate in the presence of light.✓
  • Phosphorylation aur uski jagah Jo phosphate akela hai, kisi molecule se juda nahi, use inorganic phosphate kehte hain.
  • Roshni se charging Chloroplast mein yeh charging roshni se hoti hai, bilkul waise jaise solar charger dhoop se phone charge karta hai.
  • Roshni se charging Roshni ki madad se ADP aur inorganic phosphate se ATP banane ko photo-phosphorylation kehte hain, kyunki photo ka matlab hi roshni hai.
