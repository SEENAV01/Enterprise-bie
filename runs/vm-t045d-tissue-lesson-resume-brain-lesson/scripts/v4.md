### Part c09.2 · cause_effect
_frame: process: Support limits unwanted organ displacement while fibres determine mechanical behaviour. · how: Attachments distribute forces; collagen resists pulling and elastic fibres recover after stretching. · when: During body movement and when deforming forces are applied and removed. · conditions: Normal movement remains possible; support does not mean absolute immobility. · atmosphere: A moving body with supported internal organs and a pull-and-release demonstration._
