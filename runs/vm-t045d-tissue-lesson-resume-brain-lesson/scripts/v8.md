### Part c22.3 · cause_effect
_frame: process: Explain support that allows plant parts to bend. · how: Uneven primary-wall reinforcement supplies strength without strongly lignified rigidity. · when: During bending of stalks, tendrils and climber stems. · conditions: Photosynthesis requires chloroplasts; tissue support does not itself cause tendril coiling. · atmosphere: A climber following a support and a stalk bending in wind._

**b26 · explain · step 1: the effect students can see** — झुकता डंठल टूटता क्यों नहीं? Collenchyma mechanical strength और flexibility देता है, यानी सहारा और बिना टूटे मुड़ना; book photosynthesis भी बताती है।
