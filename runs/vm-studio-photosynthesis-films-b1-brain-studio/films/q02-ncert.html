  • pehle ki baat Photosynthesis se bana kuch khana starch ban kar jama hota hai, aur iodine us starch ko blue-black dikhata hai.
    Starch ko stored food aur iodine ko uska indicator samjhaya.
  • pehle ki baat Controlled experiments mein ek baar mein ek zaroorat badalte hain, jaise recipe mein ek ingredient hata kar uska kaam jaanchte hain.
    Controlled comparison ka logic aur recipe ki misaal di.
  • pehle ki baat Pehle plant ko andhere mein destarch karte hain; test se pehle patte ka rang hot-water bath mein alcohol se hataate hain.
    Purana starch hatane aur iodine ka rang saaf dekhne ki preparation batayi; alcohol ko water bath mein garam kiya.
  • concept Aisa isliye hai kyunki black paper light rokta hai, jaise band parda dhoop rokta hai; iodine sirf illuminated hisse ko blue-black dikhata hai.
    Paper ki light-blocking action ko parde se joda.
  • pehle ki baat Ab tube ki boundary paas se dekhte hain; seal bahar ki air ko andar aane se rokta hai.
    Airtight seal ka experimental control samjhaya.
  • concept Is conclusion ki wajah samajhiye: dono taraf chlorophyll aur light hain, par air wala hissa CO2 paata rehta hai.
    Same leaf ke do hisson ko internal comparison ki tarah samjhaya.
  • concept Andar KOH CO2 hata deta hai, jaise recipe ka ek ingredient gayab ho; ab isi patte ka starch test karte hain.
    Missing ingredient ki misaal se necessity ka logic joda.
