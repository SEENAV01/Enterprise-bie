Kitab se bahar ke shabd (sirf jaankari, film inse rukti nahi): 2H2A, H2A

135A milestone contribution to the understanding of photosynthesis was that made by a microbiologist, Cornelius van Niel (1897-1985), who, based on his studies of purple and green bacteria, demonstrated that photosynthesis is essentially a light-dependent reaction in which hydrogen from a suitable oxidisable compound reduces carbon dioxide to carbohydrates.✓
  • Hydrogen donor Jaise samaan dene wala donor hota hai, suitable oxidisable compound hydrogen deta hai aur khud oxidise hota hai.
  • Hydrogen donor Light-dependent photosynthesis mein isi hydrogen ki madad se CO2 reduce hokar carbohydrate banta hai; ab chloroplast ke andar chaliye.
  • Food ke liye reducing power Dekhiye, paani se mile electrons light-powered machinery mein aage badhte hain, jisse CO2 ko reduce karne ki power milti hai.
  • Van Niel ka comparison Microbiologist Cornelius van Niel, 1897 se 1985, purple aur green bacteria ke studies se light-dependent reduction samjhate hain.
135This can be expressed by: Light 2H A+CO →2A+CH O+H O 2 2 2 2 In green plants H O is the hydrogen donor and is oxidised to O.✓
  • Paani ka oxidation Green plants mein H2O hydrogen donor hai, aur uske oxidation se O2 nikalti hai.
  • Labelled water ka safar Dekhiye, do labelled H2O se labelled O2 banti hai, saath mein chaar H+ aur chaar electrons nikalte hain.
  • Van Niel ka comparison Unka general hisaab hai: light mein 2H2A plus CO2 se 2A, CH2O aur H2O; H2A suitable hydrogen donor hai.
135Some organisms do not release O during photosynthesis.✓
  • Van Niel ka comparison Nahi, purple aur green sulphur bacteria H2S donor se, organism ke anusaar, sulphur ya sulphate banate hain, O2 nahi.
135When H S, instead is the hydrogen donor for purple and green sulphur bacteria, the ‘oxidation’ product is sulphur or sulphate depending on the organism and not O.✓
  • Van Niel ka comparison Nahi, purple aur green sulphur bacteria H2S donor se, organism ke anusaar, sulphur ya sulphate banate hain, O2 nahi.
135Hence, he inferred that the O evolved by the green plant comes from H O2, not from carbon dioxide.✓
  • Paani ka oxidation Green plants mein H2O hydrogen donor hai, aur uske oxidation se O2 nikalti hai.
  • CO2 par tag Iska seedha sa jawab hai nahi; do normal H2O se normal O2, chaar H+ aur chaar electrons nikalte hain.
  • Van Niel ka comparison Isi comparison se unka inference hai ki plant ki O2 water se aati hai, CO2 se nahi; ab plant ka hisaab paas se dekhiye.
135This was later proved by using radioisotopic techniques.✓
  • Labelled water ka safar Dekhiye, do labelled H2O se labelled O2 banti hai, saath mein chaar H+ aur chaar electrons nikalte hain.
  • Labelled water ka safar Yeh isotope tracing hai, jaise parcel ka tag destination batata hai; oxygen-18 stable isotope hai, radioactive nahi.
  • CO2 par tag CO2 ka tag is O2 route par nahi aata; radioisotopic techniques wala proof yahan isotope tracing hai, jisme stable oxygen-18 kaam aata hai.

135Note that this is not a single reaction but description of a multistep process called photosynthesis.✓
  • Food ke liye reducing power Kai steps ke baad carbohydrate milta hai, jo growth ke liye kaam aata hai; ab donor badalne ka comparison karte hain.
  • Water ka gross aur net hisaab Is water-hisaab wheel mein consumption aur regeneration dekhiye; yeh kai steps ka hisaab hai, single reaction nahi.
