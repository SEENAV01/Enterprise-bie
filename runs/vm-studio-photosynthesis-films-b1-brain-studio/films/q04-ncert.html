135It was not until about 1854 that Julius von Sachs provided evidence for production of glucose when plants grow.✓
  • Rang ka jawaab bacteria denge Lagbhag 1854 mein Julius von Sachs badhte paudhon mein glucose production ka saboot dete hain; ab isi patte ke andar chalte hain.
135Glucose is usually stored as starch.✓
  • Chloroplast ke andar Dekhiye, Sachs yeh sambandh dikhate hain ki green parts mein glucose banta hai aur aam taur par starch ke roop mein store hota hai; ab storage ko paas se dekhte hain.
135His later studies showed that the green substance in plants (chlorophyll as we know it now) is located in special bodies (later called chloroplasts) within plant cells.✓
  • Hara rang kahan rehta hai? Dekhiye, Sachs ki baad ki studies mein green substance, yani chlorophyll, cell ke khaas bodies mein milta hai, jinhe baad mein chloroplasts kaha jaata hai.
135He found that the green parts in plants is where glucose is made, and that the glucose is usually stored as starch.✓
  • Chloroplast ke andar Dekhiye, Sachs yeh sambandh dikhate hain ki green parts mein glucose banta hai aur aam taur par starch ke roop mein store hota hai; ab storage ko paas se dekhte hain.
135Now consider the interesting experiments done by T.W Engelmann (1843 – 1909).✓
  • Sugar se reserve Ab rang ka asar jaanne ke liye T.W. Engelmann, jinka jeevankaal 1843 se 1909 hai, ke experiment par chalte hain.
135Using a prism he split light into its spectral components and then illuminated a green alga, Cladophora, placed in a suspension of aerobic bacteria.✓
  • Prism aur Cladophora Yahan paani mein green alga Cladophora, aerobic bacteria ke suspension mein rakha hai; ab alag rang is tak kaise pahunchenge?
  • Prism aur Cladophora Isme kaam aata hai prism, jo light ko spectral components mein baantta hai, jisse Cladophora ke alag hisse blue, green aur red light mein nahaate hain.
135The bacteria were used to detect the sites of O2 evolution.✓
  • Prism aur Cladophora Aerobic bacteria ko respiration ke liye O2 chahiye, isliye yeh O2 evolution ki jagah pakadne wale jeete-jaagte detectors bante hain; aaiye paas se dekhein.
135He observed that the bacteria accumulated mainly in the region of blue and red light of the split spectrum.✓
  • Bheed ka matlab Dekhiye, blue aur red regions se zyada O2 nikalti hai, jiske kaaran aerobic bacteria mainly inhi regions mein jama hote hain.
135A first action spectrum of photosynthesis was thus described.✓
  • Bheed ka matlab Isse photosynthesis ka pehla action spectrum milta hai, jo wavelength ke saath activity ka badlaav batata hai; ab alga cell mein chalte hain.
135It resembles roughly the absorption spectra of chlorophyll a and b (discussed in section 11.4).✓
  • Activity aur absorption Haan, pehla action spectrum chlorophyll a aur b ke absorption spectra se roughly milta hai, bilkul identical nahi hota.
135By the middle of the nineteenth century the key features of plant photosynthesis were known, namely, that plants could use light energy to make carbohydrates from CO2 and water.✓
  • Activity aur absorption Unneesvin sadi ke madhya tak yeh saaf hai ki paudhe light energy se CO2 aur water lekar carbohydrates banaate hain; ab iska kul hisaab paas se dekhte hain.
135The empirical equation representing the total process of photosynthesis for oxygen evolving organisms was then understood as: Light CO +H O →[CH O]+O 2 2 2 2 where [CH O2] represented a carbohydrate (e.g., glucose, a six-carbon sugar).✓
  • Total process ka hisaab Dekhiye, early empirical equation hai: CO2 plus H2O, light mein, [CH2O] plus O2; jaise factory ka kul aana-jaana, yeh poore process ka hisaab hai.
  • Total process ka hisaab [CH2O] carbohydrate ki general unit hai; glucose six-carbon sugar hai, jiska formula C6H12O6 hai, yani CH2O ka chhah guna.
