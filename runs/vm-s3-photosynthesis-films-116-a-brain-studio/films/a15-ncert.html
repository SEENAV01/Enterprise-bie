140(a) Since splitting of the water molecule takes place on the inner side of the membrane, the protons or hydrogen ions that are produced by the splitting of water accumulate within the lumen of the thylakoids.✓

141(b) As electrons move through the photosystems, protons are transported across the membrane.✓
  • H carrier ki unloading Saath hi H+ lumen mein release hote hain; isliye photosystems ke electron transport ke saath protons bhi across membrane pahunchte hain.
141This happens because the primary accepter of electron which is located towards the outer side of the membrane transfers its electron not to an electron carrier but to an H carrier.✓
  • Doosra source: H carrier ki loading Yahan primary acceptor stroma wali outer side par hai; electron aage badhe toh H+ membrane kaise paar karega?
  • Doosra source: H carrier ki loading Isme H carrier kaam aata hai: primary acceptor electron ko sirf electron carrier nahi, plastoquinone, yani PQ, ko deta hai.
141Hence, this molecule removes a proton from the stroma while transporting an electron.✓
  • Doosra source: H carrier ki loading PQ do electrons ke saath stroma se do H+ leta hai aur PQH2 banta hai, jisse stroma ke protons ghatte hain.
141When this molecule passes on its electron to the electron carrier on the inner side of the membrane, the proton is released into the inner side or the lumen side of the membrane.✓
  • H carrier ki unloading Dekhiye, PQH2 membrane mein cytochrome b6f tak pahunchta hai, jahan inner-side electron carrier ko electrons milte hain.
  • H carrier ki unloading Saath hi H+ lumen mein release hote hain; isliye photosystems ke electron transport ke saath protons bhi across membrane pahunchte hain.

141Hence, within the chloroplast, protons in the stroma decrease in number, while in the lumen there is accumulation of protons.✓
  • FNR par proton accounting Isliye lumen mein H+ addition aur stroma se H+ removal, dono milkar across-membrane farq badhate hain.
  • Stored gradient aur return route Ab membrane ke is patch mein lumen ka H+ count zyada aur stroma ka kam hai; pH par kya asar padega?
141This creates a proton gradient across the thylakoid membrane as well as a measurable decrease in pH in the lumen.✓
  • Stored gradient aur return route Iska seedha sa jawab hai: H+ badhne se lumen ka pH measurably girta hai, aur thylakoid membrane ke across proton gradient banta hai.

NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • pehle ki baat Pehle samajhiye: membrane ke across H+ concentration ka farq proton gradient hai; zyada H+ ka matlab kam pH hai.
    Gradient concentration ka farq hai; H+ aur pH ka inverse relation kholta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Golden electron track aur red proton track ko alag trace karte hain; ab isi thylakoid membrane ke andar chalte hain.
    Electron aur proton routes independently trace karna sikhata hai.
  • pehle ki baat Yeh farq baandh ke do taraf unequal water levels jaisi stored energy hai, jise controlled return kaam mein la sakta hai.
    Baandh analogy gradient ki stored energy aur controlled return samjhati hai.
  • concept Dekhiye, inner side par do H2O split hote hain, jisse chaar H+, ek O2 aur chaar electrons nikalte hain.
    Pehle padhi water-splitting stoichiometry ka ek-sentence recall.
  • concept Isme H carrier kaam aata hai: primary acceptor electron ko sirf electron carrier nahi, plastoquinone, yani PQ, ko deta hai.
    H carrier ko figure ke plastoquinone naam se jodta hai.
  • concept PQ do electrons ke saath stroma se do H+ leta hai aur PQH2 banta hai, jisse stroma ke protons ghatte hain.
    PQ/PQH2 ke do-electron, do-proton loading ko kholta hai.
  • concept Dekhiye, PQH2 membrane mein cytochrome b6f tak pahunchta hai, jahan inner-side electron carrier ko electrons milte hain.
    Inner-side transfer ko b6f ki location se jodta hai.
  • concept Ab right side par hain; electron b6f se PC, yani plastocyanin, ke through PS I tak jaata hai.
    Figure ke b6f-PC-PS I labels aur electron route kholta hai.
  • concept PS I ke acceptor se electrons Fd, yani ferredoxin, tak aate hain; par NADP+ ko reduce karne ke liye aur kya chahiye?
    Fd ko ferredoxin naam aur location se pehchaan deta hai.
  • concept Iska jawab hai H+; FNR, yani NADP reductase, stroma side par hai aur Fd ke electrons leta hai.
    FNR label ko NADP-reducing enzyme se jodta hai.
  • pehle ki baat Ab isi enzyme ke paas jaakar dekhte hain ki reduction, yani electrons milne, mein stromal protons kaise lagte hain.
    Reduction ka electron-gain arth kholta hai.
  • concept Nahi, NADP+ aur do electrons ke saath stromal H+ bhi lagta hai, jisse NADPH banta hai.
    Net FNR reaction se stromal H+ consumption saaf dikhata hai.
  • concept NADPH + H+ wali notation mein bhi net baat yahi hai ki reduction ke liye protons stroma se liye jaate hain.
    NADPH + H+ notation ko net stromal proton consumption se jodta hai.

    Chemiosmosis aur controlled return route identify karta hai; ATP coupling ki detail agle film mein hai.
