a12 · PS II se NADPH tak Z scheme kaise banta hai?

10 / 10 NCERT lines film mein padhayi gayi · NCERT page 138, 139

Film ka dhaancha: sawaal: 1 · pehle ki baat: 2 · concept: 16 · kahan kaam aata hai / asar: 2 · yaad rakhiye: 1

138In photosystem II the reaction centre chlorophyll a absorbs 680 nm wavelength of red light causing electrons to become excited and jump into an orbit farther from the atomic nucleus.✓
  • P680 ka pehla boost Dekhiye, chlorophyll a 680 nm light absorb karta hai, jisse electron excited hokar nucleus se door waale orbit mein pahunchta hai.
138These electrons are picked up by an electron acceptor which passes them to an electrons transport system consisting of cytochromes (Figure 11.5).✓
  • P680 ka pehla boost Ab electron acceptor, yaani electron lene waala, is excited electron ko pakad leta hai.
  • P680 ka pehla boost Yeh acceptor electron ko cytochromes waale electron transport system mein deta hai; ab isi raaste ke andar chalte hain.
139This movement of electrons is downhill, in terms of an oxidation-reduction or redox potential scale.✓
  • Downhill, lekin electron bachta hai Nahi, oxidation-reduction, ya redox potential scale par transfer downhill hota hai, lekin electron transport chain mein electron used up nahi hota.
139The electrons are not used up as they pass through the electron transport chain, but are passed on to the pigments of photosystem PS I.✓
  • Downhill, lekin electron bachta hai Nahi, oxidation-reduction, ya redox potential scale par transfer downhill hota hai, lekin electron transport chain mein electron used up nahi hota.
  • Downhill, lekin electron bachta hai Wahi electron aage PS I ke pigments ko milta hai, jisse reaction centre ke nikle electron ki jagah bharti hai.
139Simultaneously, electrons in the reaction centre of PS I are also excited when they receive red light of wavelength 700 nm and are transferred to another accepter molecule that has a greater redox potential.✓
  • P700 ki doosri lift Iska jawab hai 700 nm ki red light, jo PS I ke reaction-centre electron ko excite karti hai; dono photosystems mein excitation saath bhi hota hai.
  • P700 ki doosri lift Ab excited electron doosre acceptor ko milta hai, jiska redox potential greater hai.
139These electrons then are moved downhill again, this time to a molecule of energy-rich NADP⁺.✓
  • P700 ki doosri lift Phir electron downhill route par energy-rich NADP plus tak jaata hai; ab isi terminal route par reduction paas se dekhte hain.
139The addition of these electrons reduces NADP⁺ to NADPH + H⁺.✓
  • NADP plus ka reduction Dekhiye, do electrons aur do H plus ke saath NADP plus reduce hota hai, jisse NADPH aur H plus milte hain.
139This whole scheme of transfer of electrons, starting from the PS II, uphill to the acceptor, down the electron transport chain to PS I, excitation of electrons, transfer to another acceptor, and finally down hill to NADP⁺ reducing it to NADPH + H⁺ is called the Z scheme, due to its characterstic shape (Figure 11.5).✓
  • NADP plus ka reduction Do lifts ki tarah, PS II se acceptor tak uphill, chain se PS I tak downhill, phir excitation, doosra acceptor aur NADP plus tak downhill milkar Z scheme banaate hain.
139Figure 11.5 Z scheme of light reaction✓
  • Do flashes ka sawaal Yeh Z scheme of light reaction hai: carriers, yaani electron aage dene waale, sequence mein redox potential scale par rakhe jaate hain.
139This shape is formed when all the carriers are placed in a sequence on a redox potential scale.✓
  • Do flashes ka sawaal Yeh Z scheme of light reaction hai: carriers, yaani electron aage dene waale, sequence mein redox potential scale par rakhe jaate hain.

NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • pehle ki baat Pehle yaad karein: electron gain reduction hai; redox potential ki scale par height membrane ki asli oonchai nahi hai.
    Reduction ka prerequisite aur diagram ki height ko geography se alag karta hai.
  • concept Yeh Z scheme of light reaction hai: carriers, yaani electron aage dene waale, sequence mein redox potential scale par rakhe jaate hain.
    Carrier ka arth kholta hai.
  • pehle ki baat Baayein Photosystem II aur daayein Photosystem I ke LHC, yaani light harvesting complex, Light lete hain; ab baayein reaction centre ke paas chalte hain.
    LHC ka full form aur location recall.
  • concept Ab cytochromes ke paas hain, jo electron transfer karne waale proteins hain; kya electron yahan kharch ho jaata hai?
    Cytochromes ko electron-transfer proteins ke roop mein kholta hai.
  • concept Wahi electron aage PS I ke pigments ko milta hai, jisse reaction centre ke nikle electron ki jagah bharti hai.
    PS I ko replacement electron milne ka sambandh.
  • concept Isse do lifts ke beech downhill raasta samajhiye: pehla boost milta hai, phir energy girti hai; ab PS I ki doosri lift paas se dekhte hain.
    Do lifts ki analogy se doosre boost ki zaroorat taiyar karta hai.
  • concept Stroma mein NADP plus electron lene waala final acceptor hai; electron milne par iska kya banta hai?
    Final acceptor ka arth aur stromal context.
  • concept Dekhiye, do electrons aur do H plus ke saath NADP plus reduce hota hai, jisse NADPH aur H plus milte hain.
    Balanced terminal reduction mein do electrons aur do protons ka hisaab deta hai.
  • concept Do lifts ki tarah, PS II se acceptor tak uphill, chain se PS I tak downhill, phir excitation, doosra acceptor aur NADP plus tak downhill milkar Z scheme banaate hain.
    Do lifts analogy ko poore route se jodta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Yahan terminal site se NADPH stroma mein nikalta hai; adhoore Z-scheme diagram mein acceptor missing ho, toh electron ka arrow kahaan pahunchega?
    Product departure ko missing-acceptor waale exam question se jodta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Iski jaanch dono excitations ke baad acceptor aur aage ke arrows se hoti hai; ATP marker aur water-source branch ko agle mechanisms se jodenge.
    Incomplete diagram ki jaanch aur agle mechanisms ka ek-line bridge.
