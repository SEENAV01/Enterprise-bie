a16 · CF₀ aur CF₁ proton gradient ko ATP se kaise couple karte hain?

10 / 10 NCERT lines film mein padhayi gayi · NCERT page 141, 142

Film ka dhaancha: sawaal: 1 · pehle ki baat: 1 · concept: 17 · kahan kaam aata hai / asar: 10 · yaad rakhiye: 1

141Why are we so interested in the proton gradient?✓
  • Gradient ki energy Ab sawaal banta hai ki proton gradient mein itni dilchaspi kyun hai?
141This gradient is important because it is the breakdown of this gradient that leads to the synthesis of ATP.✓
  • Gradient ki energy Iska jawab hai ki H⁺ ki waapsi se gradient ka breakdown ATP synthesis ki energy deta hai.
141The gradient is broken down due to the movement of protons across the membrane to the stroma through the transmembrane channel of the CF₀ of the ATP synthase.✓
  • Specific passage CF₀ se H⁺ facilitated diffusion karke lumen se stroma mein aate hain, jisse gradient ka breakdown hota hai.
142The ATP synthase enzyme consists of two parts: one called the CF₀ is embedded in the thylakoid membrane and forms a transmembrane channel that carries out facilitated diffusion of protons across the membrane.✓
  • Specific passage Yahan ATP synthase ke do parts hain; par H⁺ membrane kis raaste paar karte hain?
  • Specific passage Isme CF₀ kaam aata hai, jo membrane mein dhansa transmembrane channel hai, yaani H⁺ ka membrane ke aar-paar passage.
  • Specific passage CF₀ se H⁺ facilitated diffusion karke lumen se stroma mein aate hain, jisse gradient ka breakdown hota hai.
142The other portion is called CF₁ and protrudes on the outer surface of the thylakoid membrane on the side that faces the stroma.✓
  • Flow se shape change Ab stroma-facing CF₁ par hain, jo thylakoid membrane ki outer surface se bahar nikla hai.
142The break down of the gradient provides enough energy to cause a conformational change in the CF₁ particle of the ATP synthase, which makes the enzyme synthesise several molecules of energy-packed ATP.✓
  • Flow se shape change Ghoomta stalk CF₁ ke catalytic parts ki shape badalta hai; is conformational change ki energy gradient ke breakdown se aati hai.
  • ATP ka banna Dekhiye, stroma ke ADP aur Pi yahin milte hain, jisse CF₁ ATP banata hai aur stroma mein chhodta hai.
  • ATP ka banna ADP aur Pi se yeh action baar-baar several molecules of energy-packed ATP banata hai.
142Chemiosmosis requires a membrane, a proton pump, a proton gradient and ATP synthase.✓
  • Leak ka test Isliye chemiosmosis ko membrane, proton pump, proton gradient aur ATP synthase chaaron chahiye.
142Energy is used to pump protons across a membrane, to create a gradient or a high concentration of protons within the thylakoid lumen.✓
  • Gradient ki energy Proton pump energy se H⁺ thylakoid lumen mein jama karta hai, jisse andar concentration badhti hai; ab channel paas se dekhiye.
142ATP synthase has a channel that allows diffusion of protons back across the membrane; this releases enough energy to activate ATP synthase enzyme that catalyses the formation of ATP.✓
  • ATP ka banna Channel se H⁺ ki return diffusion energy release karke ATP synthase activate karti hai, jisse ADP aur Pi se ATP banta hai.
142Along with the NADPH produced by the movement of electrons, the ATP will be used immediately in the biosynthetic reaction taking place in the stroma, responsible for fixing CO₂, and synthesis of sugars.✓
  • Stroma tak delivery Yaad kijiye, electrons ki movement se NADPH banta hai, jo stroma ki biosynthetic reactions tak pahunchta hai.
  • Stroma tak delivery ATP bhi turant inhi biosynthetic reactions mein use hone pahunchta hai; ab inka carbon wala kaam paas se dekhte hain.
  • Carbon gain Iska jawab hai ki inki madad se fixing CO₂ hoti hai, yaani CO₂ ka inorganic carbon organic compounds mein judta hai.
  • Carbon gain ATP aur NADPH in biosynthetic reactions ko support karte hain, jinke kai steps ke baad sugars banti hain.

NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • sawaal Membrane mein H⁺ ka uncontrolled hole kar dein toh useful ATP coupling kyun khatam hogi?
    Uncontrolled proton leak ka thought experiment.
  • pehle ki baat Aaiye jaante hain. Facilitated diffusion high se low concentration ki taraf hoti hai, jabki ATP synthesis energy maangti hai.
    Diffusion ki direction aur ATP ki energy requirement ka prerequisite.
  • concept Isme CF₀ kaam aata hai, jo membrane mein dhansa transmembrane channel hai, yaani H⁺ ka membrane ke aar-paar passage.
    Transmembrane ka arth khola.
  • concept Par breakdown concentration ke farq ka hai; na H⁺ nasht hote hain, na membrane tootati hai.
    Gradient breakdown ko destruction se alag kiya.
  • concept Yeh specific passage turnstile jaisa hai; ab iske upar judi machinery ke andar chalte hain.
    Turnstile ki specific entry se analogy shuru ki.
  • concept Ab sawaal banta hai ki H⁺ ka flow CF₁ tak energy kaise pahunchata hai?
    Mechanical coupling ka sawaal.
  • concept Dekhiye, CF₀ se H⁺ ka flow central stalk ghumata hai, jaise turnstile se guzarna uske ghoomne se juda hai.
    Rotary coupling ko turnstile analogy se joda.
  • concept Ghoomta stalk CF₁ ke catalytic parts ki shape badalta hai; is conformational change ki energy gradient ke breakdown se aati hai.
    Catalytic contour ki badalti position aur stalk rotation dikhayi.
  • concept Dekhiye, stroma ke ADP aur Pi yahin milte hain, jisse CF₁ ATP banata hai aur stroma mein chhodta hai.
    Catalytic site par substrates ki meeting aur ATP release.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Ab membrane par lautkar test karte hain: H⁺ is khule hole se niklein toh kya hoga?
    Membrane leak ka test.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar H⁺ hole se stroma mein leak hote hain, jisse gradient ghatta hai par flow CF₀ ko bypass karta hai.
    Leak gradient dissipate karta hai bina synthase drive kiye.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Turnstile bypass karne se uska mechanism nahi chalta; waise hi leak ka H⁺ flow CF₁ ko ATP banane ke liye drive nahi karta.
    Turnstile analogy se uncoupling samjhayi.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Pump H⁺ jama kare bhi, leak unhe nikaalta hai; isi liye intact membrane useful coupling bachati hai.
    Membrane integrity ki functional necessity.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Iska jawab hai ki inki madad se fixing CO₂ hoti hai, yaani CO₂ ka inorganic carbon organic compounds mein judta hai.
    Fixing CO2 ka arth khola, detailed cycle nahi padhaya.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar ATP aur NADPH in biosynthetic reactions ko support karte hain, jinke kai steps ke baad sugars banti hain.
    Energy carriers aur multistep sugar output ka basic destination dikhaya.
