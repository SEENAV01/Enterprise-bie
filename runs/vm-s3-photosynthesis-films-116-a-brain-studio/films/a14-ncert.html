a14 · Cyclic aur non-cyclic photophosphorylation mein actual difference kya hai?

13 / 13 NCERT lines film mein padhayi gayi · NCERT page 139, 140

Film ka dhaancha: sawaal: 1 · pehle ki baat: 3 · concept: 14 · kahan kaam aata hai / asar: 2 · yaad rakhiye: 1

139Living organisms have the capability of extracting energy from oxidisable substances and store this in the form of bond energy.✓
  • Asli chloroplast ke andar Ab chloroplast ke andar hain; living organisms oxidisable substances, yani oxidise ho sakne wale padarthon se energy nikaalkar bond energy mein store karte hain.
139Special substances like ATP, carry this energy in their chemical bonds.✓
  • Asli chloroplast ke andar ATP yeh energy chemical bonds mein carry karta hai; par usable energy poori coupled reaction se milti hai, sirf bond tootne se nahi.
139The process through which ATP is synthesised by cells (in mitochondria and chloroplasts) is named phosphorylation.✓
  • Asli chloroplast ke andar Cells ke mitochondria aur chloroplasts mein ATP synthesis ko phosphorylation kehte hain.
140Photo-phosphorylation is the synthesis of ATP from ADP and inorganic phosphate in the presence of light.✓
  • Return flow ka ATP output Isme ADP aur inorganic phosphate, yani Pi, milkar ATP banate hain; light ki presence mein is synthesis ko photo-phosphorylation kehte hain.
140When the two photosystems work in a series, first PS II and then the PS I, a process called non-cyclic photo-phosphorylation occurs.✓
  • Onward delivery Iska jawab hai: pehle PSII, phir PSI series mein chalte hain, jinhe electron transport chain jodti hai; Z scheme ka yeh route non-cyclic photophosphorylation hai.
140The two photosystems are connected through an electron transport chain, as seen earlier – in the Z scheme.✓
  • Onward delivery Iska jawab hai: pehle PSII, phir PSI series mein chalte hain, jinhe electron transport chain jodti hai; Z scheme ka yeh route non-cyclic photophosphorylation hai.
140Both ATP and NADPH + H⁺ are synthesised by this kind of electron flow (Figure 11.5).✓
  • Onward delivery NADP reductase par electrons aur H+ ki madad se NADP+ reduce hota hai, jisse NADPH + H+ milta hai; is flow se ATP bhi milta hai.
140When only PS I is functional, the electron is circulated within the photosystem and the phosphorylation occurs due to cyclic flow of electrons (Figure 11.6).✓
  • PSI ka closed return Dekhiye, light P700 ko excite karti hai, aur electron acceptor se electron transport chain hote hue PSI mein wapas aata hai; isi cyclic flow se phosphorylation hota hai.
140A possible location where this could be happening is in the stroma lamellae.✓
  • Asli chloroplast ke andar Stroma lamellae cyclic flow ki possible jagah hain; inmein PSII aur NADP reductase enzyme nahi hote, jabki grana membranes mein PSI aur PSII dono hote hain.
140While the membrane or lamellae of the grana have both PS I and PS II the stroma lamellae membranes lack PS II as well as NADP reductase enzyme.✓
  • Asli chloroplast ke andar Stroma lamellae cyclic flow ki possible jagah hain; inmein PSII aur NADP reductase enzyme nahi hote, jabki grana membranes mein PSI aur PSII dono hote hain.
140The excited electron does not pass on to NADP⁺ but is cycled back to the PS I complex through the electron transport chain (Figure 11.6).✓
  • PSI ka closed return Dekhiye, light P700 ko excite karti hai, aur electron acceptor se electron transport chain hote hue PSI mein wapas aata hai; isi cyclic flow se phosphorylation hota hai.
140The cyclic flow hence, results only in the synthesis of ATP, but not of NADPH + H⁺.✓
  • PSI ka closed return Circular bus ki tarah electron laut raha hai, NADP+ tak net delivery nahi hoti, jiske kaaran NADPH + H+ nahi banta.
  • Return flow ka ATP output Is cyclic route ka output sirf ATP hai, NADPH + H+ nahi; ismein PSII ki water splitting nahi chalti, jisse O2 bhi nahi nikalta.
140Cyclic photophosphorylation also occurs when only light of wavelengths beyond 680 nm are available for excitation.✓
  • PSI ka closed return Jab excitation ke liye sirf 680 nm se aage ki wavelength ki light milti hai, tab bhi cyclic photophosphorylation hota hai.

NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • pehle ki baat Aaiye jaante hain: Light se Chlorophyll P700 ka electron e- acceptor tak uthta hai, phir electron transport system se lautne ka raasta milta hai.
    Return route ka visual orientation.
  • pehle ki baat Circular bus route mein bus lautti hai; waise hi electron laut sakta hai, jabki ADP aur Pi se ATP banane ke liye energy transfer hota hai.
    Circular bus analogy electron return ko energy transfer se alag karti hai.
  • pehle ki baat ATP yeh energy chemical bonds mein carry karta hai; par usable energy poori coupled reaction se milti hai, sirf bond tootne se nahi.
    Bond todne alone se energy milne ki galat soch rokta hai; ATP symbolic overlay hai.
  • concept Ab grana membrane paas se dekhiye; one-way destination wali bus ki tarah electron aage jaaye, toh photosystems kis order mein chalenge?
    Bus-route analogy ka one-way counterpart.
  • concept NADP reductase par electrons aur H+ ki madad se NADP+ reduce hota hai, jisse NADPH + H+ milta hai; is flow se ATP bhi milta hai.
    NADP reductase ka basic electron-transfer role.
  • concept Saath hi PSII ki water splitting se O2 nikalta hai; ab isi membrane par PSI ka return route paas se samajhte hain.
    Water splitting ka one-line reminder.
  • concept Circular bus ki tarah electron laut raha hai, NADP+ tak net delivery nahi hoti, jiske kaaran NADPH + H+ nahi banta.
    Bus analogy se return aur net delivery distinguish karta hai.
  • concept Is cyclic route ka output sirf ATP hai, NADPH + H+ nahi; ismein PSII ki water splitting nahi chalti, jisse O2 bhi nahi nikalta.
    Cyclic route mein water splitting absent hone se O2 output absent hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Ab stroma mein ATP ko follow kijiye; circular bus wala electron route extra NADPH banaye bina ATP ki demand kaise poori karta hai?
    Bus-route analogy ko ATP demand se jodta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Dekhiye, ATP aur H2O, yani paani, enzyme par milte hain, jisse ADP aur Pi nikalte hain aur enzyme ka shape badalta hai; cyclic flow aise kaamon ke liye ATP supplement karta hai.
    ATP hydrolysis se coupled enzyme conformational work aur independent ATP supplementation ka basic use.
