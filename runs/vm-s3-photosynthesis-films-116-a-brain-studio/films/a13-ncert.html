    Lumen aur stroma ki location ka prerequisite.
  • concept Yahan PS II se nikle electrons replace hone zaroori hain, jaise khaali seat ko phir bharna padta hai.
    Khaali seat ki analogy se replacement ki zaroorat samjhayi.
  • concept Yeh water splitting complex PS II se juda hai aur membrane ki inner side, yaani lumen ki taraf hai; aaiye iske paas chalte hain.
    Inner side ko lumen-facing ke roop mein spasht kiya.
  • concept PS II se judi splitting mein ek H2O ka hisaab do H+, ek [O] oxygen equivalent aur electrons hai; [O] alag stable product nahi hai.
    [O] ko oxygen equivalent bataya, free stable atomic oxygen nahi.
  • pehle ki baat Isi lumen-facing jagah par ginti karte hain; equation ke coefficients aur charges kaise padhenge?
    Equation padhne ka prerequisite sawaal.
  • pehle ki baat Aage ka do, do H2O molecules batata hai, jinke andar kul chaar hydrogen atoms aur do oxygen atoms hain.
    Coefficient aur atom conservation ka hisaab khola.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Har ek O2 par chaar H+ lumen mein aur chaar electrons PS II ko milte hain, jaise chaar khaali seats ke liye chaar replacements.
    Per O2 account ko khaali-seat analogy se dobara joda.
  • concept Charge bhi milta hai: chaar H+ ka plus chaar aur chaar electrons ka minus chaar judkar zero hai; ab release ki jagah paas se dekhte hain.
    Neutral water ke saath net charge conservation check kiya.
  • concept Iska seedha sa jawab hai, H+ aur O2 pehle lumen mein release hote hain, kyunki water splitting complex membrane ki isi inner side par hai.
    Release-side sawaal ka lumen jawab diya.

    Initial release aur baad ke oxygen diffusion ko alag kiya.
