137Looking at plants have you ever wondered why and how there are so many shades of green in their leaves – even in the same plant?✓
  • Ek rang ke andar kitne rang? Aaiye jaante hain; ek hi plant ke patton mein bhi green ke alag shades milte hain, jinka raaz pigments mein hai.
137We can look for an answer to this question by trying to separate the leaf pigments of any green plant through paper chromatography.✓
  • Ek rang ke andar kitne rang? Pehle samajhiye ki extract mein patte ke pigments ghule hain; ab paper chromatography se inhe alag hote hue paas se dekhte hain.
137A chromatographic separation of the leaf pigments shows that the colour that we see in leaves is not due to a single pigment but due to four pigments: Chlorophyll a (bright or blue green in the chromatogram), chlorophyll bb (yellow green), xanthophylls (yellow) and carotenoids (yellow to yellow-orange).✓
  • Paper par rang khulte hain Ab chlorophyll a ka bright blue-green band aur chlorophyll b ka yellow-green band pehchaan lijiye.
  • Paper par rang khulte hain Xanthophylls yellow hain aur carotenoids yellow se yellow-orange hain; yani patte ka rang ek nahi, in chaar pigment groups se milkar dikhta hai.
137Let us now see what roles various pigments play in photosynthesis.✓
  • Shade ka gehra hara rang Aaiye, isi darker leaf ke andar chalte hain aur photosynthesis mein pigments ka kaam samajhte hain.
137Pigments are substances that have an ability to absorb light, at specific wavelengths.✓
  • Pigment light ko chunta hai Nahi, pigments aise substances hain jo specific wavelengths absorb karte hain; dekhiye, chlorophyll a blue light ki energy apne andar le leta hai.
137Can you guess which is the most abundant plant pigment in the world?✓
  • Chloroplast ki thylakoid Par sabse abundant plant pigment kaunsa hai?
  • Chloroplast ki thylakoid Iska seedha sa jawab hai chlorophyll a, jo major photosynthetic pigment bhi hai; ab membrane ke paas chaliye.

  • Rang aur wavelength Dekhiye, curve blue aur red mein upar hai, par green mein neeche hai, yani alag wavelengths barabar absorb nahi hoti hain.
137Of course, you are familiar with the wavelength of the visible spectrum of light as well as the VIBGYOR.✓
  • Rang aur wavelength Isi pigment ki light pakadne ki pasand ka yeh graph hai; visible spectrum ka VIBGYOR yaad kijiye, jisme violet se red tak wavelength badhti hai.

152Or, compare the potted plants kept in the sunlight with those in the shade.✓
  • Shade ka gehra hara rang Yahan same plant ki shady aur sunny side, ya shade aur sunlight mein rakhe pots compare kijiye; kaunse patte darker green hain?
152Which of them has leaves that are darker green?✓
  • Shade ka gehra hara rang Yahan same plant ki shady aur sunny side, ya shade aur sunlight mein rakhe pots compare kijiye; kaunse patte darker green hain?

NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • pehle ki baat Pehle samajhiye ki extract mein patte ke pigments ghule hain; ab paper chromatography se inhe alag hote hue paas se dekhte hain.
    Extract mein pigments ghulne ka prerequisite.
  • pehle ki baat Yahan solvent paper mein upar chadh raha hai; par saare pigments ek saath kyun nahi rehte?
    Solvent ki capillary movement ka prerequisite.
  • concept Iska seedha sa jawab hai ki solvent mein ghulne aur paper se chipakne ka farq inhe alag doori tak le jaata hai, jaise alag raftaar ke runners.
    Solubility aur paper affinity ka mechanism, runners ki misaal.
  • concept Aisa isliye hai kyunki chlorophyll kam hota hai, jabki relatively stable yellow carotenoid pigments bache dikhte hain, jaise hara parda hatne par peeche ka rang nazar aaye.
    Chlorophyll loss aur carotenoid stability ka jawab; parde ki misaal.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Iska jawab hai ki shade-adapted leaves aksar zyada chlorophyll ke kaaran darker green hoti hain, jisse kam light pakadne mein madad milti hai.
    Shade adaptation mein zyada chlorophyll ka low-light capture mein use.
  • pehle ki baat Patte ke andar yeh mesophyll cell hai, jiske chloroplasts mein photosynthesis ke pigments milte hain.
    Mesophyll cell aur chloroplast ka location prerequisite.
  • pehle ki baat Chloroplast ke andar yeh band, chapti thylakoid sacs ke stacks hain; inki membrane mein pigments light pakadte hain.
    Pigments ka thylakoid membrane mein location.
  • concept Iska seedha sa jawab hai chlorophyll a, jo major photosynthetic pigment bhi hai; ab membrane ke paas chaliye.
    Chlorophyll a ko major photosynthetic pigment identify kiya.
  • concept Nahi, pigments aise substances hain jo specific wavelengths absorb karte hain; dekhiye, chlorophyll a blue light ki energy apne andar le leta hai.
    Absorption mein light ki energy pigment mein jaati dikhayi.
  • concept Jaise radio alag frequency par tune hota hai, waise pigment ki molecular banawat uski light ki pasand tay karti hai; ab isi absorption ka graph samajhte hain.
    Molecular structure aur selective absorption ki radio tuning se misaal.
  • pehle ki baat Isi pigment ki light pakadne ki pasand ka yeh graph hai; visible spectrum ka VIBGYOR yaad kijiye, jisme violet se red tak wavelength badhti hai.
    Violet se red tak wavelength badhne ka relation.
  • concept Dekhiye, curve blue aur red mein upar hai, par green mein neeche hai, yani alag wavelengths barabar absorb nahi hoti hain.
    Blue-red peaks aur green trough ki basic graph reading.
