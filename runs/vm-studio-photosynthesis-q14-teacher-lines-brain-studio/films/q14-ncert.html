q14 · Jama hue protons wapas niklein toh ATP kaise ban jaata hai?

Kitab se bahar ke shabd (sirf jaankari, film inse rukti nahi): G3P

141Why are we so interested in the proton gradient?✓
  • Energy ka khaas exit Par pehle, proton gradient mein itni dilchaspi kyun? Iski wajah jaan lete hain: aisa isliye hai kyunki iske ghatne se ATP banane ki energy milti hai.
141This gradient is important because it is the breakdown of this gradient that leads to the synthesis of ATP.✓
  • Energy ka khaas exit Par pehle, proton gradient mein itni dilchaspi kyun? Iski wajah jaan lete hain: aisa isliye hai kyunki iske ghatne se ATP banane ki energy milti hai.
  • Gradient ki taiyaari Ab sawaal banta hai, ikatthe hue H+ wapas kaise niklein? Isme kaam aata hai ATP synthase ka khaas raasta jo gradient ki energy se ATP banwata hai.
  • Flow se shape change CF1 baar-baar ADP aur Pi se several molecules of energy-packed ATP banata hai, dekhiye kaise gradient ka breakdown ab usable chemical energy de raha hai.
141The gradient is broken down due to the movement of protons across the membrane to the stroma through the transmembrane channel of the CF0 of the ATP synthase.✓
  • CF0 ka channel H+ lumen se CF0 ke transmembrane channel se stroma mein lautte hain, jisse gradient ghatta hai; ATPase ka F0 bhi isi part ka naam hai.
142The ATP synthase enzyme consists of two parts: one called the CF0 is embedded in the thylakoid membrane and forms a transmembrane channel that carries out facilitated diffusion of protons across the membrane.✓
  • CF0 ka channel ATP synthase ke do parts dekho: membrane mein dhansa CF0 H+ ko channel se paar jaane deta hai, yeh facilitated diffusion hai.
142The other portion is called CF1 and protrudes on the outer surface of the thylakoid membrane on the side that faces the stroma.✓
  • CF0 ka channel Doosra part CF1 bahar stroma ki taraf nikla hai; ADP aur Pi isi catalytic hisse tak aate hain.
142The break down of the gradient provides enough energy to cause a conformational change in the CF1 particle of the ATP synthase, which makes the enzyme synthesise several molecules of energy-packed ATP.✓
  • Flow se shape change Par gaur karne wali baat hai H+ ke flow se ATP kyun banta hai? Dekhiye, hota kya hai: gradient ki released energy CF1 mein conformational change, yaani shape change, chalati hai; ab jiske kaaran ADP aur Pi ke milne se ATP banta hai.
  • Flow se shape change CF1 baar-baar ADP aur Pi se several molecules of energy-packed ATP banata hai, dekhiye kaise gradient ka breakdown ab usable chemical energy de raha hai.
142Chemiosmosis requires a membrane, a proton pump, a proton gradient and ATP synthase.✓
  • Leak ka galat raasta Isliye chemiosmosis ko chaar cheezein saath chahiye: intact membrane, proton pump, proton gradient aur ATP synthase.
142Energy is used to pump protons across a membrane, to create a gradient or a high concentration of protons within the thylakoid lumen.✓
  • Gradient ki taiyaari Proton pump ko aap motor samajhiye, yeh energy lagaakar H+ ko membrane ke paar stroma se lumen mein pahunchata hai.
  • Gradient ki taiyaari Jisse andar H+ ki concentration zyada ho jaati hai, aur bahar kam, jiske kaaran thylakoid membrane ke across yahi proton gradient ban jaata hai, theek battery ke higher potential, lower potential.
142ATP synthase has a channel that allows diffusion of protons back across the membrane; this releases enough energy to activate ATP synthase enzyme that catalyses the formation of ATP.✓
  • Gradient ki taiyaari Ab sawaal banta hai, ikatthe hue H+ wapas kaise niklein? Isme kaam aata hai ATP synthase ka khaas raasta jo gradient ki energy se ATP banwata hai.
  • Flow se shape change Par gaur karne wali baat hai H+ ke flow se ATP kyun banta hai? Dekhiye, hota kya hai: gradient ki released energy CF1 mein conformational change, yaani shape change, chalati hai; ab jiske kaaran ADP aur Pi ke milne se ATP banta hai.
142Along with the NADPH produced by the movement of electrons, the ATP will be used immediately in the biosynthetic reaction taking place in the stroma, responsible for fixing CO2, and synthesis of sugars.✓
  • Stroma ko supply Ab sahi chalti membrane par bana ATP stroma mein aata hai; electron movement se bana NADPH bhi biosynthetic reactions ko turant supply hota hai.
  • Stroma ko supply Aakhir inki zaroorat hai kyun? CO2 fixation ke baad carbon ko sugar banane laayak badalna hai; pehle CO2 ko RuBP pakadta hai aur do 3-PGA bante hain.
  • Energy se sugar-making Ab ATP energy deta hai aur NADPH reducing power; kai reactions mein dono kharch hote hain aur 3-PGA se G3P banta hai.
  • Energy se sugar-making G3P ka kuch hissa aage kai reactions se sugar banata hai, do teen-carbon units ka carbon ek chhe-carbon glucose mein aa sakta hai.

    Lumen aur stroma ke beech band compartment ki prerequisite.

    Band membrane ko proton jamav bachane se jodta hai.
  • concept Proton pump ko aap motor samajhiye, yeh energy lagaakar H+ ko membrane ke paar stroma se lumen mein pahunchata hai.
    Proton pump ko energy lagane wali motor se samjhata hai.
  • concept Jisse andar H+ ki concentration zyada ho jaati hai, aur bahar kam, jiske kaaran thylakoid membrane ke across yahi proton gradient ban jaata hai, theek battery ke higher potential, lower potential.
    Battery ke potential difference se stored energy ki analogy.

    ADP aur Pi ko catalytic CF1 tak aate dikhata hai.

    CF0 aur F0 ki naming ko jodta hai.

    Conformational change ko shape change ke roop mein samjhata hai.
  • concept Agar membrane mein leak ho, toh kya har nikalta H+ ATP banayega? Dekho, yeh H+ ATP synthase ko bypass kar raha hai.
    Hypothetical leak mein protons enzyme ko bypass karte hain.

    Leak gradient ki energy ko ATP synthesis se uncouple karta hai.

    Intact membrane ki zaroorat leak ke contrast se samajh aati hai.

    ATP ki energy aur NADPH ki reducing power ko 3-PGA se G3P banne ke net outcome se jodta hai.

    Kai reactions ke net carbon outcome mein do teen-carbon units se chhe-carbon sugar ka sambandh.
