h11-6b · PS II se NADPH tak electron ka Z-shaped raasta kya hai?

Film ka dhaancha: sawaal: 1 · pehle ki baat: 2 · concept: 24 · kahan kaam aata hai / asar: 4 · yaad rakhiye: 1

139This whole scheme of transfer of electrons, starting from the PS II, uphill to the acceptor, down the electron transport chain to PS I, excitation of electrons, transfer to another acceptor, and finally down hill to NADP⁺ reducing it to NADPH + H⁺ is called the Z scheme, due to its characterstic shape (Figure 11.5).✓
  • Z ka matlab Isliye ise Z scheme kehte hain: PS II ka light boost, chain ka downhill transfer, PS I ka doosra boost, aur NADP⁺ tak transfer.
  • Z ka matlab NADPH + H⁺ ki notation bhi milti hai; reduced carrier NADPH hai, aur saath likha H⁺ medium ka proton dikhata hai.
  • Figure 11.5 ka poora schematic PS II se upward electron arrow pehle e⁻ acceptor tak, phir descending arrows Electron transport system se PS I tak jaate hain.
  • Figure 11.5 ka poora schematic PS I se doosra upward arrow uske e⁻ acceptor tak, aur final transfer NADP⁺ se NADPH formation tak follow karo.
  • Token aur photon ledger Ab ek marked electron token ko P680 se follow karo: light excitation ke baad woh PS II acceptor, phir carriers aur PS I tak pahunchta hai.
  • Token aur photon ledger PS I ki light-driven donation se electron flow doosre acceptor ki taraf badhta hai aur aakhir NADP⁺ reduction ko supply milti hai.
  • Poora order aur replacement source Poora order phir jodo: P680 par 680 nm light excitation, pehla acceptor, cytochrome-containing chain, aur PS I tak electron supply.
  • Poora order aur replacement source P700 par 700 nm light doosra excitation deti hai; doosre acceptor ke baad electrons NADP⁺ reduction tak pahunchte hain.
  • Poora order aur replacement source Do light boosts aur downhill transfers Z scheme ki profile dete hain, aur ATP formation ki branch bhi is transport se judi hai.

    iP ka meaning aur electron transport se ATP formation ka sambandh batata hai.
  • concept PS II ke neeche H₂O → 2e⁻ + 2H⁺ + [O] shorthand hai, aur electron arrow PS II ko supply dikhata hai.
    Water-input shorthand aur replacement arrow padhta hai.

    [O] accounting symbol hai; released gas O2 hai.
  • concept Ab ek marked electron token ko P680 se follow karo: light excitation ke baad woh PS II acceptor, phir carriers aur PS I tak pahunchta hai.
    Marked token se electron continuity follow karata hai.
  • concept Token ki count beech ke carriers par kya hoti hai?
    Electron handover ka conservation checkpoint hai.
  • concept Aisa isliye hai kyunki har handover mein electron aage transfer hota hai, consume nahi hota; marked token electron continuity samjhata hai.
    Carriers electron ko consume nahi karte.
  • concept Ab do electrons ka batch lo: har electron ke liye PS II aur PS I ke excitation events jodne par minimum chaar reaction-centre photon events bante hain.
    Do electrons ke liye minimum chaar reaction-centre photon events ginta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Yeh two-electron batch ek proton ke saath ek NADP⁺ ko ek NADPH mein reduce kar sakta hai.
    Photon ledger ko NADPH ki two-electron requirement se jodta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Socho, phir aage badhenge: PS II electrons bhej raha hai, lekin PS I ko light na mile, toh kya NADPH formation ka yeh route continuously chalega?
    PS I light band hone par NADPH supply ka prediction poochhta hai.
  • concept Kya electron replacement aur electron excitation ek hi kaam hain?
    Replacement aur excitation ka farq poochhta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Dekho, chain replacement electrons la sakti hai, lekin PS I ka light boost na hone par uske acceptor ki taraf sustained transfer rukega, isliye is route ki NADPH supply continue nahi hogi.
    Light boost ke bina replacement sustained NADPH supply nahi chala sakta.
  • concept Poora order phir jodo: P680 par 680 nm light excitation, pehla acceptor, cytochrome-containing chain, aur PS I tak electron supply.
    P680 aur 680 nm ko cytochrome-containing chain ke order se jodta hai.
  • concept P700 par 700 nm light doosra excitation deti hai; doosre acceptor ke baad electrons NADP⁺ reduction tak pahunchte hain.
    P700 par 700 nm light ko doosre excitation se jodta hai.
  • concept Do electrons aur ek proton se ek NADPH banta hai; carriers mein electrons destroy nahi hote.
    Two-electron requirement aur electron conservation dobara pakka karta hai.
  • concept Ab PS II electrons deta rahe, toh uski apni electron kami kaun poori karega?
    PS II ki replacement supply ka agla sawaal uthata hai.
  • concept Dekho, figure ka water-input arrow isi replacement source ki taraf ishara karta hai; ab water splitting ka poora hisaab kholenge.
    Water-input arrow ko replacement source se jodta hai.
  • yaad rakhiye Ab electron flow ko lagataar chalane ke liye PS II ki replacement supply chahiye; water splitting batayegi ki yeh electrons aur released oxygen kahaan se aate hain.
    Continuous flow ki replacement need ko agle water-splitting film se jodta hai.
