h11-6-2a · Cyclic aur non-cyclic photophosphorylation mein kya difference hai?

7 / 7 NCERT lines film mein padhayi gayi · NCERT page 139, 140

Film ka dhaancha: sawaal: 1 · pehle ki baat: 5 · concept: 26 · kahan kaam aata hai / asar: 1 · yaad rakhiye: 1

139Living organisms have the capability of extracting energy from oxidisable substances and store this in the form of bond energy.✓
  • ATP energy carrier Living organisms oxidisable substances se energy extract karke chemical form mein conserve kar sakte hain; photosynthesis mein light energy bhi chemical form mein capture hoti hai.
  • ATP energy carrier ATP ki chemical banawat se judi energy accounting ko yahaan bond energy kehte hain.
139Special substances like ATP, carry this energy in their chemical bonds.✓
  • ATP energy carrier ATP ko hum usable energy carrier aur oxidisable compounds ko electrons de sakne wale substances pehchaan chuke hain.
  • ATP energy carrier ATP ki chemical banawat se judi energy accounting ko yahaan bond energy kehte hain.
  • Bond aur overall reaction Hota yeh hai ki bond todne mein energy lagti hai; ATP ki overall reaction mein products banne ke saath net energy available hoti hai.
  • Bond aur overall reaction Isliye ATP energy carry karta hai kehna useful hai, lekin bond tootne ko akela energy ka source mat samjho.
139The process through which ATP is synthesised by cells (in mitochondria and chloroplasts) is named phosphorylation.✓
  • Phosphate aur phosphorylation ADP mein inorganic phosphate jodkar ATP banane ko yahaan phosphorylation kehte hain.
  • Light se ATP formation Cells mein ATP synthesis mitochondria aur chloroplasts dono mein hoti hai.
140Photo-phosphorylation is the synthesis of ATP from ADP and inorganic phosphate in the presence of light.✓
  • Phosphate aur phosphorylation Inorganic phosphate alag available phosphate hai, jise Pi ya iP likhte hain.
  • Light se ATP formation Photo-phosphorylation mein photo light ko batata hai: light ki madad se ADP aur inorganic phosphate se ATP banta hai.
  • P700 ka cyclic return Transport system ke paas ADP + Pi substrates aur ATP product ki curved arrow phosphorylation ko identify karti hai.
140When the two photosystems work in a series, first PS II and then the PS I, a process called non-cyclic photo-phosphorylation occurs.✓
  • Non-cyclic onward route Pehla route wahi Z scheme hai: PS II pehle aur PS I uske baad series mein kaam karte hain.
  • Non-cyclic onward route Water se aaye electrons PS II, chain aur PS I ke through NADP⁺ reduction tak pahunchte hain.
  • Non-cyclic onward route Dekho, electron PS I se nikal kar NADP⁺ ko milta hai, apne reaction centre par return loop nahi banata.
140The two photosystems are connected through an electron transport chain, as seen earlier – in the Z scheme.✓
  • Non-cyclic onward route Dono ko electron transport chain jodti hai, yaani electrons receive karke aage dene wale carriers ki linked kadi.
  • Non-cyclic onward route Water se aaye electrons PS II, chain aur PS I ke through NADP⁺ reduction tak pahunchte hain.
140Both ATP and NADPH + H⁺ are synthesised by this kind of electron flow (Figure 11.5).✓
  • Non-cyclic onward route Yeh non-cyclic photophosphorylation ATP aur NADPH dono ki formation support karti hai; water splitting se O₂ bhi milti hai.
  • Non-cyclic onward route NADPH + H⁺ notation mein reduced carrier NADPH hi hai, jaisa hum pehle clear kar chuke hain.
