h11-6a · PS II se NADPH tak electron ka Z-shaped raasta kya hai?

7 / 7 NCERT lines film mein padhayi gayi · NCERT page 138, 139

Film ka dhaancha: sawaal: 1 · pehle ki baat: 4 · concept: 25 · kahan kaam aata hai / asar: 1 · yaad rakhiye: 1

138In photosystem II the reaction centre chlorophyll a absorbs 680 nm wavelength of red light causing electrons to become excited and jump into an orbit farther from the atomic nucleus.✓
  • P680 ki excitation Raasta PS II ke P680 reaction centre se shuru karo, jise hum special chlorophyll a site pehchaan chuke hain.
  • P680 ki excitation 680 nm red light absorb hone par electron excited hota hai, yaani higher-energy state mein pahunchta hai.
  • P680 ki excitation Isse nucleus se farther orbit mein jump ki simple picture se samajh sakte ho; molecular level par important baat higher-energy state hai.
138These electrons are picked up by an electron acceptor which passes them to an electrons transport system consisting of cytochromes (Figure 11.5).✓
  • Primary acceptor Electron acceptor woh molecule hai jo doosre molecule se electron receive karta hai; yahaan pehla receiver primary acceptor hai.
  • Primary acceptor Hota yeh hai ki acceptor electron receive karke use aage electron transport system ko hand over karta hai.
  • Chain se PS I PS II acceptor se electron cytochromes wale transport system mein aata hai; cytochromes electron receive karke aage dene wale proteins hain.
139This movement of electrons is downhill, in terms of an oxidation-reduction or redox potential scale.✓
  • Doosra boost kyun Iska seedha sa jawab hai, beech ke transfers mein electron ki available energy ghatati hai, isliye aage reducing power banane ke liye PS I doosra boost deta hai.
  • Chain se PS I Redox potential electron lene ya dene ki tendency ka measure hai; oxidation aur reduction ko hum electron transfer se jod chuke hain.
  • Chain se PS I Chain mein transfer energetically downhill hai, yaani electron ki available energy ka kuch hissa release hota hai.
139The electrons are not used up as they pass through the electron transport chain, but are passed on to the pigments of photosystem PS I.✓
  • Chain se PS I Aksar log sochte hain ki energy dene par electron kharch ho gaya, par asal mein electron carriers ke beech transfer hota hai, destroy nahi hota.
  • Chain se PS I Yeh chain electrons PS I tak pahunchakar uske donated electrons ki replacement deti hai.
139Simultaneously, electrons in the reaction centre of PS I are also excited when they receive red light of wavelength 700 nm and are transferred to another accepter molecule that has a greater redox potential.✓
  • Chain se PS I Saath hi PS I ka P700 centre 700 nm red light se excite hokar electron apne acceptor ko donate karta hai.
  • Chain se PS I Aisa isliye hai kyunki replacement electron supply banaye rakhti hai, lekin second excitation ki energy PS I ko absorbed light se hi milti hai.
139These electrons then are moved downhill again, this time to a molecule of energy-rich NADP⁺.✓
  • NADPH formation PS I acceptor ke baad electrons phir energetically downhill transfer hote hue final acceptor NADP⁺ tak pahunchte hain.
139The addition of these electrons reduces NADP⁺ to NADPH + H⁺.✓
  • NADPH formation NADP⁺ electron receive karne ke liye available oxidised carrier hai; electron gain se iska reduction hota hai.
  • NADPH formation Ek NADPH banane ka electron ledger hai: NADP⁺ + 2e⁻ + H⁺ → NADPH.
  • NADPH formation Dekho, ek NADPH ke liye do electrons aur ek proton chahiye; electrons light-driven route se aate hain aur carrier mein reducing power jama hoti hai.
