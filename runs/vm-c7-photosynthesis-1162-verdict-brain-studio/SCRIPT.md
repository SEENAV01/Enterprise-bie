**11.6.2-k2** · samjhana · Is sawaal ka jawab isi film mein milega. Par usse pehle ek zaroori cheez pakki kar lo: ATP. Har jeev ko kaam ke liye energy chahiye: saans lene ke liye, chalne ke liye, ek naya molecule banane ke liye. Yeh energy jeev kuch cheezon se nikaalte hain, jaise khaane ka glucose. Yaad karo, oxidation ka matlab hai electron dena, aur reduction ka matlab hai electron lena. Jo cheezein oxidation mein electron de sakti hain, unhe oxidisable substances kehte hain. Glucose aisi hi ek cheez hai. Glucose se energy kaise nikalti hai, yeh aap aage respiration wale chapter mein padhoge. Abhi bas itna jaan lo ki aisi cheezon se energy nikalti hai. Nikli hui energy jeev turant kharch nahi karte. Use molecules ke andar band karke rakh lete hain. Band karke isliye rakhte hain kyunki energy ki zaroorat har jagah aur har pal hoti hai, aur band energy ko jahan chaaho wahan le ja sakte ho. Socho ek gulel, jiski rubber ko kheench kar pakad liya. Energy us khinchi hui rubber mein band hai; chhodoge toh kaam karegi. Molecule mein atom aapas mein bandhanon se jude hote hain, jinhe chemical bonds kehte hain. In bonds mein bhi aise hi energy band rehti hai; ise bond energy kehte hain. ATP ek aisa hi khaas molecule hai. Yeh apne chemical bonds mein energy lekar chalta hai, aur cell ke har kaam tak pahunchata hai. ATP ka poora naam hai adenosine triphosphate. Tri yaani teen, toh isme teen phosphate jude hain. ADP yaani adenosine diphosphate. Di yaani do, toh isme do hi phosphate hain. ATP ko ek bhari hui battery samjho, aur ADP ko ek khaali hoti battery. Khaali battery ko phir se bharna hai, toh ADP mein ek phosphate aur jodna padta hai. Aur is jodne mein energy lagti hai. Jo phosphate akela hai, kisi molecule se juda nahi, use inorganic phosphate kehte hain. Chhote mein ise Pᵢ likhte hain; i yaani inorganic. ADP mein phosphate jodkar ATP banane ko phosphorylation kehte hain. Phosphate jodna, isliye naam phosphorylation. Cell yeh kaam do jagah karta hai: mitochondria mein aur chloroplast mein. Mitochondria wala tareeka bhi aap respiration wale chapter mein padhoge. Hamari kahani chloroplast ki hai. Chloroplast mein yeh charging roshni se hoti hai, bilkul waise jaise solar charger dhoop se phone charge karta hai. Roshni ki madad se ADP aur inorganic phosphate se ATP banane ko photo-phosphorylation kehte hain, kyunki photo ka matlab hi roshni hai.
  - terms: oxidisable substances = jo oxidation mein electron de sakein; bond energy = chemical bonds mein band energy; inorganic phosphate = akela phosphate, Pᵢ; phosphorylation = ADP mein phosphate jodkar ATP banana; Photo-phosphorylation = roshni ki madad se phosphorylation

**11.6.2-k3** · samjhana · Ab Figure 11.5 wali Z scheme yaad karo, jo hum pehle padh chuke hain. Usme do photosystem the: pehle PS II, phir PS I. Dono par roshni padti thi. Dono ke beech carriers ki ek kadi thi, jo electron ko ek se doosre tak aage deti hai. Is kadi ko electron transport chain kehte hain: electron ka transport, yaani le jaana, ek chain mein. Ise ek relay daud ki tarah socho. Har khiladi baton ko agle khiladi ke haath mein deta hai, aur baton aage badhta jaata hai. Yahan baton hai electron, aur khiladi hain carriers. Roshni do jagah zor ka dhakka deti hai: PS II par aur PS I par. Is daud mein baton paani se chalta hai. PS II aur PS I se hokar, aakhir mein NADP⁺ ke haath mein pahunchta hai. NADP⁺ electron le leta hai aur NADPH ban jaata hai. Baton wahin ruk gaya; woh wapas shuru par nahi aata. Toh is raaste mein do cheezein banti hain: ATP aur NADPH. Saath mein paani tootne se O₂ bhi nikalti hai. Aksar ise NADPH + H⁺ likhte hain. Iska matlab bas itna hai ki NADPH ke saath ek H⁺ bhi likha jaata hai. Dhyan do, electron ek seedhe raaste par aage gaya, aur jahan se chala tha wahan wapas nahi lauta. Raasta gol nahi hai. Isliye ise non-cyclic photo-phosphorylation kehte hain. Cyclic yaani gol chakkar wala, aur non-cyclic yaani jo gol chakkar na ho. Yaad rakho: non-cyclic mein dono photosystem ek ke baad ek kaam karte hain, pehle PS II, phir PS I. Aur ATP ke saath NADPH bhi banta hai. Yahan exam ki ek baareek baat hai: naam PS I hai, par kaam mein woh doosre number par aata hai. Number kaam ke kram se nahi pade; PS I pehle khoja gaya tha, isliye uska naam I pada.
  - terms: electron transport chain = carriers ki kadi jo electron aage deti hai; non-cyclic = jo gol chakkar na ho

**11.6.2-k5** · samjhana · Toh shuruaat ka sawaal: jab NADP⁺ tak jaane ka raasta na ho, toh electron kahan jaata hai? Jab NADP⁺ tak raasta na ho, toh electron carriers ki kadi se hokar wapas PS I par laut aata hai. Aisa tab hota hai jab sirf PS I kaam kar raha ho. Electron PS I ke andar hi chakkar lagata rehta hai. Is gol bahav ke saath jo phosphorylation hoti hai, wahi cyclic photo-phosphorylation hai. Par chloroplast mein aisa kahan ho sakta hai, yeh samajhne ke liye pehle uske andar ka naksha yaad karo. Chloroplast mein thylakoid ki chapti thailiyan sikkon ki tarah ek ke upar ek rakhi hoti hain. Inke dher grana kehlate hain. Do dheron ko jodne wali patli, bina dher wali membrane bhi hoti hai, jaise do imaraton ke beech ka galiyara. Inhe hum pehle stroma lamellae ke naam se dekh chuke hain; lamella yaani patli parat. Grana ki membrane mein dono photosystem hote hain, PS I bhi aur PS II bhi. Par stroma lamellae ki membrane mein PS II nahi hota. Aur NADP reductase enzyme bhi nahi hota. NADP reductase woh enzyme hai jo NADP⁺ se NADPH banne ka kaam karata hai. Reductase yaani reduction karaane wala. Toh socho, stroma lamellae mein uchhle electron ko NADP⁺ tak pahunchane wala enzyme hi nahi hai. Isliye electron ke paas wapas lautne ke siva koi raasta nahi. Isliye stroma lamellae ko cyclic photo-phosphorylation ki ek sambhav jagah maana jaata hai. Nateeja pakka yaad rakho: gol raaste mein sirf ATP banta hai, NADPH + H⁺ nahi banta. Aksar log sochte hain ki light reaction mein hamesha ATP, NADPH aur O₂ teeno banenge. Par aisa nahi hai: gol raaste mein electron NADP⁺ tak jaata hi nahi, aur PS II ke bina paani bhi nahi tootta, isliye sirf ATP banta hai. Ek aur haalat hai. Yaad karo, roshni lehron mein chalti hai, aur ek lehar ki chot se agli chot tak ki doori wavelength hai. PS II ka centre P680 hai, jo lagbhag 680 nm ki roshni chahta hai. PS I ka P700, 700 nm tak ki roshni bhi pakad leta hai. Jab sirf 680 nm se aage ki roshni mile, toh PS II theek se nahi jaagta. Sirf PS I chalta hai, aur cyclic photo-phosphorylation hoti hai. Aksar log ise ulta samajh lete hain, par 680 nm se aage ka matlab hai 680 se zyada, yaani lambi wavelength, kam wavelength nahi. Paudhe ko yeh gol raasta kyon chahiye, iska ek bada kaaran hum aage Calvin cycle mein dekhenge. Abhi bas itna jaan lo ki kabhi paudhe ko NADPH se zyada ATP chahiye hota hai.
  - terms: NADP reductase = reduction karaane wala enzyme jo NADP⁺ se NADPH banwata hai
  - screen: Electron wapas PS I par; chloroplast ka naksha: grana sikkon ke dher jaise, unhe jodti stroma lamellae galiyare jaisi; grana ki membrane mein PS I aur PS II, stroma lamellae mein sirf PS I (PS II aur NADP reductase nahi); lautta electron; sirf ATP; 680 nm aur 700 nm ki lehrein, 680 se lambi lehar.
  - NCERT: p010-i12 When only PS I is functional, the electron is circulated within the photosystem 
**11.6.2-k6** · example · Ab ek electron ko pakad kar stroma lamellae mein uska poora safar chalate hain. Roshni P 700 par padi, aur uska ek electron energy lekar uchhal gaya. e⁻ acceptor ne use pakda, aur woh carriers ki kadi mein aage badha. Kadi ke aage use NADP⁺ tak le jaane wala NADP reductase mila hi nahi, kyunki yahan woh enzyme hai hi nahi. Toh electron kadi se ghoom kar wapas P 700 par aa gaya, aur agli roshni ka intezaar karne laga. Is ek chakkar ke saath ATP bana. NADPH ek bhi nahi bana, aur O₂ bhi nahi nikli.
  - screen: Stroma lamellae mein ek electron ka safar, kadam-kadam ek hi tasveer par: P 700 par roshni, electron uchhla, e⁻ acceptor, kadi, NADP reductase ki khaali jagah, wapas P 700; ATP bana, NADPH aur O₂ nahi.
**11.6.2-k7** · ruko, socho · Ruko, pehle khud socho: agar kisi patti par sirf 700 nm wali roshni pade, toh usme NADPH banega ya nahi? Aapne socha hoga ki NADPH nahi banega, aur yahi sahi hai. Aisi roshni mein sirf PS I chalega, electron gol chakkar lagayega, aur sirf ATP banega. Ab dono raaste ek saath rakh kar dekho. Seedhe raaste, yaani non-cyclic, mein electron paani se chalta hai: pehle PS II, phir PS I, aur aakhir NADP⁺. ATP, NADPH aur O₂ teeno bante hain. Gol raaste, yaani cyclic, mein sirf PS I kaam karta hai, aur electron wapas P 700 par aata hai. Sirf ATP banta hai; na NADPH, na O₂. Ab shuruaat ka sawaal phir se: NADP⁺ ka raasta band ho, toh electron kahan jaayega, aur kya kuch banega? Raasta band ho, toh electron carriers ki kadi se ghoom kar wapas P 700 par aa jaata hai. Aur tab bhi kuch banta hai: ATP, bas NADPH nahi. Ab ek ajeeb baat par gaur karo, jo hamari agli film ka sawaal hai: dono raaston mein ATP bana, par electron ne kahin bhi ADP ko chhua tak nahi. Toh ATP bana kaise? ATP bana kaise, yeh aage hum dekhenge.
  - screen: Sawaal screen par; dono raaste saath-saath: non-cyclic (paani, PS II, PS I, NADP⁺; ATP, NADPH, O₂) aur cyclic (PS I se wapas P 700; sirf ATP); shuruaat ka sawaal phir screen par, NADP⁺ ka band raasta; electron kadi se ghoom kar wapas P 700, ATP bana, NADPH nahi; aakhir mein electron aur ADP ke beech khaali jagah, sawaal khula.
