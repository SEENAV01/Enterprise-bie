q14 · Jama hue protons wapas niklein toh ATP kaise ban jaata hai?

Kitab se bahar ke shabd (sirf jaankari, film inse rukti nahi): G3P

141Why are we so interested in the proton gradient?✓
  • Jama energy ka exit Proton gradient mein itni dilchaspi kyun—kyunki iske ghatne se ATP banane ki energy milti hai.
141This gradient is important because it is the breakdown of this gradient that leads to the synthesis of ATP.✓
  • Gradient charge hota hai Ab H+ wapas kaise niklein—ATP synthase ka khaas raasta gradient ki energy se ATP banwata hai.
  • Sahi raaste se ATP CF1 baar-baar ADP aur Pi se several molecules of energy-packed ATP banata hai—gradient ka breakdown ab usable chemical energy de raha hai.
141The gradient is broken down due to the movement of protons across the membrane to the stroma through the transmembrane channel of the CF0 of the ATP synthase.✓
  • Sahi raaste se ATP H+ lumen se CF0 ke transmembrane channel se stroma mein lautte hain, toh gradient ghatta hai; ATPase ka F0 bhi isi part ka naam hai.
142The ATP synthase enzyme consists of two parts: one called the CF0 is embedded in the thylakoid membrane and forms a transmembrane channel that carries out facilitated diffusion of protons across the membrane.✓
  • Sahi raaste se ATP ATP synthase ke do parts dekho: membrane mein dhansa CF0 H+ ko channel se paar jaane deta hai—yeh facilitated diffusion hai.
142The other portion is called CF1 and protrudes on the outer surface of the thylakoid membrane on the side that faces the stroma.✓
  • Sahi raaste se ATP Doosra part CF1 bahar stroma ki taraf nikla hai; ADP aur Pi isi catalytic hisse tak aate hain.
142The break down of the gradient provides enough energy to cause a conformational change in the CF1 particle of the ATP synthase, which makes the enzyme synthesise several molecules of energy-packed ATP.✓
  • Sahi raaste se ATP H+ ke flow se ATP kyun banta hai—gradient ki released energy CF1 mein conformational change, yaani shape change, chalati hai; ab ADP aur Pi se ATP banta hai.
  • Sahi raaste se ATP CF1 baar-baar ADP aur Pi se several molecules of energy-packed ATP banata hai—gradient ka breakdown ab usable chemical energy de raha hai.
142Chemiosmosis requires a membrane, a proton pump, a proton gradient and ATP synthase.✓
  • Galat exit ka nuksaan Isliye chemiosmosis ko chaar cheezein saath chahiye: intact membrane, proton pump, proton gradient aur ATP synthase.
142Energy is used to pump protons across a membrane, to create a gradient or a high concentration of protons within the thylakoid lumen.✓
  • Gradient charge hota hai Proton pump energy lagaakar H+ ko membrane ke paar stroma se lumen mein pahunchata hai.
  • Gradient charge hota hai Andar H+ ki concentration zyada, bahar kam—thylakoid membrane ke across yahi proton gradient banta hai.
142ATP synthase has a channel that allows diffusion of protons back across the membrane; this releases enough energy to activate ATP synthase enzyme that catalyses the formation of ATP.✓
  • Sahi raaste se ATP H+ ke flow se ATP kyun banta hai—gradient ki released energy CF1 mein conformational change, yaani shape change, chalati hai; ab ADP aur Pi se ATP banta hai.
142Along with the NADPH produced by the movement of electrons, the ATP will be used immediately in the biosynthetic reaction taking place in the stroma, responsible for fixing CO2, and synthesis of sugars.✓
  • ATP ka agla kaam Ab sahi chalti membrane par bana ATP stroma mein aata hai; electron movement se bana NADPH bhi biosynthetic reactions ko turant supply hota hai.
  • ATP ka agla kaam Inki zaroorat kyun—CO2 fixation ke baad carbon ko sugar banane laayak badalna hai; pehle CO2 ko RuBP pakadta hai aur do 3-PGA bante hain.
  • ATP ka agla kaam Ab ATP energy deta hai aur NADPH reducing power; kai reactions mein dono kharch hote hain aur 3-PGA se G3P banta hai.
  • ATP ka agla kaam G3P ka kuch hissa aage kai reactions se sugar banata hai—do teen-carbon units ka carbon ek chhe-carbon glucose mein aa sakta hai.
151During this process a proton gradient is created across the membrane of the thylakoid.✓
  • Gradient charge hota hai Andar H+ ki concentration zyada, bahar kam—thylakoid membrane ke across yahi proton gradient banta hai.
151The breakdown of the protons gradient due to movement through the F0 part of the ATPase enzyme releases enough energy for synthesis of ATP.✓
  • Sahi raaste se ATP H+ ke flow se ATP kyun banta hai—gradient ki released energy CF1 mein conformational change, yaani shape change, chalati hai; ab ADP aur Pi se ATP banta hai.
  • Sahi raaste se ATP CF1 baar-baar ADP aur Pi se several molecules of energy-packed ATP banata hai—gradient ka breakdown ab usable chemical energy de raha hai.

NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • pehle ki baat Pehle jagah pehchaan lo: leaf ki is mesophyll cell ke chloroplast mein photosynthesis hota hai.
    Mesophyll cell aur chloroplast ki location ka prerequisite recall.
  • pehle ki baat Dhoop yahan pahunchti hai; ab isi chloroplast ke andar chalte hain.
    Cell se chloroplast tak spatial bridge.
  • pehle ki baat Yeh thylakoid band chapti thaili hai; andar lumen aur bahar stroma hai.
    Band compartment aur dono taraf ki spatial prerequisite.
  • pehle ki baat Dhoop ki energy kaam aati hai, par band membrane kyun chahiye—protons ka jamav bachane ke liye; ab paas se dekho.
    Intact membrane ko jamav bachane ke cause se jodta hai.
  • concept Doosra part CF1 bahar stroma ki taraf nikla hai; ADP aur Pi isi catalytic hisse tak aate hain.
    CF1 ko ADP-Pi ke catalytic site se jodta hai.
  • concept H+ lumen se CF0 ke transmembrane channel se stroma mein lautte hain, toh gradient ghatta hai; ATPase ka F0 bhi isi part ka naam hai.
    CF0 aur summary mein istemaal F0 naam ko jodta hai.
  • concept H+ ke flow se ATP kyun banta hai—gradient ki released energy CF1 mein conformational change, yaani shape change, chalati hai; ab ADP aur Pi se ATP banta hai.
    Central stalk ki rotation ko catalytic shape change se jodta hai.
  • concept Agar membrane mein leak ho, toh kya har nikalta H+ ATP banayega—dekho, yeh H+ ATP synthase ko bypass kar raha hai.
    Hypothetical leak se ATP synthase bypass hota dikhata hai.
  • concept Nahi; leak se lautte H+ gradient ki energy waste karte hain, CF1 ko ATP banane ke liye yeh energy nahi milti.
    Leak ke proton flow ki energy ATP synthesis se uncouple hoti hai.
  • concept Isliye chemiosmosis ko chaar cheezein saath chahiye: intact membrane, proton pump, proton gradient aur ATP synthase.
    Membrane ka intact hona zaroori kyun hai, explicit karta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Inki zaroorat kyun—CO2 fixation ke baad carbon ko sugar banane laayak badalna hai; pehle CO2 ko RuBP pakadta hai aur do 3-PGA bante hain.
    CO2 fixation ka basic input-output: RuBP aur CO2 se do 3-PGA.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Ab ATP energy deta hai aur NADPH reducing power; kai reactions mein dono kharch hote hain aur 3-PGA se G3P banta hai.
    ATP ki energy aur NADPH ki reducing power ko reduction ke net outcome se jodta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar G3P ka kuch hissa aage kai reactions se sugar banata hai—do teen-carbon units ka carbon ek chhe-carbon glucose mein aa sakta hai.
    Kai reactions ka net carbon outcome dikhata hai, direct single-step mechanism nahi.
