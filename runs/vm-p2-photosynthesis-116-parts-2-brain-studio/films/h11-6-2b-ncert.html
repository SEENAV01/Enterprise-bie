h11-6-2b · Cyclic aur non-cyclic photophosphorylation mein kya difference hai?

6 / 6 NCERT lines film mein padhayi gayi · NCERT page 140

Film ka dhaancha: sawaal: 1 · pehle ki baat: 4 · concept: 19 · kahan kaam aata hai / asar: 6 · yaad rakhiye: 2

140When only PS I is functional, the electron is circulated within the photosystem and the phosphorylation occurs due to cyclic flow of electrons (Figure 11.6).✓
  • Electron ka ledger Starting P700 ne ek electron diya aur loop complete hone par ek electron wapas paaya.
  • Electron ka ledger Electron flow ATP synthesis support karta hai, lekin ek loop ko exactly ek ATP ke barabar nahi maan sakte.
  • Membrane ki location Sirf PS I functional ho, toh excited electron transport chain se PS I par laut sakta hai aur ATP-only cyclic photophosphorylation hoti hai.
  • Dono routes Cyclic flow mein P700 excite hota hai, electron acceptor aur transport system se guzarta hai, phir P700 par laut aata hai.
140A possible location where this could be happening is in the stroma lamellae.✓
  • Membrane ki location Stroma lamellae, wahi unstacked membrane connections, is flow ki possible location hain; inhe exclusive location mat samjho.
140While the membrane or lamellae of the grana have both PS I and PS II the stroma lamellae membranes lack PS II as well as NADP reductase enzyme.✓
  • Membrane ki location Grana membranes mein PS I aur PS II dono hote hain, jabki stroma lamellae mein PS II aur NADP reductase absent hote hain.
140The excited electron does not pass on to NADP⁺ but is cycled back to the PS I complex through the electron transport chain (Figure 11.6).✓
  • Return route Ab P700 se nikle ek marked electron ko follow karo: light excitation ke baad woh acceptor aur transport system se hokar P700 par laut aata hai.
  • Electron ka ledger Iska seedha sa jawab hai, electron return route mein raha, isliye NADP⁺ ko zero net electrons mile aur NADPH nahi bana.
  • Bus aur electron Yahaan travelling token electron hai, circular route PS I ka cyclic flow hai, aur depot P700 hai.
  • Wavelength aur return Cyclic flow mein NADP⁺ reduction ke bajay return route chalta hai, isliye net NADPH ya O₂ nahi banti.
  • Products predict karo Hota yeh hai ki returning electron NADP⁺ ko reduce nahi karta aur is route mein PS II-associated water splitting nahi chalti, isliye cyclic flow ATP deta hai, net NADPH aur O₂ nahi.
  • Dono routes Cyclic flow mein P700 excite hota hai, electron acceptor aur transport system se guzarta hai, phir P700 par laut aata hai.
140The cyclic flow hence, results only in the synthesis of ATP, but not of NADPH + H⁺.✓
  • Electron ka ledger Iska seedha sa jawab hai, electron return route mein raha, isliye NADP⁺ ko zero net electrons mile aur NADPH nahi bana.
  • Electron ka ledger Electron flow ATP synthesis support karta hai, lekin ek loop ko exactly ek ATP ke barabar nahi maan sakte.
  • Electron ka ledger Is cyclic route mein water splitting nahi judi, isliye isse net O₂ production bhi nahi hoti.
  • Bus aur electron Onward route water se photosystems ke through NADP⁺ tak non-cyclic flow hai; dono routes ATP formation support karte hain, par NADP⁺ ko delivery sirf onward route deta hai.
  • Membrane ki location Sirf PS I functional ho, toh excited electron transport chain se PS I par laut sakta hai aur ATP-only cyclic photophosphorylation hoti hai.
  • Wavelength aur return Cyclic flow mein NADP⁺ reduction ke bajay return route chalta hai, isliye net NADPH ya O₂ nahi banti.
  • Products predict karo Hota yeh hai ki returning electron NADP⁺ ko reduce nahi karta aur is route mein PS II-associated water splitting nahi chalti, isliye cyclic flow ATP deta hai, net NADPH aur O₂ nahi.
  • Dono routes Is return route ka net product ATP hai; NADPH aur O₂ nahi.
140Cyclic photophosphorylation also occurs when only light of wavelengths beyond 680 nm are available for excitation.✓
  • Wavelength aur return Jab excitation ke liye sirf 680 nm se beyond wavelengths available hon, tab bhi cyclic photophosphorylation ho sakti hai.

NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • pehle ki baat Ab P700 se nikle ek marked electron ko follow karo: light excitation ke baad woh acceptor aur transport system se hokar P700 par laut aata hai.
    Marked electron follow karke route ka prerequisite deta hai.
  • concept Starting P700 ne ek electron diya aur loop complete hone par ek electron wapas paaya.
    P700 ka electron ledger dikhata hai.
  • concept Is batch se NADP⁺ ko kitne net electrons mile?
    Net delivery ka checkpoint hai.
  • concept Iska seedha sa jawab hai, electron return route mein raha, isliye NADP⁺ ko zero net electrons mile aur NADPH nahi bana.
    Zero net electron delivery samjhata hai.
  • concept Electron flow ATP synthesis support karta hai, lekin ek loop ko exactly ek ATP ke barabar nahi maan sakte.
    Ek loop ko ek ATP ki fixed stoichiometry se alag rakhta hai.
  • concept Is cyclic route mein water splitting nahi judi, isliye isse net O₂ production bhi nahi hoti.
    Water splitting aur oxygen output jodta hai.
  • pehle ki baat Socho, ek bus depot se nikal kar circular route par stations se guzarti hai aur usi depot par laut aati hai.
    Depot mapping screen par aati hai; agle analogy shot mein boli jaati hai.
  • pehle ki baat Doosri bus onward route lekar alag terminal tak pahunchti hai; woh is trip mein starting depot par nahi lautti.
    Onward delivery ki analogy hai.
  • concept Dono trips mein movement hai, lekin unki destination ka farq kya hai?
    Destination ka sawaal hai.
  • concept Hota yeh hai ki circular trip starting supply ko wapas deti hai, jabki onward trip passenger ko alag final destination tak pahunchati hai.
    Return aur delivery compare karta hai.
  • concept Yahaan travelling token electron hai, circular route PS I ka cyclic flow hai, aur depot P700 hai.
    Analogy explicitly map karta hai.
  • concept Onward route water se photosystems ke through NADP⁺ tak non-cyclic flow hai; dono routes ATP formation support karte hain, par NADP⁺ ko delivery sirf onward route deta hai.
    Shared ATP aur alag delivery compare karta hai.
  • concept Stroma lamellae, wahi unstacked membrane connections, is flow ki possible location hain; inhe exclusive location mat samjho.
    Possible ko exclusive se alag rakhta hai.
  • pehle ki baat NADP reductase woh enzyme hai jo supplied electrons se NADP⁺ ko NADPH banane mein madad karta hai.
    Reductase ka basic catalytic role dikhata hai; full stoichiometric equation nahi deta.
  • concept Ab aap sochoge, NADP⁺ reduction ke electrons kis carrier se milte hain?
    Carrier ki pehchaan poochhta hai.
  • concept Dekho, ferredoxin naam ka carrier electrons supply karta hai aur NADP reductase reaction catalyse karta hai; aksar log dono roles ko ek samajhte hain, par asal mein yeh alag hain.
    Carrier aur enzyme ke roles alag karta hai.
  • concept Cyclic flow mein NADP⁺ reduction ke bajay return route chalta hai, isliye net NADPH ya O₂ nahi banti.
    Oxygen absence bhi jodta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Socho, phir aage badhenge: PS I ka electron acceptor aur carriers se P700 par laut raha hai; kya ATP ke saath NADPH aur oxygen bhi zaroor banengi?
    Product prediction checkpoint hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Apna jawab electron ki destination aur water-splitting requirement se jodo.
    Prediction ko mechanism se jodta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Hota yeh hai ki returning electron NADP⁺ ko reduce nahi karta aur is route mein PS II-associated water splitting nahi chalti, isliye cyclic flow ATP deta hai, net NADPH aur O₂ nahi.
    PS II water splitting ko oxygen se jodta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Aksar log ATP bante dekhkar saare light-reaction products maan lete hain, par product list electron route par depend karti hai.
    Common product misconception sudhaarta hai.
  • concept Ab dono routes order mein jodo: non-cyclic flow mein water electrons deta hai, PS II aur PS I successive light boosts dete hain, aur electrons NADP⁺ tak pahunchte hain.
    Non-cyclic source aur destination compare karta hai.
  • concept Isse ATP aur NADPH milte hain, aur water oxidation O₂ release karti hai.
    Non-cyclic products aur oxygen ka origin dikhata hai.
  • yaad rakhiye Is return route ka net product ATP hai; NADPH aur O₂ nahi.
    Oxygen absence bhi compare karta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Ab dono mein ATP banta kaise hai?
    Agla energy-link sawaal kholta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Iska seedha sa jawab hai, electron route aur products pehchaan liye hain; ab thylakoid membrane ke across proton arrangement se ATP formation ka actual connection kholenge.
    Proton arrangement ko agle mechanism se jodta hai.
  • yaad rakhiye Electron flow ATP formation support karta hai, lekin beech ka energy link abhi chhupa hai; chemiosmotic hypothesis proton gradient se yeh link samjhayegi.
    Chemiosmotic hypothesis ki next-film bridge hai.
