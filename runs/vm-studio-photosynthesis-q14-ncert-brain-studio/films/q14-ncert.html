q14 · Jama hue protons wapas niklein toh ATP kaise ban jaata hai?

141Why are we so interested in the proton gradient?✓
  • Jamav mein kya khaas hai? Proton gradient mein interest isliye hai: isi ke breakdown se ATP synthesis chalti hai; chalo, andar iska raasta dekhein.
141This gradient is important because it is the breakdown of this gradient that leads to the synthesis of ATP.✓
  • Jamav mein kya khaas hai? Proton gradient mein interest isliye hai: isi ke breakdown se ATP synthesis chalti hai; chalo, andar iska raasta dekhein.
141The gradient is broken down due to the movement of protons across the membrane to the stroma through the transmembrane channel of the CF0 of the ATP synthase.✓
  • Sahi raaste se waapsi Ab H+ lumen se CF0 ke channel ke raaste stroma mein lautte hain; isi movement se proton gradient breakdown hota hai.
142The ATP synthase enzyme consists of two parts: one called the CF0 is embedded in the thylakoid membrane and forms a transmembrane channel that carries out facilitated diffusion of protons across the membrane.✓
  • Exit bhi, machine bhi Yeh ATP synthase do parts mein kaam karta hai: membrane mein dhansa CF0 H+ ke liye transmembrane channel banata hai.
  • Sahi raaste se waapsi Kya CF0 inhein ulta pump karta hai? Nahi, yeh facilitated diffusion hai: H+ apne electrochemical gradient ke saath behte hain, gravity ke hisaab se nahi.
142The other portion is called CF1 and protrudes on the outer surface of the thylakoid membrane on the side that faces the stroma.✓
  • Exit bhi, machine bhi Doosra part CF1 hai; dekho, iska head membrane ki outer surface se stroma ki taraf nikla hai.
142The break down of the gradient provides enough energy to cause a conformational change in the CF1 particle of the ATP synthase, which makes the enzyme synthesise several molecules of energy-packed ATP.✓
  • CF1 mein ATP banta hai Energy CF1 mein kya badalti hai? Iske catalytic parts ki shape—isi ko conformational change kehte hain.
  • CF1 mein ATP banta hai Proton flow chalta rehta hai, toh CF1 ke conformational changes baar-baar hote hain aur several molecules of energy-packed ATP bante aur stroma mein chhootte hain.
142Chemiosmosis requires a membrane, a proton pump, a proton gradient and ATP synthase.✓
  • Exit bhi, machine bhi Chemiosmosis ke liye chaaron saath chahiye: membrane, proton pump, proton gradient aur ATP synthase.
142Energy is used to pump protons across a membrane, to create a gradient or a high concentration of protons within the thylakoid lumen.✓
  • Pehle energy jama hoti hai Ab proton pump energy use karke H+ ko stroma se thylakoid lumen ki taraf pahunchata hai.
  • Pehle energy jama hoti hai Isse thylakoid membrane ke aar-paar proton gradient banta hai: lumen mein H+ concentration zyada ho jaati hai.
142ATP synthase has a channel that allows diffusion of protons back across the membrane; this releases enough energy to activate ATP synthase enzyme that catalyses the formation of ATP.✓
  • Sahi raaste se waapsi Is waapsi mein release hui energy ATP synthase ko activate karti hai; ATPase enzyme ka yahi F0 part proton flow ko ATP synthesis se jodta hai.
  • CF1 mein ATP banta hai Ab CF1 par ADP aur inorganic phosphate, yaani Pi, aate hain; enzyme inhein jodkar energy-packed ATP banata hai.
142Along with the NADPH produced by the movement of electrons, the ATP will be used immediately in the biosynthetic reaction taking place in the stroma, responsible for fixing CO2, and synthesis of sugars.✓
  • ATP ka agla kaam Bana hua ATP turant stroma ki biosynthetic reactions ko milta hai; electron movement se bana NADPH bhi wahin aata hai.
  • ATP ka agla kaam Inka kaam kya hai? Stroma ki kai reactions mein CO2 fixation aur sugar synthesis hoti hai; ATP energy deta hai aur NADPH reducing power deta hai.
151During this process a proton gradient is created across the membrane of the thylakoid.✓
  • Pehle energy jama hoti hai Isse thylakoid membrane ke aar-paar proton gradient banta hai: lumen mein H+ concentration zyada ho jaati hai.
151The breakdown of the protons gradient due to movement through the F0 part of the ATPase enzyme releases enough energy for synthesis of ATP.✓
  • Sahi raaste se waapsi Is waapsi mein release hui energy ATP synthase ko activate karti hai; ATPase enzyme ka yahi F0 part proton flow ko ATP synthesis se jodta hai.

NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • pehle ki baat Pehle yaad rakho: chloroplast ke thylakoid ke andar lumen hai, aur uske bahar stroma hai.
    Recalls compartments separated by the thylakoid membrane.
  • concept Ab proton pump energy use karke H+ ko stroma se thylakoid lumen ki taraf pahunchata hai.
    Recalls pumping direction; electron transport details remain in their own film.
  • pehle ki baat H+ turant wapas kyun nahi chale jaate? Intact membrane unhein seedhe lipid layer se aasaani se paar nahi hone deti.
    Low proton permeability preserves the gradient.
  • concept Agar uncontrolled leak se H+ machine ko bypass karke niklein toh? Gradient ghatta hai, par us energy se ATP nahi banta—jaise turbine ko bypass karta paani.
    Localized uncontrolled leakage dissipates gradient energy without ATP production; turbine bypass analogy.
  • concept Kya CF0 inhein ulta pump karta hai? Nahi, yeh facilitated diffusion hai: H+ apne electrochemical gradient ke saath behte hain, gravity ke hisaab se nahi.
    Distinguishes downhill electrochemical diffusion from pumping or gravitational motion.
  • concept Is waapsi mein release hui energy ATP synthase ko activate karti hai; ATPase enzyme ka yahi F0 part proton flow ko ATP synthesis se jodta hai.
    Shows central stalk rotation as mechanical coupling; the whole catalytic head does not rotate.
  • concept Energy CF1 mein kya badalti hai? Iske catalytic parts ki shape—isi ko conformational change kehte hain.
    Explains conformational change as catalytic protein shape change.
  • concept Ab CF1 par ADP aur inorganic phosphate, yaani Pi, aate hain; enzyme inhein jodkar energy-packed ATP banata hai.
    Names ATP synthesis substrates ADP and inorganic phosphate.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Inka kaam kya hai? Stroma ki kai reactions mein CO2 fixation aur sugar synthesis hoti hai; ATP energy deta hai aur NADPH reducing power deta hai.
    Distinguishes ATP energy and NADPH reducing power; distributed enzymes show that fixation and sugar production require multiple reactions.
