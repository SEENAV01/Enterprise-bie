- Game: hisse jodo. Naye country payment systems common connector se jodo; har addition par usable cross-border routes badhein bina har pair ka naya link banaye.

## Naya paisa kya hai, aur total paisa kaise ginte hain?

Polymer, crypto aur RBI ke money aggregates ko alag-alag pehchaano.

**25 · Note polymer ka bana dein, toh kya woh bas aam plastic ka tukda hoga?**

- Paheli: Roz mudne aur bheegne wala note agar zyada chale, toh replacement ka kharcha bhi bach sakta hai.
- Baatein (4): Polymer banknote traditional cotton substrate ki jagah security-grade BOPP aur embedded security features se banta hai, ordinary plastic se nahi.; 2013 mein ₹10 polymer field trial approve hua; ₹10-₹20 jaise small notes ka proposal aur paper ke saath coexistence ko confirmed nationwide rollout mat samjho.; Polymer ka aim longer life, counterfeit resistance aur lower replacement cost hai; lifespan up to chaar guna ho sakta hai.; Australia ne 1988 mein polymer banknote introduce kiya aur 50 se zyada countries polymer notes use karti hain.
- Film: lambi film. Screen par: Repeated handling cycles mein cotton note replacement line tak pahunchta hai, jabki polymer note aur cycles survive karta hai.
- Game: slider se kaaran-asar. Handling cycles badhao aur cotton-polymer notes ki surviving quantity aur replacement cost compare karo.

**26 · Crypto bhi screen par number hai—phir woh digital rupee ya UPI balance jaisa kyun nahi?**

- Paheli: Teen digital screens dikhne mein similar hain, par issuer, ownership record aur legal status alag hain.
- Baatein (5): Cryptocurrency blockchain par created-stored private digital asset hai; India mein na RBI-issued hai aur na legal tender.; Distributed ledger ownership records ko network par maintain karta hai, central bank-account ledger ke bina bhi.; 2007–08 crisis ke baad fiat-banking par distrust aur transaction fees-interoperability ki complaints kuch crypto supporters ki motivations bani, crypto value ki guarantee nahi.; Public permissionless blockchain ke Prelims case mein inspectable non-single-user-controlled ledger aur permissionless applications sahi hain; blockchain ko crypto-only kehna galat hai, isliye answer 1 aur 3 hai.; Digital rupee RBI currency hai, UPI bank deposits ka payment channel hai aur crypto private digital asset hai.
- Film: lambi film. Screen par: Ek central ledger ki jagah transaction record ki matching copies distributed network ke nodes par update hoti hain.
- Game: samooh mein baanto. Issuer, ledger aur legal-status parts se digital rupee, UPI deposit payment aur crypto ke profiles banao; sahi profile par ownership-record animation chale.

**27 · RBI total paisa ginta hai, toh cash aur bank balance ko ek hi number mein kyun nahi jod deta?**

- Paheli: Bank ki RBI reserve balance aur tumhari pocket ka cash dono rupees hain, par dono public spending money nahi.
- Baatein (6): Money supply kisi given time par economy mein spending, saving aur payments ke liye available money ka stock hai.; RBI M0 aur M1–M4 jaise measures alag components ke liye use karta hai; M3 key broad-money indicator hai, M4 broadest hai.; Households, businesses, banks aur RBI ke beech money flow hota rehta hai, par money supply ek time-point ka stock hai.; M0 reserve ya high-powered money hai, jo RBI-created monetary base ko capture karta hai.; M0 = currency in circulation + other deposits with RBI + bankers' deposits with RBI, jisme CRR balances bhi aate hain.; Reserve base banking-system lending aur deposit expansion ko support karta hai; expansion automatic ya unlimited nahi hota.
- Film: lambi film. Screen par: RBI base ke teen components assemble hote hain aur banking lending-deposit cycle se public deposits ka layer expand hota hai.
- Game: hisse jodo. Currency, RBI other deposits aur bank reserves se M0 banao; public deposit ko galti se base mein jodne par double-count warning aaye.

**28 · Pocket ka cash, savings account aur FD—RBI inhe alag baskets mein kyun rakhta hai?**

- Paheli: FD bhi tumhara paisa hai, lekin chai ka payment cash ya demand deposit jitni seedhi tarah nahi hota.
- Baatein (7): M1 narrow money = public ke paas currency + demand deposits + other deposits with RBI; iske components everyday use ke liye highly liquid hain.; M2 = M1 + Post Office Savings Banks ke savings deposits; NSC jaise instruments is addition mein nahi aate.; M3 broad money = M1 + banks ke time deposits, jisme fixed aur recurring deposits shamil hain; reported money-supply growth aam taur par M3 hoti hai.; RBI M3 ko inflationary pressure, liquidity, credit growth aur monetary-policy effectiveness samajhne ke liye track karta hai.; M4 broadest measure = M3 + Post Office Savings Banks ke all deposits, NSC ko chhodkar.; NSC long-term savings instrument hai, readily spendable money nahi; isliye M4 mein count nahi hota.; M0 ki currency in circulation aur M1 ki public currency alag hain; M2 aur M3 bhi M1 se nikli alag branches hain, seedhi M1–M2–M3 nesting nahi.
- Film: lambi film. Screen par: M1 basket se do branches nikalti hain—post-office savings jodne par M2 aur bank time deposits jodne par M3; M3 mein eligible post-office deposits se M4 banta hai.
- Game: hisse jodo. Cash, demand deposits, FD, post-office deposits aur NSC tokens se aggregates banao; sahi formula par total update ho aur excluded instrument par gate band rahe.
