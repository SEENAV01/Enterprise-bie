  • Leaf ke andar Hota yeh hai ki lumen ke H⁺ ka ATP synthase se stroma ki taraf controlled return gradient ki stored energy ko ATP synthesis se couple karta hai.

  • Baandh aur turbine Stroma low-level side hai, aur ATP synthase turbine machine hai jo controlled return ko ATP formation se jodti hai.

141The gradient is broken down due to the movement of protons across the membrane to the stroma through the transmembrane channel of the CF₀ of the ATP synthase.✓
  • Chloroplast ka context Return route transmembrane hai, yaani thylakoid membrane ke aar-paar phaila hai.
  • Downhill return H⁺ CF₀ se lumen se stroma ko facilitated diffusion karte hain, yaani protein route se apne gradient ki direction mein cross karte hain.
  • CF₁ par ATP formation Ek H⁺ ko follow karo: woh lumen se CF₀ cross karke stroma tak aata hai, aur coupled enzyme changes ATP production support karte hain.
  • Chemiosmosis ek picture par Phir H⁺ lumen se CF₀ ke through stroma lautte hain, aur CF₁ ke conformational changes ATP formation support karte hain.
142The ATP synthase enzyme consists of two parts: one called the CF₀ is embedded in the thylakoid membrane and forms a transmembrane channel that carries out facilitated diffusion of protons across the membrane.✓
  • ATP synthase ke parts Ab return route ko enlarge karo: ATP synthase ke do main parts CF₀ aur CF₁ hain.
  • ATP synthase ke parts CF₀ thylakoid membrane mein embedded proton channel hai; zero subscript wala yeh part membrane ko span karta hai.
  • Downhill return H⁺ CF₀ se lumen se stroma ko facilitated diffusion karte hain, yaani protein route se apne gradient ki direction mein cross karte hain.
142The other portion is called CF₁ and protrudes on the outer surface of the thylakoid membrane on the side that faces the stroma.✓
  • ATP synthase ke parts Ab return route ko enlarge karo: ATP synthase ke do main parts CF₀ aur CF₁ hain.
  • ATP synthase ke parts CF₁ stroma ki taraf nikla enzyme head hai, lumen ki taraf nahi.
  • Stroma mein supplies Aisa isliye hai kyunki ATP synthase ka working head stroma-facing hai aur sugar-making reactions stroma mein hoti hain, isliye membrane ki supplies wahin use hoti hain.
142The break down of the gradient provides enough energy to cause a conformational change in the CF₁ particle of the ATP synthase, which makes the enzyme synthesise several molecules of energy-packed ATP.✓
  • CF₁ par ATP formation Proton return se released gradient energy CF₁ ki conformation badalne ke kaam aati hai.
  • CF₁ par ATP formation Conformational change protein ki three-dimensional shape mein change hai.
  • CF₁ par ATP formation In changes se ATP synthase ADP aur inorganic phosphate, Pi, se ATP synthesis kar paata hai.
  • CF₁ par ATP formation Ek H⁺ ko follow karo: woh lumen se CF₀ cross karke stroma tak aata hai, aur coupled enzyme changes ATP production support karte hain.
  • CF₁ par ATP formation Iska seedha sa jawab hai ki ATP synthase repeated proton flow se kaam karta hai; single proton aur single ATP ko one-to-one token pair nahi banana chahiye.
  • Chemiosmosis ek picture par Phir H⁺ lumen se CF₀ ke through stroma lautte hain, aur CF₁ ke conformational changes ATP formation support karte hain.

142Along with the NADPH produced by the movement of electrons, the ATP will be used immediately in the biosynthetic reaction taking place in the stroma, responsible for fixing CO₂, and synthesis of sugars.✓
  • Stroma mein supplies Ab light-driven electron movement se mila NADPH aur chemiosmosis se bana ATP stroma ki biosynthetic reactions ko supply hote hain.
