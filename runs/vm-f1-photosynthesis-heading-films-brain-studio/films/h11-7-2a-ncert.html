143Let us now see how the Calvin pathway operates and where the sugar is synthesised.✓
  • Acceptor ka puzzle Ab RuBP se shuru karke carbon aur energy ka poora Calvin-cycle hisaab jodenge, aur dekhenge ki sugar banne ke saath acceptor dobara kaise milta hai.
  • Shared pathway aur teen jobs Calvin pathway wahi Calvin cycle hai, jo chloroplast ke stroma mein carbon se carbohydrate banane ka kaam karta hai.
143Let us at the outset understand very clearly that the Calvin pathway occurs in all photosynthetic plants; it does not matter whether they have C₃ or C₄ (or any other) pathways (Figure 11.8).✓
  • Shared pathway aur teen jobs Aksar log sochte hain ki yeh sirf C₃ plants mein hota hai, par asal mein C₄ aur doosre photosynthetic plants bhi Calvin pathway use karte hain.
  • Shared pathway aur teen jobs Initial fixation ka first stable product alag ho sakta hai, lekin Calvin pathway unka shared biosynthetic route hai.
143For ease of understanding, the Calvin cycle can be described under three stages: carboxylation, reduction and regeneration.✓
  • Acceptor ka puzzle Is process ke teen stages hain: carboxylation, reduction aur regeneration; inhe isi order mein follow karenge.
  • Shared pathway aur teen jobs Ab in teen stages ke alag jobs aur unke beech carbon ka connection pehchaano.
  • Shared pathway aur teen jobs Pehli job CO₂ ka carbon organic pathway mein laati hai, doosri fixed carbon ko carbohydrate ki direction mein badalti hai, aur teesri RuBP renew karti hai.
143Carboxylation – Carboxylation is the fixation of CO₂ into a stable organic intermediate.✓
  • Carboxylation Carboxylation mein CO₂ ka carbon RuBP se judkar stable organic intermediate, yaani aage ki reactions mein use hone wale carbon compound, mein pahunchta hai.
143Carboxylation is the most crucial step of the Calvin cycle where CO₂ is utilised for the carboxylation of RuBP.✓
  • Carboxylation Carboxylation mein CO₂ ka carbon RuBP se judkar stable organic intermediate, yaani aage ki reactions mein use hone wale carbon compound, mein pahunchta hai.
143This reaction is catalysed by the enzyme RuBP carboxylase which results in the formation of two molecules of 3-PGA.✓
  • Carboxylation Ek RuBP aur ek CO₂ follow karo: RuBP ke five aur CO₂ ka one carbon milkar six-carbon total dete hain, jisse two three-carbon 3-PGA molecules bante hain.
  • Carboxylation 3-PGA wahi pehle pehchaana PGA hai; iska naam 3-phosphoglyceric acid hai.
  • Carboxylation Yeh reaction RuBP carboxylase se catalysed hoti hai, yaani enzyme reaction ko tez chalne mein madad karta hai.
  • Three CO₂ ka ledger Three RuBP ke fifteen carbons aur three CO₂ ke three carbons milkar eighteen carbons dete hain: six 3-PGA molecules.
143Since this enzyme also has an oxygenation activity it would be more correct to call it RuBP carboxylase-oxygenase or RuBisCO.✓
  • Carboxylation Is enzyme ka fuller naam RuBP carboxylase-oxygenase, ya RuBisCO, kyun hai?
  • Carboxylation Aisa isliye hai kyunki yeh CO₂ wali carboxylation ke alawa O₂ ke saath RuBP ki alag reaction bhi catalyse kar sakta hai; isi ability ko oxygenation activity kehte hain.
144Reduction – These are a series of reactions that lead to the formation of glucose.✓
  • Reduction Ab reduction stage mein two 3-PGA molecules ko carbohydrate intermediate banne ki direction mein process karte hain.
  • Reduction Is stage ka carbohydrate intermediate triose phosphate hai: triose mein tri three batata hai, isliye har molecule mein three carbons hain.
  • Reduction Iska seedha sa jawab hai, glucose ko six carbons chahiye, isliye six CO₂ fixation aur six turns ka net carbon account ek glucose equivalent deta hai.
  • Three CO₂ ka ledger Reduction mein six ATP aur six NADPH use hokar six three-carbon triose phosphates ka pool banta hai; carbon total eighteen hi rehta hai.
  • Three CO₂ ka ledger Inmein one triose phosphate ka three-carbon equivalent net carbohydrate output hai, aur five triose phosphates ke fifteen carbons regeneration mein jaate hain.
  • Three CO₂ ka ledger Six CO₂ par yahi ledger double hota hai: two net triose-phosphate equivalents ke six carbons ek glucose equivalent provide karte hain.
144The steps involve utilisation of 2 molecules of ATP for phosphorylation and two of NADPH for reduction per CO₂ molecule fixed.✓
  • Reduction Har 3-PGA ke liye ek ATP phosphorylation support karta hai, aur ek NADPH reducing power deta hai.
  • Reduction Isliye per CO₂ molecule fixed, yaani ek CO₂ jodne ke account mein, reduction ka bill two ATP aur two NADPH hai.
  • Three CO₂ ka ledger Reduction mein six ATP aur six NADPH use hokar six three-carbon triose phosphates ka pool banta hai; carbon total eighteen hi rehta hai.
  • Mould aur RuBP Yahaan reusable mould RuBP ke acceptor role ko samjhata hai, product-making cost reduction ki ATP–NADPH demand hai, aur reset cost regeneration ka ATP hai.
144The fixation of six molecules of CO₂ and 6 turns of the cycle are required for the formation of one molecule of glucose from the pathway.✓
  • Reduction Iska seedha sa jawab hai, glucose ko six carbons chahiye, isliye six CO₂ fixation aur six turns ka net carbon account ek glucose equivalent deta hai.
  • Reduction Turn ka matlab yahaan ek CO₂ fixation ke saath cycle ka ek accounting round hai; har round ka direct product free glucose nahi hota.
  • Three CO₂ ka ledger Six CO₂ par yahi ledger double hota hai: two net triose-phosphate equivalents ke six carbons ek glucose equivalent provide karte hain.
144Regeneration – Regeneration of the CO₂ acceptor molecule RuBP is crucial if the cycle is to continue uninterrupted.✓
  • Regeneration Ab regeneration mein carbohydrate intermediates ka ek hissa rearrange hokar RuBP pool dobara banata hai.
  • Regeneration CO₂ acceptor wahi molecule hai jo incoming CO₂ receive karta hai; Calvin cycle mein yeh RuBP hai.
  • Regeneration Hota yeh hai ki RuBP renew na ho toh available acceptor pool ghat jaayega, aur ATP tatha NADPH hone ke bawajood next CO₂ fixation continue nahi hogi.
  • Regeneration Renewal cycle ko uninterrupted, yaani agla round acceptor ki kami se ruke bina, chalne deta hai.
  • Three CO₂ ka ledger Inmein one triose phosphate ka three-carbon equivalent net carbohydrate output hai, aur five triose phosphates ke fifteen carbons regeneration mein jaate hain.
  • Three CO₂ ka ledger Yeh fifteen carbons three five-carbon RuBP molecules ko restore karte hain, jisme three additional ATP lagte hain.
  • Three CO₂ ka ledger Hota yeh hai ki incoming three CO₂ ke three carbons ke barabar net output mila, jabki starting RuBP pool replenish ho gaya.
  • Mould aur RuBP Dekho, agla batch ready mould maangta hai, waise hi next CO₂ fixation available RuBP maangti hai; isliye regeneration ka energy bill total mein judega.
144The regeneration steps require one ATP for phosphorylation to form RuBP.✓
  • Regeneration RuBP formation ki phosphorylation ke liye har fixed CO₂ ke account mein ek additional ATP chahiye.
  • Three CO₂ ka ledger Yeh fifteen carbons three five-carbon RuBP molecules ko restore karte hain, jisme three additional ATP lagte hain.
  • Mould aur RuBP Yahaan reusable mould RuBP ke acceptor role ko samjhata hai, product-making cost reduction ki ATP–NADPH demand hai, aur reset cost regeneration ka ATP hai.
  • Mould aur RuBP Dekho, agla batch ready mould maangta hai, waise hi next CO₂ fixation available RuBP maangti hai; isliye regeneration ka energy bill total mein judega.
