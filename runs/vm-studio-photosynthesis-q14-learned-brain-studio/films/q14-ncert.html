q14 · Jama hue protons wapas niklein toh ATP kaise ban jaata hai?

12 / 12 NCERT lines film mein padhayi gayi · NCERT page 141, 142, 151

Film ka dhaancha: sawaal: 1 · pehle ki baat: 6 · concept: 15 · kahan kaam aata hai / asar: 5 · yaad rakhiye: 1

141Why are we so interested in the proton gradient?✓
  • Gradient ki energy Ab band thylakoid ki membrane ke paas hain; aakhir proton gradient mein itni dilchaspi kyun hai?
141This gradient is important because it is the breakdown of this gradient that leads to the synthesis of ATP.✓
  • Gradient ki energy Iska seedha sa jawab hai ki gradient ghatne ki energy se ATP banta hai, jaise baandh se chhootne wala paani kaam karwaata hai.
141The gradient is broken down due to the movement of protons across the membrane to the stroma through the transmembrane channel of the CF0 of the ATP synthase.✓
  • CF0 se waapsi Isme kaam aata hai membrane mein dhansa CF0, jiska transmembrane channel H+ ko lumen se stroma mein facilitated diffusion se laata hai.
142The ATP synthase enzyme consists of two parts: one called the CF0 is embedded in the thylakoid membrane and forms a transmembrane channel that carries out facilitated diffusion of protons across the membrane.✓
  • CF0 se waapsi Isme kaam aata hai membrane mein dhansa CF0, jiska transmembrane channel H+ ko lumen se stroma mein facilitated diffusion se laata hai.
142The other portion is called CF1 and protrudes on the outer surface of the thylakoid membrane on the side that faces the stroma.✓
  • CF0 se waapsi Isi enzyme ka doosra hissa CF1 bahari surface par stroma ki taraf nikla hai, jahan ab hum ATP banta dekhenge.
142The break down of the gradient provides enough energy to cause a conformational change in the CF1 particle of the ATP synthase, which makes the enzyme synthesise several molecules of energy-packed ATP.✓
  • Turning stalk, badalti shape Dekhiye, CF0 se H+ lautne par central stalk ghoomta hai, jisse CF1 ke catalytic hisse shape badalte hain; isi ko conformational change kehte hain.
  • Turning stalk, badalti shape Ab stroma ke ADP aur Pi isi catalytic site par milte hain, jahan enzyme energy-packed ATP banata hai.
  • Turning stalk, badalti shape Yeh kaam baar-baar hota hai, jisse ADP aur Pi se ATP ke several molecules bante hain, sirf ek nahi.
142Chemiosmosis requires a membrane, a proton pump, a proton gradient and ATP synthase.✓
  • Membrane leak Isliye chemiosmosis ko intact membrane, proton pump, proton gradient aur ATP synthase, chaaron chahiye; pump ka jama kiya H+ leak se bachna chahiye.
142Energy is used to pump protons across a membrane, to create a gradient or a high concentration of protons within the thylakoid lumen.✓
  • Gradient ki energy Dekhiye, proton pump energy lagaakar H+ ko stroma se lumen mein jama karta hai, jisse lumen mein high concentration aur membrane ke across proton gradient banta hai.
142ATP synthase has a channel that allows diffusion of protons back across the membrane; this releases enough energy to activate ATP synthase enzyme that catalyses the formation of ATP.✓
  • Turning stalk, badalti shape Dekhiye, CF0 se H+ lautne par central stalk ghoomta hai, jisse CF1 ke catalytic hisse shape badalte hain; isi ko conformational change kehte hain.
  • Turning stalk, badalti shape Ab stroma ke ADP aur Pi isi catalytic site par milte hain, jahan enzyme energy-packed ATP banata hai.
142Along with the NADPH produced by the movement of electrons, the ATP will be used immediately in the biosynthetic reaction taking place in the stroma, responsible for fixing CO2, and synthesis of sugars.✓
  • Stroma ko supply Iska seedha sa jawab hai ki ATP turant stroma ki biosynthetic reactions ko energy supply karta hai.
  • Stroma ko supply Saath hi electron movement se bana NADPH bhi yahin pahunchta hai, jo synthesis ke liye reducing power deta hai; ab in enzymes ke paas chalte hain.
  • Energy se sugar tak Dekhiye, CO2 yahan fix hota hai, aur ATP-NADPH ki madad se aage sugars banti hain, jisse pakdi hui energy khane mein judti hai.
151During this process a proton gradient is created across the membrane of the thylakoid.✓
  • Gradient ki energy Dekhiye, proton pump energy lagaakar H+ ko stroma se lumen mein jama karta hai, jisse lumen mein high concentration aur membrane ke across proton gradient banta hai.
151The breakdown of the protons gradient due to movement through the F0 part of the ATPase enzyme releases enough energy for synthesis of ATP.✓
  • CF0 se waapsi Jaise paani dhalan par behta hai, waise H+ electrochemical gradient ke saath lautte hain, jisse ATP synthesis ki energy milti hai; F0 bhi isi hisse ka naam hai.

NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • pehle ki baat Aaiye jaante hain. Pehle yaad kijiye ki is patte mein aati light ki energy photosynthesis ko chalati hai.
    Light energy ka prerequisite recall hai.
  • pehle ki baat Ab hum leaf ki mesophyll cell ke andar hain, jiske kinaare par chloroplasts maujood hain.
    Cell se chloroplast tak spatial context dete hain.
  • pehle ki baat Dekhiye, light is chloroplast tak pahunchti hai; aaiye, isi ke andar ATP banne ki jagah ko paas se samajhte hain.
    Light aur organelle ka spatial bridge hai.
  • pehle ki baat Chloroplast ke andar yeh thylakoid band chapti thaili hai, jiske andar lumen aur bahar fluid stroma hai.
    Band thaili ki analogy se compartments samjhate hain.
  • pehle ki baat Light ki energy se chalne wala kaam isi thylakoid membrane par hota hai; ab iski deewar ke paas chalte hain.
    Light reactions ki location ka bridge hai.
  • concept Iska seedha sa jawab hai ki gradient ghatne ki energy se ATP banta hai, jaise baandh se chhootne wala paani kaam karwaata hai.
    Baandh se stored energy samjhate hain.
  • concept Jaise paani dhalan par behta hai, waise H+ electrochemical gradient ke saath lautte hain, jisse ATP synthesis ki energy milti hai; F0 bhi isi hisse ka naam hai.
    Electrochemical downhill flow ko paani ki dhalan se samjhate hain.
  • concept Dekhiye, CF0 se H+ lautne par central stalk ghoomta hai, jisse CF1 ke catalytic hisse shape badalte hain; isi ko conformational change kehte hain.
    Stalk rotation ko catalytic conformational change se jodte hain.
  • concept Ab stroma ke ADP aur Pi isi catalytic site par milte hain, jahan enzyme energy-packed ATP banata hai.
    ADP aur inorganic phosphate ka catalytic meeting dikhate hain.
  • concept Ab paas ki membrane mein yeh khula leak dekhiye; agar H+ yahin se nikal jaayein toh kya hoga?
    Leak ka counterfactual sawaal hai.
  • concept Aisa hone par H+ gap se stroma mein nikalte hain, jisse gradient ki energy ATP synthase ko chalaaye bina waste hoti hai, jaise baandh mein daraar pad jaaye.
    Baandh ki daraar se gradient dissipation aur synthase bypass samjhate hain.
  • concept Isliye chemiosmosis ko intact membrane, proton pump, proton gradient aur ATP synthase, chaaron chahiye; pump ka jama kiya H+ leak se bachna chahiye.
    Intact membrane ki functional zaroorat samjhate hain.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Saath hi electron movement se bana NADPH bhi yahin pahunchta hai, jo synthesis ke liye reducing power deta hai; ab in enzymes ke paas chalte hain.
    ATP ki energy aur NADPH ki reducing power alag karte hain.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Dekhiye, CO2 yahan fix hota hai, aur ATP-NADPH ki madad se aage sugars banti hain, jisse pakdi hui energy khane mein judti hai.
    Sugar mein energy storage ka basic outcome dikhate hain; cycle ke detailed steps nahi.
