  • Teesra kaam Yeh enzyme NADP reductase hai, aur yeh membrane ke bahar, stroma wale chehre par baitha hai.
141Along with electrons that come from the acceptor of electrons of PS I, protons are necessary for the reduction of NADP⁺ to NADPH+ H⁺.✓
  • Teesra kaam PS I se uchhla electron uske acceptor se Fd, yaani ferredoxin, ke paas jaata hai.
  • Teesra kaam Fd use aage ek enzyme tak pahunchata hai.
  • Reduction NADP⁺ electron lekar NADPH banta hai, isliye yeh NADP⁺ ka reduction hai.
  • Reduction Is reduction ke liye sirf electron kaafi nahi.
  • Reduction Saath mein H⁺ bhi chahiye, aur yeh H⁺ paas ke stroma se hi liye jaate hain.
  • Bahar kharch Yeh NADPH mein judkar wahin bahar kharch ho gaye.
141These protons are also removed from the stroma.✓
  • Reduction Saath mein H⁺ bhi chahiye, aur yeh H⁺ paas ke stroma se hi liye jaate hain.
  • Bahar kharch Yeh H⁺ andar lumen mein nahi pahunche.
  • Bahar kharch Yeh NADPH mein judkar wahin bahar kharch ho gaye.

141Hence, within the chloroplast, protons in the stroma decrease in number, while in the lumen there is accumulation of protons.✓
  • Bahar kharch Phir bhi farq badha, kyunki bahar se H⁺ kam hue.
  • Bahar kharch Andar utne hi rahe aur bahar kam ho gaye, toh dhalaan aur oonchi hui.

141This creates a proton gradient across the thylakoid membrane as well as a measurable decrease in pH in the lumen.✓

NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • concept Teesra kaam raaste ke aakhir mein hai, jahan NADP⁺ se NADPH banta hai.
    Teesre cause ka product preview deta hai.
  • concept PS I se uchhla electron uske acceptor se Fd, yaani ferredoxin, ke paas jaata hai.
    Fd tak electron ka raasta kholta hai.
  • concept Fd use aage ek enzyme tak pahunchata hai.
    Fd electron aage deta hai.
  • concept Figure mein ise FNR likha hai.
    FNR abbreviation pehchaanata hai.
  • pehle ki baat Yaad karo, electron lena reduction kehlata hai.
    Reduction ka matlab electron gain yaad dilata hai.
  • concept NADP⁺ electron lekar NADPH banta hai, isliye yeh NADP⁺ ka reduction hai.
    Reduction ko NADP⁺ par apply karta hai; H⁺ ki requirement agle lines mein khulti hai.
  • concept Toh socho, kya yeh H⁺ andar lumen mein pahunche?
    Consumption aur transport ka farq poochhta hai.
  • concept Yeh H⁺ andar lumen mein nahi pahunche.
    H⁺ membrane cross nahi karte.
  • concept Andar utne hi rahe aur bahar kam ho gaye, toh dhalaan aur oonchi hui.
    Bahar ki kami bhi gradient badhaati hai.
  • concept Baandh mein socho, aage ki nadi se koi paani nikal le.
    Nadi ki nikaasi stromal H⁺ consumption se map hoti hai.
  • concept Jheel utni hi bhari hai, par aage ka paani aur neeche chala gaya.
    Unchanged jheel aur lower nadi ka farq dikhata hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Ab hum teeno kaam ek saath jodte hain.
    Teen causes ko ek membrane par jodta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Lumen mein H⁺ do raaston se aaye: paani ke tootne se, aur PQ ke laane se.
    Paani splitting aur PQ lumen ke do sources hain.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Stroma se H⁺ do raaston se gaye: PQ ne uthaaye, aur NADPH ne kharch kiye.
    Stromal depletion ke dono causes jodta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Baandh ke peeche jheel bharti ja rahi hai, aur aage ki nadi utarti ja rahi hai.
    Accumulation aur depletion ko jheel aur nadi se map karta hai.
  • pehle ki baat Ab pH yaad karo.
    Analogy se pH recall ki taraf transition hai.
  • pehle ki baat Kisi ghol mein H⁺ jitne zyada, woh utna acidic, aur uska pH utna kam.
    H⁺ aur pH ka ulta sambandh yaad dilata hai.
  • concept Toh batao, H⁺ bharne par lumen ka pH badhega ya ghatega?
    pH relationship apply karne ka sawaal hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Roshni mein lumen ka pH lagbhag 5 tak aa jaata hai, jabki stroma lagbhag 8 par rehta hai.
