  • Electron aur ADP alag ATP bana kaise, yahi aaj ka sawaal hai: chloroplast mein ATP asal mein banta kaise hai.
140The chemiosmotic hypothesis has been put forward to explain the mechanism.✓
  • Mitchell ka vichaar Mitchell ke vichaar ka naam hai chemiosmotic hypothesis.

140Like in respiration, in photosynthesis too, ATP synthesis is linked to development of a proton gradient across a membrane.✓
  • Proton gradient Membrane ke ek taraf H⁺ zyada aur doosri taraf kam hon, toh H⁺ ki is dhalaan ko proton gradient kehte hain; gradient ka matlab hi dhalaan hai.
  • Proton gradient ATP banna isi dhalaan ke banne se juda hai: pehle dhalaan banti hai, phir H⁺ dhalaan se neeche bahte hain, aur us bahav se ATP banta hai.
  • Proton gradient Dhalaan nahi, toh ATP nahi.
  • Respiration mein bhi Yahi tareeka aap aage respiration wale chapter mein mitochondria mein bhi padhoge.
  • Tareeka ek, jagah alag Chloroplast mein H⁺ thylakoid ke andar, lumen mein, jama hote hain; mitochondria mein do membranes ke beech, intermembrane space mein.

  • Jagah pakki karein Har thaili ki deewar thylakoid membrane hai.

140There is one difference though, here the proton accumulation is towards the inside of the membrane, i.e., in the lumen.✓
  • Jagah pakki karein Thaili ke andar ki band jagah lumen hai, aur thaili ke bahar, chloroplast ka gaadha dravya, stroma hai.

140In respiration, protons accumulate in the intermembrane space of the mitochondria when electrons move through the ETS (Chapter 12).✓
