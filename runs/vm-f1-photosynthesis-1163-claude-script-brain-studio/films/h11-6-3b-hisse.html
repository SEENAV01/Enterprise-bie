#LineKya hilta hai

2Beech mein thylakoid ki band membrane hai.—
3Andar ki jagah par likha hai lumen, high H⁺; bahar likha hai stroma, low H⁺.—
4Yaani andar H⁺ zyada, bahar kam.—

8Ab electron ke raaste par ungli rakh kar chalo: PS II se PQ, yaani plastoquinone; phir cytochrome b6f; phir PC, yaani plastocyanin; phir PS I.cytochrome b6f molecule ko pakadkar us paar chhodta hai, savaar e-

10Yeh wahi Z scheme ka raasta hai jo humne pehle padha tha.—
