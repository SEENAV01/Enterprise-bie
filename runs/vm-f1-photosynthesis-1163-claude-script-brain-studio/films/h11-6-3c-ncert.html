140(a) Since splitting of the water molecule takes place on the inner side of the membrane, the protons or hydrogen ions that are produced by the splitting of water accumulate within the lumen of the thylakoids.✓
  • Andar paani tootna Paani PS II ke paas toot-ta hai, aur yeh jagah membrane ke andar wale chehre par hai, yaani lumen ki taraf.
  • Andar paani tootna Pichhli film mein humne dekha tha: paani ke do molecule toot kar chaar electron, chaar H⁺ aur ek O₂ dete hain.
  • Andar paani tootna Tootna andar ki taraf hota hai, isliye yeh chaar H⁺, yaani protons ya hydrogen ions, bahar nahi jaate.
  • Andar paani tootna Woh seedhe lumen mein hi chhoot jaate hain.

141(b) As electrons move through the photosystems, protons are transported across the membrane.✓
  • Electron ka raasta Doosra kaam electron ke raaste mein chhupa hai.

141This happens because the primary accepter of electron which is located towards the outer side of the membrane transfers its electron not to an electron carrier but to an H carrier.✓
  • Do carriers ka farq Inhe H carrier kehte hain, yaani hydrogen carrier.

141Hence, this molecule removes a proton from the stroma while transporting an electron.✓
  • Primary acceptor se H carrier H carrier ko electron ke saath H⁺ bhi chahiye, aur woh bahar wale chehre par hai.
