### Part c19.3 · cause_effect
_frame: process: Explain compact packing and the active cell-production role of meristems. · how: Connect absent between-cell gaps to close packing, and repeated division to new building units. · when: During active growth. · conditions: Between-cell spaces differ from intracellular vacuoles; division is the principal role, not the only cellular activity. · atmosphere: Picture a tightly packed workshop supplying new cells to a growing plant._

**b20 · explain · step 2: the cause** — Intercellular spaces यानी cells के बीच खाली जगह absent है; इसलिए cells पास-पास लगे हैं।

**b22 · explain · step 4: the evidence** — Root tip में देखो: सटे cells packing दिखाते हैं; बनते नए cells उनकी activity का संकेत हैं।
