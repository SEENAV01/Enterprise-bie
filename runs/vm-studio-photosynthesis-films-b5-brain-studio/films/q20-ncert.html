147To understand photorespiration we have to know a little bit more about the first step of the Calvin pathway – the first CO2 fixation step.✓
  • Leaf ke andar Photorespiration samajhne ke liye Calvin pathway ka pehla CO2 fixation step jaanana zaroori hai; chaliye isi chloroplast ke andar chalte hain.
147This is the reaction where RuBP combines with CO2 to form 2 molecules of 3PGA, that is catalysed by RuBisCO.✓
  • Sahi gas ka milan Dekhiye, RuBisCO par RuBP aur CO2 milte hain, jisse do molecules 3-PGA bante hain; yahi pehla CO2 fixation step hai.
147RuBisCo RuBP+CO2 →2× 3PGA RuBisCO that is the most abundant enzyme in the world (Do you wonder why?) is characterised by the fact that its active site can bind to both CO and O – hence the name.✓
  • Sahi gas ka milan Stroma mein yeh RuBisCO duniya ka most abundant enzyme hai; par yeh CO2 ko kis molecule se jodta hai?

147Here the RuBP instead of being converted to 2 molecules of PGA binds with O2 to form one molecule of phosphoglycerate and phosphoglycolate (2 Carbon) in a pathway called photorespiration.✓
  • Oxygenation ka product Dekhiye, RuBP aur O2 se do 3-PGA nahi, ek 3-PGA, yaani phosphoglycerate, aur ek 2-carbon phosphoglycolate bante hain.
147In the photorespiratory pathway, there is neither synthesis of sugars, nor of ATP.✓

147In C4 plants photorespiration does not occur.✓
  • Carboxylation ko faayda 3-PGA aage badhta hai; C4 mein photorespiration na ke barabar hone se productivity aur yield better hoti hain, aur yeh plants higher temperatures bhi tolerate karte hain.
147This is because they have a mechanism that increases the concentration of CO2 at the enzyme site.✓
  • C4 ka bachav Ab C4 maize leaf ke andar dekhiye; mesophyll ke paas bundle sheath cells hain, jahan RuBisCO ke liye CO2 concentrate hoti hai.
  • CO2 parcel khulta hai Yeh CO2 RuBisCO ke paas pahunchti hai; jaise sahi customers ki bheed se doosre ko mauka kam milta hai, waise O2 ki binding ghatati hai.
147This takes place when the C4 acid from the mesophyll is broken down in the bundle sheath cells to release CO2 – this results in increasing the intracellular concentration of CO2.✓
  • C4 ka bachav Isme C4 acid kaam aata hai; mesophyll se malate jaisa C4 acid bundle sheath tak carbon ka parcel laata hai.
  • CO2 parcel khulta hai Dekhiye, malate tootkar pyruvate aur CO2 deta hai, jisse bundle sheath ke andar CO2 concentration badhti hai.
147In turn, this ensures that the RuBisCO functions as a carboxylase minimising the oxygenase activity.✓
  • CO2 parcel khulta hai Yeh CO2 RuBisCO ke paas pahunchti hai; jaise sahi customers ki bheed se doosre ko mauka kam milta hai, waise O2 ki binding ghatati hai.
  • Carboxylation ko faayda Dekhiye, CO2 aur RuBP se do 3-PGA bante hain; carboxylase activity badhti hai, jabki oxygenase activity aur phosphoglycolate formation ghatati hain.
