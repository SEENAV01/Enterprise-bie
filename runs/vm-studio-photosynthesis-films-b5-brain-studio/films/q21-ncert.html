NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • pehle ki baat Aaiye jaante hain; pehle yaad karein ki dono hawa ka CO2 lekar sugar banate hain.
    Dono pathways ka common carbon source recall karaya.
  • concept Iska seedha sa jawab hai Kranz anatomy, jismein bundle sheath ki mala ko mesophyll gher leta hai.
    Kranz ko mala ki everyday tasveer se joda.
  • concept Yeh relay race jaisi do-cell partnership hai, teen fixing cell types nahi; ab acceptor aur enzymes ke paas chalte hain.
    Do-cell division of labour ko relay race se samjhaya.
  • concept C3 mein RuBisCO par CO2 aur paanch-carbon RuBP milte hain, jisse teen-carbon PGA ke do molecules bante hain.
    PGA ke do molecules ki stoichiometry recall karayi.
  • concept C4 mein teen-carbon PEP initial acceptor hai; mesophyll ki PEPcase bicarbonate lekar chaar-carbon OAA banati hai.
    PEPcase ka actual inorganic-carbon substrate bicarbonate bataya.
  • concept Isliye C3 aur C4 naam pehle stable product ke carbon gin kar milte hain; ab C4 ke agle carbon-delivery point ko dekhein.
    Pathway naam ko first stable product se joda.
  • pehle ki baat Nahi; OAA se bana malate yahan CO2 chhodta hai aur pyruvate bachta hai, jisse RuBisCO ke paas carbon ki supply badhti hai.
    C4 carbon-concentrating mechanism ka basic delivery step dikhaya.
  • concept Comparison mein initial PEPcase ke liye C4 mein yes aur C3 mein no bharte hain; C3 mein doosre kaamon ke liye yeh enzyme ho sakta hai.
    C3 mein PEPcase ki absolute absence aur pathway role ka farq saaf kiya.
  • concept Aisa isliye hai kyunki O2 ko mauka milta hai; RuBisCO par uska pahunchna photorespiration aur fixed carbon ki haani badhata hai.
    RuBisCO par oxygen competition recall karaya.
  • concept C3 mein high CO2 dene par yeh loss ghat sakta hai, jaise bheed mein apne customer ki sankhya badha dein.
    Substrate competition ke liye customer analogy.
