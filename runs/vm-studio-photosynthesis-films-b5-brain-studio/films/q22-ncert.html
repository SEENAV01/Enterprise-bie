14911.10 FACTORS AFFECTING PHOTOSYNTHESIS An understanding of the factors that affect photosynthesis is necessary.✓
  • Factory ki raftaar Is photosynthesis ki rate crop plants samet paudhon ki yield tay karne mein bahut important hai, isliye iske factors samajhna zaroori hai.
149The rate of photosynthesis is very important in determining the yield of plants including crop plants.✓
  • Factory ki raftaar Is photosynthesis ki rate crop plants samet paudhon ki yield tay karne mein bahut important hai, isliye iske factors samajhna zaroori hai.
149Photosynthesis is under the influence of several factors, both internal (plant) and external.✓
  • Patte ki apni taiyaari Ab isi patte ke paas hain; photosynthesis par internal, yaani plant ke apne factors, aur external factors dono asar karte hain.
149The plant factors include the number, size, age and orientation of leaves, mesophyll cells and chloroplasts, internal CO2 concentration and the amount of chlorophyll.✓
  • Patte ki apni taiyaari Leaves ki number, size, age aur orientation rate ko affect karti hain; orientation ko solar panel ke rukh jaisa samajhiye.
  • Andar aur baahar ke factors Patte ke andar mesophyll cells aur unke chloroplasts bhi rate ko affect karte hain, saath hi internal CO2 concentration aur chlorophyll ki amount bhi.
149The plant or internal factors are dependent on the genetic predisposition and the growth of the plant.✓
  • Patte ki apni taiyaari Yeh internal factors genetic predisposition, yaani viraasat mein mili pravritti, aur plant ki growth par depend karte hain; ab patte ke andar chalte hain.
149The external factors would include the availability of sunlight, temperature, CO2 concentration and water.✓
  • Andar aur baahar ke factors Baahar se sunlight ki availability, temperature, CO2 concentration aur water is kaam par asar daalte hain.
149As a plant photosynthesises, all these factors will simultaneously affect its rate.✓
  • Andar aur baahar ke factors Photosynthesis ke dauraan yeh saare factors simultaneously, yaani ek hi samay rate ko affect karte hain; ab isi chloroplast mein chalte hain.
149Hence, though several factors interact and simultaneously affect photosynthesis or CO2 fixation, usually one factor is the major cause or is the one that limits the rate.✓
  • Sabse tang darwaaza Iska seedha sa jawab hai ki photosynthesis, ya CO2 fixation, mein aam taur par ek major limiting factor hota hai, jo suboptimal level par milta hai.
149Hence, at any point the rate will be determined by the factor available at sub-optimal levels.✓
  • Sabse tang darwaaza Iska seedha sa jawab hai ki photosynthesis, ya CO2 fixation, mein aam taur par ek major limiting factor hota hai, jo suboptimal level par milta hai.
149When several factors affect any [bio] chemical process, Blackman’s (1905) Law of Limiting Factors comes into effect.✓
  • Sabse tang darwaaza Kai factors wale biochemical process ke liye yahi Blackman ka 1905 Law of Limiting Factors hai; factory ka sabse tang darwaaza poori line ko slow karta hai.
149This states the following: If a chemical process is affected by more than one factor, then its rate will be determined by the factor which is nearest to its minimal value: it is the factor which directly affects the process if its quantity is changed.✓
  • Thand mein atki factory Ab extra light aur CO2 dete hain, lekin output nahi badhta, kyunki kami inki nahi, temperature ki hai.
  • Raftaar se growth tak Isi stromal jagah ko aur paas se dekhiye; law mein apne minimum ke sabse kareeb factor rate tay karta hai, aur usi ki quantity badalne se rate seedhe badalti hai.
149For example, despite the presence of a green leaf and optimal light and CO2 conditions, the plant may not photosynthesise if the temperature is very low.✓
  • Thand mein atki factory Aisa ho sakta hai ki photosynthesis ho hi na paaye; CO2 pahunchta hai, par bahut low temperature par fixation aage nahi badhti.
  • Thand mein atki factory Ab extra light aur CO2 dete hain, lekin output nahi badhta, kyunki kami inki nahi, temperature ki hai.
