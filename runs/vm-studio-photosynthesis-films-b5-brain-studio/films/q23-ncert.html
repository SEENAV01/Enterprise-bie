NCERT se bahar ki baat (BIE ne samjhane ke liye joda)

  • concept Aaiye jaante hain: light quality uska rang hai, intensity uski teevrata hai, aur exposure duration batata hai ki roshni kitni der milti hai.
    Teen factors ka seedha arth kholta hai.
  • pehle ki baat Jaise bulb ka rang, brightness aur jalne ka samay alag hain, waise yeh teen factors bhi ek nahi hain.
    Bulb ki rozmarra misaal se teen variables alag karta hai.
  • pehle ki baat Patte ke andar yeh mesophyll cell hai, jiske chloroplast light ki energy se CO2 ko organic food mein jodne ka kaam chalate hain.
    Photosynthesis mein energy aur carbon ke alag roles ka prerequisite.
  • concept Iske liye temperature optimal rakhte hain aur baaki conditions samaan rakhkar incident light badhate hain, jisse patta photosynthesis kar sake.
    Light ka effect alag jaanne ke liye controlled comparison batata hai.
  • concept Lekin higher intensity par doosre factors limiting ho jaate hain, isliye rate aur nahi badhti, jaise samaan kam ho toh extra workers bhi rukte hain.
    Workers aur supply ki misaal se bottleneck samjhata hai.
  • pehle ki baat Ab thylakoid membrane ke andar emerald chlorophyll light pakadta hai; par bahut excessive light milti rahe toh kya hoga?
    Pigment ko uske membrane-protein context mein pehchaan deta hai.
  • concept Aisa hone par ek point ke baad chlorophyll breakdown hota hai, jisse pigment ke tukde bante hain aur photosynthesis ghat sakti hai.
    Breakdown ko pigment ke tukdon se drishya roop deta hai.
  • kahan kaam aata hai / asar Isliye shade ke patte ko sahi roshni dena kaam aa sakta hai, lekin saturated patte par aur dhoop daalna food-making rate badhane ki guarantee nahi hai.
    Plant lighting ka practical use: light-limited aur saturated patte mein alag response.
  • concept B par limitation light se CO2 supply jaise doosre factors ki taraf shift hoti hai.
    B ko transition aur CO2 supply ko possible next bottleneck batata hai.
  • concept Curve par C saturation represent karta hai; excessive exposure se chlorophyll damage ho toh rate girti hai.
    Excess exposure ki girawat ko plateau se alag branch mein dikhata hai.
  • concept Curve par D zero-light respiratory loss represent karta hai, kyunki respiration CO2 chhodti hai.
    D ki negative net rate ko respiratory CO2 loss se samjhata hai.
