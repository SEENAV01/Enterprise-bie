11.10.4 Water · 1 gyan-bindu · lagbhag 2 min
samjhana11.10.4-k1
Water light reaction ka reactant hai; water splitting mein uska role hum samajh chuke hain. Lekin environmental factor ke roop mein water ka main effect sirf splitting ke liye substrate ki kami nahi, plant ki overall condition ke through hota hai. Water stress matlab plant ki zaroorat ke comparison mein paani kam padna. Iske do connected effects dekho. Pehla, stomata close hote hain; stomata leaf ke gas-exchange pores hain, jinka stomatal pore aur guard cells wala arrangement opening mein dekha tha. In pores se water vapour bhi nikalti hai; closure further water loss kam karta hai, lekin CO₂ entry bhi restrict karta hai. CO₂ availability ghatne par carbon fixation aur photosynthetic rate kam ho sakti hai. Doosra, water stress se leaves wilt karti hain: paani ki kami se dheeli padkar jhuk jaati hain. Cells ke andar water pressure leaf ko firm rakhne mein help karta hai; paani kam hone par yeh support ghatta hai. Wilting se leaf ki effectively exposed surface area kam hoti hai; water stress metabolic activity bhi reduce karta hai. Metabolic activity matlab cells ke andar chalne wali chemical reactions ka kaam. Toh dono branches jodo: stomatal closure CO₂ supply ghataata hai, aur wilting ke saath reduced surface area aur metabolic activity leaf ki functioning ghataate hain. Isliye water shortage ka photosynthesis par major effect indirect hai, water ke reactant hone ke bawajood.

p020-i11 Even though water is one of the reactants in the light reaction, the effect of water as a factor is more through its effect on the plant, rather than directly on photosynthesis. Water stress causes the stomata to close hence reducing the CO₂ availability. Besides, water stress also makes leaves wilt, thus, reducing the surface area of the leaves and their metabolic activity as well.
