# Jode: what went wrong, beside the gold standard

Each pair: a line BIE wrote (run, heading 11.6.3 unless named) and how the gold standard says the same, then the lesson.
All from NCERT Class 11 Biology, Chapter 11. Use them to see the way, never as text to copy into another chapter.

## 1. The answer before the child is ready

- **Codex s12, line 3:** "Electron transport ki energy ATP banane tak H⁺ ka fark banaakar pahunchti hai; unka lautna ATP
  synthesis chalaata hai."
- **Gold:** "ATP bana kaise, yahi aaj ka sawaal hai: chloroplast mein ATP asal mein banta kaise hai." Then the wrong idea,
  Mitchell's story, the name, the places, the balloon, H⁺, agarbatti, the slope, and only then the gradient.
- **Lesson:** the puzzle is held open; every step earns the next. The answer comes when the child can follow it.

## 2. Two facts in one sentence

- **Codex s12:** "H⁺ positive hydrogen ion hai; ADP aur inorganic phosphate se ATP banane mein energy lagti hai."
- **Gold:** "Ab H⁺ ko pehchano. Hydrogen ke atom mein ek proton aur ek electron hota hai. Electron nikal jaaye, toh bachta
  hai sirf ek proton, jis par positive charge hai." / "Isliye H⁺ ko hydrogen ion bhi kehte hain aur proton bhi; teeno naam
  ek hi cheez ke hain."
- **Lesson:** one new step per sentence; a fact gets as many sentences as a child needs (gold: about 2.4 per fact; BIE's
  runs 1.3 to 1.8).

## 3. A term defined, not built

- **Codex s12:** "Membrane ke dono taraf H⁺ ki concentration aur electrical haalat ke fark ko proton gradient kehte hain."
  ("concentration" is never opened.)
- **Gold:** "Class 9 mein aapne padha tha: koi cheez jahan zyada hoti hai, wahan se apne-aap kam wali jagah ki taraf
  failti hai, jaise kamre mein agarbatti ki khushboo." / "Yeh dhalaan jaisa hai: gend dhalaan par apne-aap neeche
  ludhakti hai, par upar chadhaane mein zor lagta hai." / "Membrane ke ek taraf H⁺ zyada aur doosri taraf kam hon, toh H⁺
  ki is dhalaan ko proton gradient kehte hain; gradient ka matlab hi dhalaan hai."
- **Lesson:** known thing → picture → the idea in plain words → "isi ko ... kehte hain". Never the term first.

## 4. Definitions in a row

- **Codex s12, point k1:** 7 of 20 lines are "term" lines, most back to back (gradient, proton gradient, electrochemical,
  hypothesis, chemiosmotic, chemiosmotic hypothesis, intermembrane space).
- **Gold c2:** 4 term lines in 23, each after its idea was built.
- **Lesson:** a lesson, not a glossary.

## 5. The book's line said again in English

- **s6:** "Proton return se released gradient energy CF₁ ki conformation badalne ke kaam aati hai."
- **Navin's "bilkul yahi tha" (Claude's example):** H⁺ held like water behind a dam; its energy reaches CF₁; CF₁'s shape
  changes like a fist opening and closing; ADP and Pᵢ go in, are pressed together, ATP comes out; again and again, so
  several ATP.
- **Lesson:** teach what the line means with a picture and a why; keep the book's words as the named end of it.

## 6. Office words

- **s6/s8:** "Pehla contribution water splitting ka hai"; "electron handover"; "ledger NADP⁺ + 2e⁻ + H⁺ → NADPH hai";
  "do-water batch ka ledger jodo".
- **A teacher says:** kaam, aage dena, ginti, "do paani ke molecule se shuru karke ginte hain".
- **Lesson:** everyday words in Hindi. The rule word "worked ledger" once grew into these lines, so watch your own
  wording too.

## 7. A puzzle that holds its answer

- **s9 goal:** "Electron transport ki energy ATP banane wali machine tak proton ke fark ke zariye kaise pahunchti hai?"
- **Gold goal:** "Dono raaston mein kisi bhi electron ne ADP ko chhua tak nahi; toh ATP bana kaise?"
- **Lesson:** the question names nothing the heading will teach.

## 8. Figure labels joined and said aloud

- **s11:** "Thylakoid membrane band thaili ki boundary hai; Stroma (low H⁺); Stroma labels uske bahar ke kam-H⁺ wale fluid
  ko dikhate hain."
- **Gold c3:** "Beech mein thylakoid ki band membrane hai. Andar ki jagah par likha hai lumen, high H⁺; bahar likha hai
  stroma, low H⁺. Yaani andar H⁺ zyada, bahar kam."
- **Lesson:** one label at a time, with a finger on it, said as a sentence.

## 9. Cut short to keep a rule (Claude's own first try, 25 Nov)

- Claude's first c2–c3 taught 8 of the 22 things fully, 8 half and 6 not at all: what earlier headings had taught was cut
  as "known", and book words (cytochrome b6f, ferredoxin, electrochemical) were dropped to keep English low. Navin:
  "concept poora padhaya nhi gaya".
- **Lesson:** recall what is needed here, keep every book word and open it. A rule never outranks teaching the book.

## 10. Patching after checks (a fresh Claude through BIE, 26 Nov)

- Its lesson had 235 sentences. After BIE's fix rounds it had 344; one round pushed the average to 16.8 words; lines like
  "Is badlav se enzyme energy-packed, yaani energy se bhare, ATP ke several molecules bana deta hai. Several yaani ek
  nahi, kai molecules." came in to satisfy "keep the book's 'several'".
- **Lesson:** weigh each finding; fix by rewording, not adding; if a block grows a lot, write it again as one flow.

## What the fresh Claude did well (keep doing this)

- "Yahan H⁺ kam wali jagah stroma se zyada wali jagah lumen mein ja raha hai, jo dhalaan ke ulta hai. Toh is ulte safar
  ki energy kahan se aati hai?": a question at a real turn, from what the child has just seen.
- Counting H⁺ for two water molecules (eight into the lumen, six out of the stroma) and asking why the counts differ.
- "Hypothesis" tied to an idea the child met earlier in the chapter (Priestley's), instead of a dictionary line.
