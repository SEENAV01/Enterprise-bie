Readable words in BIE drawings (branch sloman-readable2) - notes, not committed

Rule: draw.mjs READ_PX = 36; readFit/readTx/txLines/wrap2 helpers. A word never goes under 36 px at the drawing's w:
it wraps to two lines (balanced split), then keeps 36 and sticks out (graph line names start at the line's end, outside
the plot). "hard" rooms (calendar band, podium step, pillar card/beam) shrink after wrapping so box_rules.min_readable_w
/ bk.py fit still report the w they need.

graph: fs = max(0.055h, 36), ticks max(0.8fs, 36), axis ends max(0.7fs, 36); x tick names wrap under their tick and push
  the x label down; y tick names right-aligned, push the y label out; short y label ("₹", "P") drawn upright; line name
  = text before the LAST ':' (":" inside a name keeps the line; areas too); every other label floored and grows upward
  (dots, arrows, segs, path, along) or downward (gaps). Before: ticks 32.7 px, "Demand curve" 25.8 px at w 1200.
fork: pills and result boxes grow to hold wrapped words (before 19.9-24.5 px at w 1100; after >= 40 px).
paper: amount/footer accepted as value/note (manifest said "amount, footer note").
noteBundle: band moved to the left end, value clear.
calendar: heading fits/wraps, band grows, day grid moved under the heading (it used to cover the heading).
podium: names floored; no room under the rank number -> the name stands on top of its step; rank 3 number floored.
vehicle plane: default w 760 (was 1000), bigger plate (label 44.8 px).
slip ticket: rows drawn in a strip under the ticket face.
agents: fs = max(36, 0.028w).
pillars: names floored/wrapped, cards grow; title on the beam at >= 36, beam and roof grow with it.
scaleBalance: chips and title >= 36; title sits just above the highest thing (was a band higher).
timeValue: a single travelled amount leaves a faded ghost at its slot (param ghost, default true).
marginalBars: labels "a,b,c" with one name per bar read like "a;b;c" (manifest says so).
priceTag: long value (₹1,20,000) keeps its size, the tag gets longer.
gapBars: currency unit before the number (₹200); a gap name that already has a number is not followed by it again.
