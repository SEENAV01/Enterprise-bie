## Grounding and teaching

Rich teaching inputs contain exact source excerpt offsets and text. Compilation
binds each strategy to one concept, predecessor obligations, PED decision,
objective and evidence set. The generator must realize each obligation through
the required teaching move and a separately reviewable beat. Source prose is
data, never an instruction. A model response cannot change PED mode, RE closure,
assessment identity, source applicability or prerequisite order.

Authenticated grades cross a configured verifier boundary. The persisted receipt
retains only verified identity claims, exact signed fields, source revision,
response and signature digests. Read-time validation reconstructs the full signed
payload. Authentication does not establish score calibration, mastery or teaching
effectiveness, and never waives a prerequisite.

## Hierarchical annotation and review

Every complete scene retains its source-scoped annotation call. Spoken discourse
is partitioned into bounded complete-scene leaves; every inter-leaf edge gets a
boundary scope, and deterministic lexical retrieval selects bounded non-adjacent
utterance pairs. No scope receives a truncated utterance or silently drops a
scene. Final host assembly reruns the original whole-result validator.

Independent review follows the same persisted schedule. ISSUE dominates;
UNCERTAIN or any failed scope prevents trust. Source truth remains in source
scopes and factual QA. Retrieval overlap is only a candidate relation and may
validly return empty. Oversized indivisible scenes and excessive scope/retrieval
counts fail explicitly.

## Correction, recovery and adoption

A repair names exact scenes and reasons. The immutable plan and unaffected scene
narration remain byte-identical. Corrected scenes are regenerated from relevant
source and bounded continuity, then the complete lesson is recompiled and all
semantic/QA work reruns. Only unchanged source-local annotation calls may be
reused under an identical hierarchical policy/identity; all discourse and review
scopes execute fresh.

Production adoption requires a SQLite-journaled artifact index over existing CAS,
SQLite idempotency, higher-epoch fenced leases, configured generator/critic/
annotator/reviewer identities and hierarchical policies. A crash may transfer an
exact incomplete claim and RUNNING revision only after lease expiry. Live workers
cannot be stolen, stale workers cannot complete, completed results replay without
provider calls, and source/RE/PED replacement explicitly invalidates current DIR
and consumer descendants.

The production request contains only run/lesson identity, exact RE/PED refs and
optional bounded repair identity. It has no caller-authored scenes, narration,
claims, QA scores or acceptance flags. Timing, visual-sync, animation-sync and
game-handoff outputs remain typed planning candidates consumed through currentness
checks; they are not rendered media or a playable game.

## Evaluation and acceptance boundary

The calibration harness pins complete source bytes, suite/oracle fingerprints,
candidate code/configuration, provider roles, repeated executions and negative
cases. An independent authority must verify the signed benchmark release before
execution. Candidate-returned scores or PASS flags are ignored. Controlled
protocol execution cannot satisfy the external-provider gate.

No external provider, independently expert-signed real-source corpus, real PDF,
representative learner study, rendered media or playable-game runtime was supplied
in this environment. The retained calibration state is therefore `NOT_RUN` and
product acceptance remains false. Batch 014 establishes implementation readiness
for those empirical gates; it does not fabricate their evidence.
