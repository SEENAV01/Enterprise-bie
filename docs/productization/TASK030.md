## Grounded technical behavior / Math contract gap

The credential-free provider reads actual Task029 source-derived Knowledge claims.
Only bounded full explicit statements such as a concept requiring another concept
are considered. They are cross-checked through native prerequisite extraction,
exact concept IDs, claim IDs and common source anchors. Source order, proximity
and term-overlap heuristics do not establish accepted dependencies. Unsupported
references remain review records; self loops and cycles fail closed. A sparse or
edge-free graph is truthful, not artificially connected. Stable topological
tie-breaking is technical ordering, not a semantic curriculum recommendation.

`bie.prerequisite.graph/1` and `bie.reasoning.decision_set/1` are PRIVATE artifacts.
They bind run/source/knowledge identities, schemas/profile/policy and attempts;
Reasoning additionally binds exact prerequisite ID/hash. Candidate receipts bind
provider/model, prompt/policy, inputs/outputs and validation. Every accepted edge
has claim/anchor support. Reasoning decisions reference verified knowledge and
prerequisite artifacts plus source claim/anchor identities. They retain explicit
assumptions, uncertainty and review-required state. Confidence 0.5 is a bounded
technical evidence strength, NOT a calibrated truth probability.

Only prerequisite graph / teaching-order reasoning executes. Provider results
are checked against independent native deterministic computation. Invented
nodes/claims/anchors/relations, causal/temporal/spatial interpretations and
certainty promotion cannot pass merely because a provider proposed them.
No live transport/paid credentials are used. Unavailable provider selections
are BLOCKED; malformed/timeout responses fail closed with safe codes.

The default graph still lacks an explicit Math stage. A bounded conservative
syntactic gate checks FULL extracted text (including beyond KI excerpts) for
equations, mathematical operations/derivation/proof and numeric units. Such
content is BLOCKED at Reasoning with `math_evidence_required`; no equations are
discarded to obtain success. This is NOT a complete semantic math-domain
classifier. Governed Math artifact production/admission and reconciliation of
the global Math/Audio stage contracts remain open before multi-domain acceptance.
No permanent Math stage is invented here.
