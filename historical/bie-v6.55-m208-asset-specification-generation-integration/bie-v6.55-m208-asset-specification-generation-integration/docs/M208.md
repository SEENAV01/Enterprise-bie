# M208 — Asset Specification & Generation Integration

M208 connects the M207 storyboard to the asset-production layer:

```text
Storyboard
    ↓
Asset Registry
    ↓
Asset Specifications
    ↓
Generation Prompts / Structured Specs
    ↓
Evidence Binding
    ↓
Production Manifest
    ↓
Verified Asset Plan
```

## Asset registry

Every required asset has a stable ID, type, purpose and target scene.

## Specifications

Specifications define generation method, dimensions, format, style and constraints.

## Prompt contract

Generated assets may carry positive prompts, negative prompts and conditioning data.

## Structured assets

Diagrams, equations and animations have dedicated machine-readable specifications rather than relying only on free-form prompts.

## Evidence

Claims represented in educational assets can remain bound to source evidence and source records.

## Manifest

The asset manifest groups all assets for downstream production and can carry a global production profile.

## Provenance and verification

The manifest remains linked to storyboard scenes and evidence and passes an explicit verification gate.

## Boundary

M208 does not itself generate images, render equations, animate scenes, synthesize audio or choose a vendor. It defines the canonical asset-production contract consumed by those systems.
