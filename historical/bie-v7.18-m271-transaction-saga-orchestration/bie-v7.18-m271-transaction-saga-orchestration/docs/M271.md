# M271 — Transaction & Saga Orchestration

```text
Saga
 ↓
Step 1 → Effect
 ↓
Step 2 → Effect
 ↓
Step 3 → Failure
 ↓
Failure Policy
 ├── Retry
 ├── Compensate
 └── Abort
      ↓
Reverse Compensation
      ↓
Rolled Back / Compensated
```

## Saga
A saga is a sequence of independently executable steps with optional compensation actions.

## Transaction lifecycle
Transactions support ACTIVE, COMMITTED and ROLLED_BACK states and can record effects.

## Failure policy
Failures are classified into retryable, compensatable and abort classes. Retry attempts are bounded.

## Compensation
Completed saga steps are compensated in reverse order.

## Exactly-once effect contract
Effect keys make repeated execution idempotent. A duplicate is acknowledged without applying the effect again.

## Partial failure
A failure after earlier effects have completed can trigger compensation and transaction rollback.

## Boundary
M271 defines orchestration contracts. It does not provide a distributed database transaction protocol; adapters must integrate actual transactional systems.
