# M198 — Resource, Capability & Quota Management Layer

M198 defines vendor-neutral resource management semantics.

## Resources and capabilities

A resource identifies a manageable capacity-bearing entity. Capabilities describe what the resource can provide, with optional units and attributes.

## Capacity units

Capacity units provide normalization through a scale factor. Implementations may map these to domain-specific units.

## Quotas

Quotas limit consumption for a subject against a resource. An optional burst allows temporary usage above the steady-state limit.

## Budgets

Budgets define a bounded amount that may be consumed during a period.

## Reservations and allocations

Reservations express an intended claim over capacity and can be activated. Allocations represent capacity actually assigned from a reservation.

## Admission control

Admission evaluates requested capacity against availability plus quota and budget checks and returns an explicit `ADMIT` or `REJECT` decision.

## Utilization

Utilization records used capacity, total capacity and a derived ratio. Saturation is evaluated against a configurable threshold.

## Audit and observability

Resource operations emit audit events. Metrics expose utilization and allocation amount.

## Important boundary

M198 does not implement a cloud scheduler, Kubernetes resource manager, operating-system allocator, billing engine or physical capacity controller. It establishes portable resource-management contracts.
