# M231 — Assessment Feedback, Remediation Paths, Mastery Estimation & Adaptive Re-teaching

```text
Assessment Results
       ↓
Feedback Generation
       ↓
Mastery Estimation
 ┌─────┴────────┐
Overall      Concept-level
       ↓
Remediation Decision
       ↓
Prerequisite-aware Adaptive Plan
 ┌──────┼───────────────┐
Advance Practice     Re-teach
```

## Feedback
Each assessment result can produce correctness, score, explanation and misconception metadata.

## Mastery
Overall and concept-level mastery are estimated from normalized scores.

## Remediation
High mastery advances the learner, intermediate mastery triggers targeted practice, and low mastery triggers re-teaching plus practice.

## Adaptive planning
Concepts are prioritized from lowest to highest mastery, with prerequisite metadata carried into the plan.

## Boundary
M231 provides deterministic learner-state contracts. Production systems can add Bayesian mastery models, item-response theory, longitudinal learner models and richer pedagogical strategies.
