# M210 — Video Composition & Render Specification Integration

M210 is the bridge from production plans to an executable video composition:

```text
Storyboard + Assets + Audio
          ↓
      Layer Graph
          ↓
 Captions + Transitions
          ↓
 Script / Visual / Audio Sync
          ↓
     Render Profile
          ↓
   Verified Composition
```

## Layer graph

Composition layers represent background, visual, text, equations, narration, audio, captions, overlays and transitions.

## Composition

The composition defines canvas size, FPS, duration and ordered layers.

## Captions

Captions are time-addressable and may retain their source script-block reference.

## Transitions

Scene boundaries can specify cut, fade, dissolve, wipe or no transition.

## Synchronization

Sync points bind scenes to narration segments and asset IDs.

## Render profile

The render contract defines output format, codec, dimensions, FPS, bitrate and audio codec.

## Provenance and verification

The composition remains traceable to storyboard, assets, audio and scenes and passes an explicit verification gate.

## Boundary

M210 does not perform actual rendering. It defines the canonical composition/render contract that a downstream engine such as Remotion or another renderer can execute.
