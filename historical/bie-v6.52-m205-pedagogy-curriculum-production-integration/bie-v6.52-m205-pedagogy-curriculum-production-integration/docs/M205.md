# M205 — Pedagogy & Curriculum Production Integration

M205 connects the M204 Learning Graph to the instructional-design layer:

```text
Learning Graph
      ↓
Learning Objectives
      ↓
Pedagogy Strategies
      ↓
Assessment Alignment
      ↓
Curriculum Units
      ↓
Lesson Plans
```

## Objectives

Objectives are explicit learner outcomes and can retain evidence references.

## Pedagogy

The canonical boundary supports explanation, examples, demonstrations, guided practice, retrieval, spaced review, comparison and misconception repair.

## Assessment

Assessments are explicitly aligned to objectives and carry success criteria.

## Curriculum

Curriculum units contain objectives, prerequisites and lesson references.

## Lesson planning

Lesson plans bind objectives, pedagogy strategies, assessments and prerequisites, with optional duration.

## Alignment

The alignment record makes the objective → strategy → assessment relationship explicit.

## Provenance and verification

Curriculum artifacts remain traceable to source/graph/evidence context and pass an explicit verification gate.

## Boundary

M205 defines the production integration contract. It does not hard-code a specific instructional-design framework, LMS, question generator, or learner model.
