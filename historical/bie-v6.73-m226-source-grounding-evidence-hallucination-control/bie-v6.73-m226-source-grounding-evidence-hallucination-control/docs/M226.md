# M226 — Source Grounding, Citation/Reference Mapping, Evidence Retrieval & Hallucination Risk Control

```text
Factual Claims
      ↓
Evidence Retrieval
      ↓
Source Registry
      ↓
Claim ↔ Evidence Grounding
      ↓
Citation Mapping
      ↓
Grounding Coverage + Hallucination Risk
      ↓
QA Gate
```

## Source registry
Sources carry stable IDs, titles, references, types and optional publisher/version metadata.

## Evidence
Evidence records identify their source, excerpt reference, linked claims and relevance score.

## Grounding
Each claim is checked for supporting evidence and best evidence relevance.

## Citation mapping
Grounded claims receive evidence references that can be resolved to registered sources.

## Hallucination risk
Unsupported claims are HIGH risk. Weakly supported claims receive MEDIUM/HIGH risk according to relevance.

## Gate
A configurable risk threshold determines whether grounded content is acceptable for downstream generation.

## Boundary
M226 defines grounding contracts. Production systems can add web/database retrieval, vector search, exact excerpt storage, citation rendering and domain-specific fact verification.
