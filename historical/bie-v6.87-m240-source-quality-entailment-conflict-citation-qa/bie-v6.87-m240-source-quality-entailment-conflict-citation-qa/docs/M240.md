# M240 — Source Quality Ranking, Evidence Entailment, Conflict Detection & Citation Completeness QA

```text
Sources
  ↓
Quality Ranking
  ↓
Evidence ↔ Claim
  ↓
Entailment Check
  ↓
Cross-Claim Conflict Detection
  ↓
Citation Completeness
  ↓
Source QA Gate
```

## Source quality
Sources receive configurable scores for authority, recency, specificity and stability.

## Evidence entailment
A deterministic lexical-overlap baseline estimates whether evidence supports the claim. This is a structural baseline, not a proof of semantic entailment.

## Conflict detection
Claims can carry topic and polarity metadata; opposing polarity claims on the same topic are flagged.

## Citation completeness
The QA layer measures the fraction of claims with resolved citation evidence.

## Gate
The source QA gate requires acceptable source quality, entailment, complete citation coverage and no detected conflicts.

## Boundary
M240 is a deterministic QA contract. Production systems should add trusted-source policies, temporal validity, semantic/NLI entailment, numerical verification and human adjudication for conflicts.
