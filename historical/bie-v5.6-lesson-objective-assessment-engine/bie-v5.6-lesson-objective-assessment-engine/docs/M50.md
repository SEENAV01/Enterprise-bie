# M50 — Lesson Objective & Assessment Engine

M50 converts curriculum concepts into measurable learning objectives and evidence of learner understanding.

## Objective levels

Objectives can represent:
- recall
- explain
- apply
- analyze
- create
- transfer

The objective must describe an observable outcome rather than simply "understand the topic."

## Assessment types

M50 supports:
- conceptual questions
- numerical problems
- applications
- misconception probes
- transfer questions
- explanation questions
- multi-step problems

A strong lesson can therefore test more than memorization.

## Prerequisite checks

Before teaching a dependent concept, the engine can inspect learner mastery of required prerequisites.

If mastery is insufficient, the adaptive layer can route the learner to prerequisite teaching.

## Misconceptions

Misconceptions are explicit objects rather than accidental wrong answers.

A misconception record can store:
- the misconception
- the wrong response
- why it is wrong
- the corrective explanation

This allows the video/course generator to deliberately address common conceptual errors.

## Mastery

Assessment results produce a mastery signal.

The current implementation uses a simple weighted update. It is deliberately replaceable; production systems can later use item-response theory, Bayesian knowledge tracing, or other validated models.

## Adaptive actions

Depending on mastery, the system can recommend:
- teach prerequisite
- reteach with another explanation
- practice
- transfer practice
- advance

## Video integration

M50 does not force assessment into every second of a video.

Instead, it produces assessment opportunities that can be embedded:
- after a concept
- between sections
- at lesson end
- in an interactive version
- as homework/practice

The explanatory video remains driven by pedagogy and synchronized narration, not by an arbitrary assessment count.
