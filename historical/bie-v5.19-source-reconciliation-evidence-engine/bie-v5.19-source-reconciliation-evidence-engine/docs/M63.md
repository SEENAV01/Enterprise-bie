# M63 — Source Reconciliation & Evidence Engine

M63 is the evidence layer between source ingestion and domain reasoning.

## Purpose

Multiple sources may:
- agree
- disagree
- use different terminology
- provide different levels of detail
- leave a claim uncertain

M63 prevents these differences from being silently collapsed.

## Claim objects

A claim is an explicit statement linked to:
- source references
- concept references
- reconciliation status

## Evidence objects

Evidence records identify:
- the claim they address
- the source
- the locator
- whether they support or contradict the claim
- optional strength and notes

## Contradictions

Contradictions are represented explicitly between claims.

This allows downstream reasoning to decide what to do rather than hiding disagreement during ingestion.

## Evidence weighting

Source categories can have configurable weights.

These are heuristics, not universal truth rules.

The system should allow domain-specific policies to override generic defaults.

## Uncertainty

A claim can be:
- accepted
- tentative
- review required

Uncertainty can also record the reason and alternative interpretations.

## Evidence packet

For a concept, M63 produces an evidence packet containing:
- relevant claims
- supporting evidence
- contradictions
- unresolved status

This becomes an input to the domain reasoning layer.

## Critical rule

M63 does **not** automatically declare a claim true merely because one source has higher weight.

Weighting helps organize evidence; domain validation determines the final authoritative interpretation.

## Relationship to M62

M62 preserves the source faithfully.

M63 compares and structures claims across those preserved sources.

## Relationship to M53

M53 can consume M63 evidence packets when constructing domain models and constraints.
