## Assessment items
Items retain stable IDs, objective links, type, prompt, answer and difficulty.

## Rubrics
Open-response objectives can receive multi-criterion rubrics with ordered proficiency levels.

## Difficulty
Difficulty is calibrated from Bloom level, procedural steps and abstraction.

## Distractors
MCQ distractors can be derived from misconception candidates and are validated for uniqueness and presence of the correct answer.

## Evaluation planning
The engine selects answer-key or rubric scoring and verifies every required objective has assessment coverage.

## Boundary
M230 provides deterministic assessment contracts. Production systems can add psychometric calibration, item-response models, domain-specific distractors and automated rubric judging.
