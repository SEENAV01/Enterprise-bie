# M209 — Voice, Audio & Narration Production Integration

M209 connects M206/M207 script and scene planning to the audio-production layer:

```text
Script + Storyboard
       ↓
Voice Profile
       ↓
Narration Segments
       ↓
Pronunciation / Emphasis
       ↓
Audio Cues
       ↓
Synchronized Timeline
       ↓
Mix Profile
       ↓
Verified Audio Plan
```

## Voice profile

A voice is represented by a stable ID, profile, language, pace and pitch metadata.

## Narration segments

Narration is split into time-addressable segments bound to script blocks.

## Pronunciation

Terms can carry phonetic guidance, aliases and language metadata.

## Audio cues

Music, SFX, pauses, emphasis, ducking and fades are represented as timeline events.

## Timeline

Narration and cues share a common timeline for downstream synchronization.

## Mix profile

The canonical boundary carries target loudness and relative level metadata for narration, music and SFX.

## Evidence

Instructional narration can retain evidence/source-record bindings.

## Provenance and verification

The audio plan remains traceable to script/scenes/evidence and passes an explicit verification gate.

## Boundary

M209 does not synthesize speech, edit waveforms, perform mastering or select a specific TTS/audio vendor. It defines the production contract consumed by those systems.
