# M245 — Assessment Generation, Diagnostic Questions, Mastery Measurement & Feedback-Driven Adaptation

```text
Lesson / Concept
      ↓
Diagnostic Questions
      ↓
Learner Responses
      ↓
Response Scoring
      ↓
Mastery Measurement
      ↓
Learning-Gap Diagnosis
      ↓
Targeted Feedback
      ↓
Adaptive Next Action
```

## Assessment generation
Each concept can receive diagnostic questions tagged with difficulty and a rationale.

## Mastery measurement
Responses are scored per question and aggregated by concept into a normalized mastery score.

## Diagnosis
Concepts are classified as MASTERED, DEVELOPING or GAP.

## Feedback
Correct answers receive reinforcement; incorrect answers trigger review-oriented feedback.

## Adaptation
Gaps trigger foundation reteaching and extra practice; developing concepts receive intermediate practice; mastered concepts progress toward advanced material.

## Boundary
M245 provides a deterministic assessment/feedback contract. It does not claim that two template questions are sufficient to measure true mastery. Production systems should use calibrated item banks, psychometrics, misconception models and richer evidence.
