# M48 — Knowledge Expansion & External Research Layer

M48 is the bridge between the book's grounded knowledge and legitimate higher-level knowledge.

## Purpose

The system should not stop at what a book literally states.

It should be able to discover:
- prerequisites the learner needs
- deeper theoretical concepts
- higher-order abstractions
- applications
- cross-domain connections
- counterexamples
- limitations
- modern context

But it must clearly distinguish these from source facts.

## Expansion workflow

Book claim
→ candidate expansion
→ classify expansion
→ research when external evidence is required
→ assess source quality
→ assign confidence
→ add to enriched knowledge graph
→ review before curriculum insertion

## External research

Research requests carry a source policy:
- prefer primary sources
- prefer authoritative sources
- use recent sources for date-sensitive topics

The layer records external evidence separately from book evidence.

## Confidence

Confidence is a metadata signal, not truth.

A high confidence score does not permit unsupported claims.

## Speculation guard

Hypotheses and speculation are never silently promoted to established educational knowledge.

They remain review-only.

## Why this matters

This module directly implements the earlier requirement:

**Book knowledge + higher knowledge beyond the book**

without creating a hidden mixture where the learner cannot tell what came from the book and what was added by the system.

## Curriculum safety

External expansions are marked for review before they become required curriculum content.

A production workflow can later support:
- automatic acceptance for high-confidence well-supported expansions
- expert review
- user-selected depth
- age/level filtering
- source freshness policies

## Next
