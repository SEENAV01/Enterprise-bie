# M244 — Adaptive Difficulty, Learner Mastery Signals, Prerequisite Readiness & Personalized Learning Path Selection

```text
Learner Signals
      ↓
 Mastery Score
      ↓
Mastery Level
      ↓
Prerequisite Readiness
      ↓
Adaptive Difficulty
      ↓
Personalized Path Selection
      ↓
Adaptive Learning Gate
```

## Mastery
Assessment, practice, completion and confidence signals are aggregated into a normalized mastery score.

## Readiness
A dependent concept is ready when its prerequisite mastery scores meet the readiness threshold.

## Difficulty
Foundation, intermediate and advanced difficulty are selected from mastery; repeated recent failures trigger foundation scaffolding.

## Personalized path
The target concept is expanded through prerequisites and each concept receives a learner-specific difficulty decision.

## Boundary
M244 uses explicit learner signals and deterministic thresholds. It does not infer mastery from unvalidated behavioral proxies or claim pedagogical efficacy without learner outcome data.
