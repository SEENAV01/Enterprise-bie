# M229 — Lesson Objective Generation, Bloom-Level Assignment, Concept Coverage & Assessment Mapping

```text
Lesson Concepts
      ↓
Objective Generation
      ↓
Bloom Cognitive Level
      ↓
Concept Coverage
      ↓
Assessment Mapping
      ↓
Objective QA Gate
```

## Objective generation
Each lesson concept can produce a measurable objective linked directly to the concept ID.

## Bloom assignment
Action verbs map to six cognitive levels: REMEMBER, UNDERSTAND, APPLY, ANALYZE, EVALUATE and CREATE.

## Coverage
The engine verifies that lesson concepts are represented by at least one objective.

## Assessment mapping
Each Bloom level maps to a corresponding assessment pattern, creating an explicit objective → assessment relationship.

## Validation
Plans fail when objectives are absent, concepts are uncovered or Bloom levels are invalid.

## Boundary
M229 defines planning contracts. Production systems can add richer objective-language generation, rubric generation, item banks and subject-specific assessment policies.
