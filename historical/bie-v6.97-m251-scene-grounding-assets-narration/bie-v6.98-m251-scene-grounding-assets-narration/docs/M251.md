# M251 — Multimodal Concept-to-Scene Grounding, Visual Asset Selection, Equation Rendering & Narration Synchronization

```text
Concept
   ↓
Semantic / Multimodal Grounding
   ↓
Scene Intent
   ├── Visual Asset
   ├── Diagram
   ├── Equation
   └── Narration
          ↓
      Timeline Sync
          ↓
     Scene Specification
          ↓
   Remotion Rendering Layer
```

## Grounding
A concept is converted into explicit visual, equation and narration intents.

## Asset selection
Candidate assets are ranked by required type and concept-tag overlap. The selected asset becomes part of the scene specification.

## Equation rendering
Equations receive a renderable representation while preserving the source expression for validation.

## Narration synchronization
Narration segments have explicit start/end frames and are checked against scene bounds.

## Render handoff
The combined scene specification is designed as the contract consumed by a downstream renderer such as Remotion.

## Boundary
M251 does not perform generative image search, speech synthesis or final rendering itself. Those are downstream providers connected through this contract.
