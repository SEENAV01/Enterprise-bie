# M242 — Knowledge-Graph-Grounded Content Planning, Prerequisite-Aware Lesson Generation & Concept Coverage Validation

```text
Knowledge Graph
      ↓
Target Concepts
      ↓
Content Planning
      ↓
Prerequisite Expansion
      ↓
Ordered Lesson Sequence
      ↓
Lesson Units
      ↓
Concept + Prerequisite Coverage
      ↓
Planning Gate
```

## Grounded content planning
Target concepts are resolved against graph nodes and their prerequisite edges.

## Prerequisite-aware generation
A recursive dependency expansion places prerequisite concepts before their dependent concepts.

## Lesson units
The ordered concept sequence becomes explicit lesson units, distinguishing prerequisite and core roles.

## Coverage
The engine reports direct target coverage and expanded prerequisite coverage.

## Validation
The plan is rejected when a required prerequisite is absent or appears after its dependent concept.

## Boundary
M242 plans from an existing knowledge graph. It does not generate the pedagogical prose, visuals or narration itself; those layers consume these lesson units.
