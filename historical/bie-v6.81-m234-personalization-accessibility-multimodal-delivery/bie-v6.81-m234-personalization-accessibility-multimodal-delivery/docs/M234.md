# M234 — Personalization Policies, Learner Preferences, Accessibility Adaptation & Multi-Modal Delivery Selection

```text
Learner Preferences + Accessibility Profile
                    ↓
           Personalization Policy
             ┌──────┼─────────┐
          Pace   Visual     Language
                    ↓
          Accessibility Constraints
                    ↓
          Multi-Modal Delivery
          ┌─────────┼─────────┐
       Video   Captioned    Text
                    ↓
              Fallback Mode
```

## Preferences
The learner can specify language, pace, visual density and caption requirements.

## Accessibility
Profiles support captions, audio description, high contrast, reduced motion and text-first delivery.

## Policy resolution
Explicit learner and accessibility requirements are merged into a deterministic rendering policy.

## Delivery selection
The engine selects a compatible delivery modality from available outputs and falls back to text when necessary.

## Visual and narration constraints
Resolved policies control visual density, contrast, motion, language, pacing and audio description.

## Boundary
M234 defines personalization and accessibility contracts. Production systems can add richer locale support, screen-reader semantics, WCAG validation, device capabilities and modality-specific renderers.
