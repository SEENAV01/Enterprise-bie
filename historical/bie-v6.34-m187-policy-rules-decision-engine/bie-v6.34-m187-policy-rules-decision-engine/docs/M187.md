# M187 — Policy / Rules Evaluation & Decision Engine Layer

M187 provides vendor-neutral policy and rules contracts.

## Rules

A rule contains a predicate, effect, priority and version.

## Policies

Policies group rules and define evaluation mode:

```text
ALL
FIRST_MATCH
BEST_MATCH
```

## Deterministic evaluation

The reference evaluator implements a deliberately small equality predicate contract. Rich domain predicates are extension points.

## Priority

Rules can be ordered by descending priority with deterministic rule-ID tie breaking.

## Overrides

An override records target, replacement value, reason, actor and optional expiry metadata.

## Versioning

Policies are explicitly versioned. A new version increments the previous version rather than mutating historical versions.

## Explainability

Decision traces preserve the evaluations and final decision. The explanation contract exposes matched rule IDs.

## Simulation

A policy can be evaluated against a collection of contexts and compared with another result set.

## Observability

Evaluation events record policy ID, version, operation, status and match count.

## Relationship

## Important boundary

M187 does not embed a full expression language, authorization product, machine-learning model, or domain-specific policy semantics. Those can implement the policy interfaces.
