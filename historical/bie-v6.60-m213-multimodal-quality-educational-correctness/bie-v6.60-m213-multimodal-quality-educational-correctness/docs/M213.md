# M213 — Multi-Modal Quality Evaluation & Educational Correctness Integration

M213 adds the final evaluation intelligence after rendering:

```text
Rendered Video
      ↓
Multi-Modal Evaluation
      ↓
Factual + Conceptual + Pedagogical Checks
      ↓
Objective Coverage
      ↓
Cross-Modal Alignment
      ↓
Weighted Quality Score
      ↓
PASS / REVIEW / FAIL
      ↓
Remediation Signals
```

## Modalities

Visual, audio, text, timing and caption quality are independently represented with score and confidence.

## Educational correctness

Checks cover factual, conceptual, visual, temporal, pedagogical and objective-coverage dimensions.

## Objective coverage

Learning objectives are explicitly mapped to scenes and evidence and assigned coverage scores.

## Cross-modal alignment

Script blocks can be aligned with scenes, assets, narration and captions.

## Overall evaluation

The engine combines modality, correctness, objective coverage and alignment into a weighted final score.

Default decision thresholds:
- PASS >= 0.90
- REVIEW >= 0.75
- FAIL < 0.75

## Remediation signals

Quality findings can emit targeted remediation signals rather than forcing a complete regeneration.

## Provenance

The report remains linked to the rendered artifact, script, storyboard and evidence.

## Boundary

M213 defines evaluation contracts and decision logic. Actual computer-vision, speech, audio, semantic and educational evaluators are downstream implementations.
