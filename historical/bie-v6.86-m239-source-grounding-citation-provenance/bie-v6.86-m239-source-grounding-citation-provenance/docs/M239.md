# M239 — Source Grounding, Citation Mapping, Evidence Extraction, Claim-to-Source Verification & Provenance Tracking

```text
Trusted Sources
      ↓
Evidence Extraction
      ↓
Evidence Records
      ↓
Grounded Claims
      ↓
Claim → Evidence Mapping
      ↓
Citation Coverage
      ↓
Provenance Chain
      ↓
Grounding QA Gate
```

## Source registry
Sources carry stable IDs, URIs, titles, publishers and optional locators.

## Evidence
Evidence is tied to a source and records an excerpt, locator and evidence kind.

## Claim verification
Claims must reference known evidence and meet a configurable confidence threshold.

## Citation mapping
Every grounded claim can be mapped back to its supporting evidence.

## Coverage
The pipeline measures the fraction of claims whose evidence references resolve.

## Provenance
Outputs record the operation and source IDs that contributed to them.

## Boundary
M239 creates traceability contracts; it does not independently establish that a source is authoritative or that an excerpt logically entails a claim. Those require source-quality and semantic-entailment validators.
