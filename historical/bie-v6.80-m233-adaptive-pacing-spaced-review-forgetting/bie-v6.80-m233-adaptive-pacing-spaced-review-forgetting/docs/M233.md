# M233 — Learning Experience Sequencing, Adaptive Pacing, Spaced Review & Forgetting-Aware Scheduling

```text
Learner Knowledge State
        ↓
Forgetting / Retention Estimate
        ↓
Review Risk
        ↓
Spaced Review Schedule
        ↓
Mastery-aware Pacing
        ↓
Lesson Sequencing
        ↓
Session Plan
```

## Adaptive pacing
Average mastery controls a recommended session duration factor: lower mastery receives more time, high mastery receives less.

## Spaced review
Review intervals are selected from mastery bands and can be explicitly overridden.

## Forgetting model
Predicted retention decays exponentially using a configurable half-life.

## Review risk
The difference between full retention and predicted retention becomes a risk score used to prioritize review.

## Sequencing
Lessons are prioritized by unmet mastery plus review risk.

## Session planning
The sequence and recommended study duration are combined into a session plan.

## Boundary
M233 uses a simple deterministic forgetting model. Production systems can replace it with learner-specific forgetting curves, Bayesian models, IRT or empirical spaced-repetition algorithms.
