## Policy
Execution policy captures priority class, deadline, preemptibility and retry limits.

## Priority
CRITICAL/HIGH/NORMAL/LOW classes produce deterministic scheduling scores with queue age and deadline urgency.

## SLA/SLO
Targets can be represented as service-level objectives and evaluated against observed compliance.

## Deadline awareness
Jobs are classified as ON_TRACK, AT_RISK or MISSED.

## Preemption
High-priority work can request preemption of eligible lower-priority, preemptible work.

## Autoscaling
Queue depth, arrival rate and service rate drive desired worker capacity within configured bounds.

## Boundary
M220 defines the policy and decision contracts. Production control loops, cloud autoscalers and scheduler integrations remain deployment-specific.
