# M238 — Factual Accuracy, Formula/Definition Verification, Concept Consistency & Educational Content QA

```text
Generated Educational Content
          ↓
 ┌────────┼──────────┐
Claims  Definitions  Formulas
   ↓         ↓          ↓
Validation / Verification
          ↓
 Concept Consistency
          ↓
 Cross-Reference QA
          ↓
 Educational Content Gate
```

## Claims
Claims carry source references and confidence metadata and can be required to include sources.

## Formulas
Formula expressions are syntax-checked and require declared variables; units can be attached for downstream dimensional validation.

## Definitions
Definitions can declare required semantic features that must occur in the statement.

## Consistency
Claims are grouped by topic for cross-scene consistency tracking.

## Cross-reference
Claims, definitions and formulas are counted and linked as a content QA inventory.

## Boundary
M238 supplies deterministic structural QA. It does not independently prove scientific truth. Production systems should connect claims to trusted references, symbolic algebra, unit/dimensional analysis and domain-specific validators.
